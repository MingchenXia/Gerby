Proof. Take the common less singular potential $\phi$ in the hypotheses as the reference potential. The inequalities $\varphi\preceq\phi$, $\psi\preceq\phi$ and the two mass equalities say exactly that $\varphi,\psi\in\mathcal E(X,\theta,\phi)$. The reference mass is positive, because it equals the positive mass of $\varphi$. The second hypothesis says that the Monge--Ampère measure of $\varphi$ vanishes on $\{\varphi<\psi\}$. The relative full-mass domination principle therefore gives $\varphi\geq\psi$. This identifies every hypothesis of the earlier proof; in particular the common full-mass reference cannot be dropped.
