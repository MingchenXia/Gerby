Proof. The big-class part of the theorem on positivity loci constructs a single Kähler current with analytic singularities whose pole set is $E_{\mathrm{nK}}(\alpha)$. This set is the same as $\operatorname{nK}(\alpha)$ here, by the defining intersection of singular loci of Kähler currents in $\alpha$. The same theorem proves that this pole set is a proper analytic subset. Thus its constructed current has all the required properties, including equality, rather than just containment, of the polar locus.
