Proof. The construction of mixed Alexandrov measures gives a positive, symmetric, additive and positively homogeneous measure in the $n$ arguments, with diagonal equal to the Alexandrov measure defined here. Repeatedly expand the diagonal measure of $\sum_i\lambda_iF_i$ using this additivity and homogeneity. Each ordered choice $(i_1,\ldots,i_n)$ contributes $\lambda_{i_1}\cdots\lambda_{i_n}\operatorname{MA}_{\mathbb R}(F_{i_1},\ldots,F_{i_n})$, which is the asserted polynomial formula.

The earlier proof first verifies positivity for smooth convex functions using mixed determinants, and then uses convolution and local uniform continuity of the diagonal measure to pass to finite convex functions. In particular positivity is proved rather than inferred from a signed polarization formula. Uniqueness follows by recovering each coefficient from the diagonal polynomial, for instance by polarization. The normalization remains $n!$ on the diagonal.
