Proposition 13.5.2649F62FDFBAD

Proposition 13.5.2. Let $X$ be a compact complex manifold, let $\varphi $ and $\Phi $ be quasi-plurisubharmonic functions on $X$ with $\varphi \leq \Phi $, where $\Phi $ has analytic singularities, and let $(\Psi _ k)_{k\geq 1}$ be quasi-plurisubharmonic functions on $X$ such that for all $0<\lambda <\lambda '$

\[ \mathcal I(\lambda '\Psi _ k)\subseteq \mathcal I(\lambda \varphi ) \quad \text{for every sufficiently large }k. \]

Then for each $0<\varepsilon <1$,

\[ \Psi _ k\preceq (1-\varepsilon )\Phi \quad \text{for every sufficiently large }k. \]

In particular, if $(X,\omega )$ is a compact Kähler manifold, $\theta $ is a smooth closed real $(1,1)$-form on $X$, $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$, and $(\Phi _ j)$ and $(\Psi _ k)$ are quasi-equisingular approximations of $\varphi $, then for each $j$ and each $0<\varepsilon <1$ we have $\Psi _ k\preceq (1-\varepsilon )\Phi _ j$ for every sufficiently large $k$.

Proof. Choose $0<\eta <\varepsilon $ and $\delta >0$ so small that $(1-\eta )/(1+\delta )\geq 1-\varepsilon $. Fix $t\geq t_0$, where $t_0$ is given by lemma 13.5.1 for $v=\Phi $ and $\eta $. By the hypothesis on $(\Psi _ k)$, applied with $\lambda =t$ and $\lambda '=t(1+\delta )$, and since $\varphi \leq \Phi $,

\[ \mathcal I(t(1+\delta )\Psi _ k) \subseteq \mathcal I(t\varphi )\subseteq \mathcal I(t\Phi ) \]

for every sufficiently large $k$. Fix such a $k$ and choose local generators $g_ i$ of $\mathcal I(t(1+\delta )\Psi _ k)$ near a point of $X$. They belong to $\mathcal I(t\Phi )$, so the first part of lemma 13.5.1 gives $\log |g_ i|^2\leq t(1-\eta )\Phi +O(1)$ near the point, and hence $\log \sum _ i|g_ i|^2\leq t(1-\eta )\Phi +O(1)$. Its second part, applied to $u=\Psi _ k$ and $t(1+\delta )$, then gives

\[ \Psi _ k\leq \frac1{t(1+\delta )}\log \sum _ i|g_ i|^2+O(1) \leq \frac{1-\eta }{1+\delta }\Phi +O(1). \]

The upper semicontinuous function $\Phi $ is bounded above on the compact space $X$, say by $M$. Since $\Phi -M\leq 0$ and $(1-\eta )/(1+\delta )\geq 1-\varepsilon $, we have $\frac{1-\eta }{1+\delta }(\Phi -M)\leq (1-\varepsilon )(\Phi -M)$, and therefore $\Psi _ k\leq (1-\varepsilon )\Phi +O(1)$ near every point. By compactness $\Psi _ k\preceq (1-\varepsilon )\Phi $; the constant may depend on $k$.

For the last assertion, recall from definition 13.4.4 that each $\Phi _ j$ is quasi-plurisubharmonic with analytic singularities and that $\varphi \leq \Phi _ j$, since the sequence decreases to $\varphi $; and $(\Psi _ k)$ satisfies the hypothesis on the inclusions of multiplier ideals. Hence the first part applies with $\Phi =\Phi _ j$. $\square$
