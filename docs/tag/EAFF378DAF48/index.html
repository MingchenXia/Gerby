Chapter 15 · Multiplier-ideal models and partial equilibrium · Tag EAFF378DAF48

I envelope operations

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 16.2.6

Proposition 16.2.6. For $u,v\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$,

\[ u\preceq _{\mathcal I}v\quad \Longrightarrow \quad P_\theta [u]_{\mathcal I}\leq P_\theta [v]_{\mathcal I}. \]

For $a>0$ one has $P_{a\theta }[a u]_{\mathcal I}=aP_\theta [u]_{\mathcal I}$. For smooth real $(1,1)$-forms $\theta _ i$ and potentials $u_ i\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta _ i)$, $i=1,2$,

\[ P_{\theta _1}[u_1]_{\mathcal I}+P_{\theta _2}[u_2]_{\mathcal I} \leq P_{\theta _1+\theta _2}[u_1+u_2]_{\mathcal I}. \]

Proof. Let $h$ be a competitor for $P_\theta [u]_{\mathcal I}$. Since $h\sim _{\mathcal I}u\preceq _{\mathcal I}v$, the Lelong-number criterion in theorem 1.16.3 shows that $\max (h,v-\sup _ Xv)$ is multiplier-ideal equivalent to $v$. This function is nonpositive and dominates $h$, so it is a competitor for $P_\theta [v]_{\mathcal I}$; taking the supremum over $h$ proves that the envelope preserves the order. Multiplication by $a$ is a bijection between the defining families in the scaling identity. For the sum, $P_{\theta _1}[u_1]_{\mathcal I}+P_{\theta _2}[u_2]_{\mathcal I}$ is nonpositive and $(\theta _1+\theta _2)$-plurisubharmonic, and it is multiplier-ideal equivalent to $u_1+u_2$ by projection and lemma 1.16.4. It is therefore a competitor for the envelope on the right. $\square$
