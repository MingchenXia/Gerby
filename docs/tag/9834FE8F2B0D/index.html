Tensor product

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Definition 1.8.8

Definition 1.8.8. Given two holomorphic line bundles $L_1$, $L_2$ on $X$ and plurisubharmonic metrics $h_1$ on $L_1$ and $h_2$ on $L_2$, we define the tensor product plurisubharmonic metric $h_1\otimes h_2$ on $L_1\otimes L_2$ as follows: for each $x\in X$, define

\[ (h_1\otimes h_2)_ x=h_{1,x}\otimes h_{2,x} \]

in the sense of definition 1.8.2.
