Chapter 6 · Lelong numbers, multiplier ideals, and regularization · Tag BA0E41A3C821

Analytic integrability bounded growth

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Corollary 13.6.3

Corollary 13.6.3. Let $v$ be a quasi-plurisubharmonic function with analytic singularities on a compact complex manifold $X$. For every $\varepsilon >0$ there is $k_0>0$ such that for every real $k\geq k_0$

\[ \mathcal I(k(1+\varepsilon )v)\subseteq \mathcal I_\infty (kv). \]

Proof. Apply lemma 13.5.1 with $\eta =\varepsilon /(1+\varepsilon )$ and $t=k(1+\varepsilon )$, so that $t(1-\eta )=k$. The lemma provides one threshold $t_0$ for all germs on $X$; put $k_0=t_0/(1+\varepsilon )$. For $k\geq k_0$, every germ $f\in \mathcal I(k(1+\varepsilon )v)$ satisfies $\log |f|^2\leq kv+O(1)$ near its base point, that is, $f\in \mathcal I_\infty (kv)$. In dimension zero all these ideals are the unit ideal. $\square$
