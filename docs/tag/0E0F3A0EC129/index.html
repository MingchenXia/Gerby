Chapter 10 · Hermitian geometry, torsion, and Gauduchon duality · Tag 0E0F3A0EC129

Selection forcing probability

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 15.12.5

Lemma 15.12.5. The functional $\mathcal F_{\lambda ,x}$ is finite, concave, decreasing and $\lambda ^{-1}$-Lipschitz for the uniform norm on $C(X)$. It satisfies

\[ \mathcal F_{\lambda ,x}(0)=w_\lambda (x),\qquad \mathcal F_{\lambda ,x}(r+b) =\mathcal F_{\lambda ,x}(r)-b/\lambda . \]

There is a probability measure $\nu _{\lambda ,x}$ such that, for every $r\in C(X)$ and every $s$ admissible for $r$ in (15.59),

15.60
\begin{equation} \label{eq:selection-forcing-support} \lambda s(x)+\int _ Xr\, \mathrm{d}\nu _{\lambda ,x} \leq \lambda w_\lambda (x). \end{equation}

Proof. By part (2) of corollary 14.12.3, $w_\lambda $ is the greatest admissible potential for $r=0$. If $m=\min _ Xr$ and $M=\max _ Xr$, then $w_\lambda -M/\lambda $ is admissible for $r$, and translating any potential admissible for $r$ by $m/\lambda $ makes it admissible for $r=0$. Hence

\[ w_\lambda (x)-M/\lambda \leq \mathcal F_{\lambda ,x}(r) \leq w_\lambda (x)-m/\lambda . \]

The mixed density inequality on $\Omega $ and the arithmetic–geometric mean inequality show that a convex combination of potentials admissible for $r_0$ and $r_1$ is admissible for the same convex combination of the forcing terms. Approximating the two suprema at $x$ proves concavity. Inclusion of the admissible classes proves monotonicity, and translation by constants proves the translation rule and the Lipschitz bound.

Now apply the Hahn–Banach construction in the proof of theorem 15.2.1 to $-\lambda \mathcal F_{\lambda ,x}$ at zero. This functional is finite, continuous, convex and increasing, and it satisfies $-\lambda \mathcal F_{\lambda ,x}(r+b)=-\lambda \mathcal F_{\lambda ,x}(r)+b$ for constants $b$. The same tests with nonnegative functions and constants show that its subgradient is a probability measure $\nu _{\lambda ,x}$. The resulting supporting inequality, combined with $s(x)\leq \mathcal F_{\lambda ,x}(r)$, gives (15.60); it is not necessary that the supremum be attained. $\square$
