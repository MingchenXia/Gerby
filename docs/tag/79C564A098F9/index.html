Corollary 1.2.1379C564A098F9

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Corollary 1.2.13
Corollary 1.2.13. Let $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb {C}^ n$ be a domain and $\Omega '\subseteq \Omega $ be a subdomain
Note. In [Proposition 1.30, GZ17], Guedj–Zeriahi assumed in addition that $\Omega '$ is relatively compact in $\Omega $. This assumption is not necessary for the proof, and the applications of this proposition in [GZ17] usually do not satisfy this condition.
. Consider $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(\Omega )$ and $\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(\Omega ')$. Assume that
\[ \varlimsup _{\Omega '\ni y\to x}\psi (y)\leq \varphi (x) \]

for any $x\in \Omega \cap \partial \Omega '$. Define

\[ \eta (z)=\begin{cases} \varphi (z)\lor \psi (z),& \quad \textup{if }z\in \Omega ';\\ \varphi (z),& \quad \textup{if }z\in \Omega \setminus \Omega '. \end{cases} \]

Then $\eta \in \mathrm{PSH}(\Omega )$.
