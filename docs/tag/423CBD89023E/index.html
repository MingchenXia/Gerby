Positive elliptic automorphy

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Example 26.19.33

Example 26.19.33. The factors

\[ b_{m\tau +n}(z)=\exp \bigl (-\pi \mathrm{i}(m^2\tau +2mz)\bigr ) \]

define a line bundle of degree one on $E$.

Proof. For $\gamma =r\tau +s$, the cocycle identity follows from

\[ \begin{aligned} b_{m\tau +n}(z+\gamma )b_{r\tau +s}(z) & =b_{(m+r)\tau +n+s}(z)\exp (-2\pi \mathrm{i}ms)\\ & =b_{(m+r)\tau +n+s}(z). \end{aligned} \]

Put $v=\operatorname {Im}\tau $. The squared norm

\[ |t|^2\exp \bigl (-2\pi (\operatorname {Im}z)^2/v\bigr ) \]

is invariant under the action, because $\operatorname {Im}(z+m\tau +n)=\operatorname {Im}z+mv$ and

\[ |b_{m\tau +n}(z)|^2=\exp \bigl (2\pi (m^2v+2m\operatorname {Im}z)\bigr ). \]

Its first Chern form is

\[ \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2\pi }\partial \bar\partial \left(\frac{2\pi (\operatorname {Im}z)^2}{v}\right) =\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2v}\, \mathrm{d}z\wedge \mathrm{d}\bar z. \]

This positive form integrates to one over a fundamental parallelogram, which has area $v$. Thus the degree is one. $\square$
