Chapter 20 · Hopf and locally conformally Kähler geometry · Tag DB27896B9A96

Lee gauduchon duality

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Theorem 29.6.4

Theorem 29.6.4. The cone $\mathcal C_ X$ is closed, and

\[ \mathcal C_ X =\{ \xi \in V_ X:\langle \xi ,a\rangle \geq 0 \text{ for every }a\in \operatorname {LG}(X)\} . \]

Conversely,

\[ \operatorname {LG}(X) =\{ a\in W_ X:\langle \xi ,a\rangle >0 \text{ for every }\xi \in \mathcal C_ X\setminus K_ X\} . \]

Proof. The first equality is theorem 4.3.1 applied to the smooth closed form $\kappa _\lambda $, together with (29.34). It expresses $\mathcal C_ X$ as an intersection of closed half-spaces, so this cone is closed.

If $a=[\mathrm d^{\mathrm c}(\gamma ^{n-1})]\in \operatorname {LG}(X)$ and $\xi =[\lambda ]\in \mathcal C_ X\setminus K_ X$, choose a closed positive current $T=\kappa _\lambda +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u$. It is nonzero because it represents the nonzero class $j_ X(\xi )$. Therefore

\[ \langle \xi ,a\rangle =\int _ XT\wedge \gamma ^{n-1}>0. \]

Conversely, suppose that $a\in W_ X\setminus \operatorname {LG}(X)$. Separating the point $a$ from the nonempty open convex cone $\operatorname {LG}(X)$ in the finite-dimensional space $W_ X$ gives a nonzero linear functional $\ell $ on $W_ X$ such that

\[ \ell (a)\leq 0,\qquad \ell (b)\geq 0 \quad (b\in \operatorname {LG}(X)). \]

Indeed, in the usual separating inequalities the constant can be taken to be zero, by scaling the elements of the cone. By Poincaré duality, $\ell =\langle \xi ,\cdot \rangle $ for a class $\xi \in V_ X$ that is unique modulo $K_ X$, and $\xi \notin K_ X$ because $\ell \neq 0$. The first equality of the theorem gives $\xi \in \mathcal C_ X$, and then $\langle \xi ,a\rangle \leq 0$ shows that $a$ does not belong to the right side of the second equality. This proves the second equality; if $\operatorname {LG}(X)=W_ X$, there is nothing to prove. $\square$
