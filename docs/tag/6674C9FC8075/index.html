Lemma 8.2.16674C9FC8075

Lemma 8.2.1. For such a function, at $\xi \ne 0$ the inequality $\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}V>0$ is equivalent to

8.5
\begin{equation} 1+V_{w\bar w}>0,\qquad \mu +V_ x>0,\qquad Q[V]:=V_{xx}+\mu - \frac{|\mu +V_{x\bar w}|^2}{1+V_{w\bar w}}>0. \end{equation}

At $\xi =0$, the inequality $\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}V>0$ is equivalent to $1+V_{w\bar w}>0$.

Proof. In these coordinates,

\[ s=w+\bar w+\log (1+\mathrm{e}^ x),\qquad \partial s=\mathrm{d}w+\mu \partial x, \]

and

\[ 2\pi \alpha =\mathrm i\mu \partial \bar\partial x +\mathrm i\mu (1-\mu )\partial x\wedge \bar\partial x. \]

Choose horizontal vectors of unit length for $2\pi \alpha $, one orthogonal to the radial direction and the other

\[ e_ r=\frac{\xi _1\partial _{\xi _1}+\xi _2\partial _{\xi _2}}{\sqrt{\mu (1-\mu )}}. \]

Adjoin the vertical vector $\partial _ w$. In this frame the matrix of $2\pi (\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}V)$ is

\[ \begin{pmatrix} (\mu +V_ x)/\mu & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & (\mu +V_{xx})/[\mu (1-\mu )] & (\mu +V_{x\bar w})/\sqrt{\mu (1-\mu )} \\ 0 & (\mu +V_{wx})/\sqrt{\mu (1-\mu )} & 1+V_{w\bar w} \end{pmatrix}. \]

Taking the Schur complement of the last diagonal entry gives (8.5). At $\xi =0$, the derivatives of $V$ in the base directions vanish and $\partial s=\mathrm{d}w$, which gives the last assertion. $\square$
