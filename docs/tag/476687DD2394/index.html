Chapter 17 · B-divisors and restriction · Tag 476687DD2394

Lemma 11.1.12

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Lemma 11.1.12

Lemma 11.1.12. Let $\pi \colon X\rightarrow Z$ be a proper bimeromorphic morphism from $X$ to a Kähler manifold $Z$. Consider non-divisorial closed positive $(1,1)$ currents $T,S$ on $X$ in the same cohomology class. If $T\preceq _{\mathcal{I}} S$, then $\pi _*T\preceq _{\mathcal{I}} \pi _*S$.

Proof. By Hironaka's Chow lemma theorem B.1.6 and 6.1.18, we may assume that $\pi $ is a modification.

By 7.3.10,

\[ \pi ^*\pi _*T=T+F_ T,\quad \pi ^*\pi _*S=S+F_ S, \]

where $F_ T$ and $F_ S$ are effective $\pi $-exceptional divisors. Since $\{ T\} =\{ S\} $, the exceptional divisors $F_ T$ and $F_ S$ have the same cohomology class. Factoring $\pi $ into blow-ups and using proposition 11.1.1 at each step, the classes of $\pi $-exceptional prime divisors are linearly independent in the relative cohomology; hence $F_ T=F_ S$. It follows that

\[ \pi ^*\pi _*T=T+F_ T\preceq _{\mathcal{I}}S+F_ S=\pi ^*\pi _*S. \]

Our assertion then follows from 6.1.18. $\square$
