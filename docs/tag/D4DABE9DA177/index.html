Lee gauduchon modification

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 29.6.5

Lemma 29.6.5. Let $\pi :Y\to X$ be a modification between connected compact complex manifolds, and let $\theta $ be a smooth closed real $(1,1)$-form on $X$. Then

\[ \{ \theta \} \text{ is pseudoeffective} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \{ \pi ^*\theta \} \text{ is pseudoeffective}. \]

The pullback on real Bott–Chern cohomology in bidegree $(1,1)$ is injective.

Proof. By proposition 28.1.2, both pullback and pushforward preserve pseudoeffectivity; the pullback of potentials is the one used in lemma 1.22.13. Since $\pi $ has degree one,

\[ \pi _*\pi ^*\{ \theta \} =\{ \theta \} . \]

Indeed, the identity of currents $\pi _*\pi ^*\theta =\theta $ follows by integration over the locus where $\pi $ is an isomorphism, whose complement has volume zero. This left inverse proves injectivity and the reverse implication for pseudoeffectivity. $\square$
