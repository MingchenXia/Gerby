Reading the book

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Reading the book

We assume familiarity with complex manifolds, differential forms and currents. Chapter 1 develops the plurisubharmonic tools used throughout. On a first reading, the maximum principle, normalized compactness and exponential integrability are the starting points; the longer approximation and descent arguments can be consulted when needed. The mass and energy calculations explain the obstructions above, and Gauduchon traces, non-pluripolar products and the Hopf construction complete the foundations.

The equation chapters develop smooth and weak estimates and comparison principles; the Hessian chapter examines how these arguments change when positivity is weakened. Envelopes and analytic approximation then develop the comparison of ordinary and ideal types; interpolation, restriction and holomorphic sections connect it to geometry. 9 gives the volume and equation theory under mass hypotheses. The appendix explains descent to compact unibranch spaces and solves equations on cones, normalized curves and cuspidal products. Open questions are collected in chapter 33, and the sources and precise dependencies in chapter 34; monograph references also appear where they are used.
