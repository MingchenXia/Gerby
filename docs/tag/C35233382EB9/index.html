Lemma 12.8.1C35233382EB9

Lemma 12.8.1. If $u$ is bounded admissible, then $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u$ has signed Radon measure coefficients. For $K\Subset U$ contained in a coordinate chart,

\[ \| \mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u\| _ K\leq C_{K,U,\omega }(1+\| u\| _{L^\infty (X)}). \]

Proof. Choose smooth strictly admissible $u_ j\downarrow u$ by 12.5.5, and put $\sigma _ j=\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ j$. After discarding finitely many terms, $\| u_ j\| _\infty \leq \| u\| _\infty +1$. Indeed, the increasing open sets $\{ u_ j<\sup _ Xu+1\} $ cover $X$, and compactness gives a single index whose open set is all of $X$; the lower bound follows from $u_ j\geq u$.

The closed $m$-cone lies in the closed $2$-cone. In an $\omega $-unitary frame, its eigenvalues satisfy

\[ \sum _ i\lambda _ i^2\leq (\operatorname {tr}\sigma _ j)^2, \qquad \operatorname {tr}\sigma _ j\geq 0. \]

Thus every coefficient of $\sigma _ j$ is bounded by its trace. The degree-one Chern–Levine–Nirenberg estimate in 12.6.1, applied to these smooth functions, bounds the integral of that trace on $K$ by $C(1+\| u\| _\infty )$. Subtracting the fixed smooth $\omega $ gives the same bound for the coefficient variations of $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ j$. Bounded convergence gives $u_ j\to u$ in local $L^1$, and therefore $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ j\to \mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u$ in distributions. The uniform coefficient bounds show that this limit has measure coefficients with the asserted bound. $\square$
