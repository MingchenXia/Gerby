Chapter 13 · Singularity types, envelopes, and models · Tag 0C703BE43035

Proposition 3.2.19

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 3.2.19

Proposition 3.2.19. Let $(\varphi _ i)_{i\in I}$ be an increasing net in $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ uniformly bounded from above. Let $\varphi \coloneqq \operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*}\varphi _ i$. Then

\[ \operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*} P_{\theta }[\varphi _ i]_{\mathcal{I}}=P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}. \]

In particular, if the $\varphi _ i$'s are all $\mathcal{I}$-model, then so is $\varphi $.

Proof. Let $\eta =\operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*} P_{\theta }[\varphi _ i]_{\mathcal{I}}$. Then $\eta \leq P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}$ as a consequence of 3.2.17.

By corollary 2.4.9, we have

\[ \lim _{i\in I} \int _ X\theta _{\varphi _ i}^ n=\int _ X\theta _{\varphi }^ n>0. \]

We may assume that for each $i\in I$, $\int _ X\theta _{\varphi _ i}^ n<\int _ X\theta _{\varphi }^ n$, since otherwise $P_{\theta }[\varphi _ i]=P_{\theta }[\varphi ]$ by theorem 3.1.9, and hence $P_{\theta }[\varphi _ i]_{\mathcal{I}}=P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}$ due to (3.185); the same holds for all $j\geq i$, and there is nothing to prove.

So by 2.4.15, we can find a decreasing net $\epsilon _ i\searrow 0$ ($i\in I$) as in (3.50) with $\epsilon _ i\in (0,1)$ and $\psi _ i\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ such that for all $i\in I$,

\[ (1-\epsilon _ i) \varphi +\epsilon _ i\psi _ i\leq \varphi _ i. \]

By proposition 3.2.16 and 3.2.17, we have

\[ P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}+\epsilon _ i P_{\theta }[\psi _ i]_{\mathcal{I}}\leq (1-\epsilon _ i) P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}+\epsilon _ i P_{\theta }[\psi _ i]_{\mathcal{I}}\leq P_{\theta }[\varphi _ i]_{\mathcal{I}}\leq \eta . \]

As above, $\epsilon _ iP_{\theta }[\psi _ i]_{\mathcal{I}}\to 0$ in $L^1$. It follows that $P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}\leq \eta $ almost everywhere, hence everywhere by proposition 1.2.6. $\square$
