Corollary 14.2.9E6419D4591F9

Corollary 14.2.9. Let $B'$ be a coordinate ball in $X$ and $B\Subset B'$ a ball in the same coordinates, and let $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ be bounded near $\overline{B'}$. There is $\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ with $\psi \geq \varphi $, $\psi =\varphi $ on $X\setminus B$, $\psi $ bounded near $\overline B$, and $(\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\psi )^ n=0$ on $B$. It is the largest function in $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ with these properties, and any two functions with these properties that are continuous on $\overline B$ coincide.

Proof. Choose a ball $B''$ in the same coordinates with $\overline B\subset B''$ and $\overline{B''}\subset B'$. Write $\theta =\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}h$ on $B''$, with $h$ smooth (lemma 13.1.2), and apply corollary 9.6.4 to the locally bounded plurisubharmonic function $\varphi +h$ on $B''$. Subtracting $h$ from the result on $B''$ and keeping $\varphi $ outside $B''$ gives $\psi $, since the result equals $\varphi +h$ on $B''\setminus B$; here $(\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\psi )^ n=(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(\psi +h))^ n$ is the Bedford–Taylor product of the bounded plurisubharmonic function $\psi +h$. If $\psi '$ is another function with the stated properties, then by upper semicontinuity $\varlimsup _{z\to \zeta ,\, z\in B}\psi '(z)\leq \psi '(\zeta )=\varphi (\zeta )$ for every $\zeta \in \partial B$. Hence $\psi '+h$ belongs to the family whose upper envelope is $\psi +h$ in corollary 9.6.4, and $\psi '\leq \psi $ on $B$. If $\psi '$ is continuous on $\overline B$, then its boundary values $\varphi |_{\partial B}$ are continuous, and $\psi '+h$ is the solution of the homogeneous Dirichlet problem with the continuous boundary values $(\varphi +h)|_{\partial B}$; by the uniqueness in theorem 9.6.3, any two such continuous functions coincide. $\square$
