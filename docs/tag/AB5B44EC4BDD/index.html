From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 14.25.2

Corollary 14.25.2. Let $\nu $ be a finite nonzero positive measure with $M_\rho (\nu )<\infty $. There are $c>0$ and a Hölder continuous $w\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,h)$ satisfying

\[ \sup _ Xw=0,\qquad \mathrm{MA}_ h(w)=c\nu , \qquad 0<\mathrm{MA}_ h(w)(X)<\infty . \]

Proof. By proposition 14.25.1, $\nu \leq (M_\rho (\nu )/k)\mathrm{MA}_ h(u_\rho )$, where $u_\rho $ is Hölder continuous with exponent $1/2$. Apply theorem 14.24.1. The equation and $0<c<\infty $ give its positive finite mass. $\square$
