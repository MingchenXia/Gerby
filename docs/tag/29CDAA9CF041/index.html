Volume equations in Gauduchon gauge

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · 29.4 Volume equations in Gauduchon gauge

Let $X$ again be a compact LCK manifold of dimension $n\geq 2$. Prescribing the volume of an LCK metric in Gauduchon gauge couples two equations: one for the determinant and one for the conformal factor. To compare metrics with a fixed Lee class, we first identify the twisted groups for cohomologous Lee forms. For a closed real one-form $\lambda $, smooth real functions $a,u$ and a smooth differential form $\eta $, multiplication by $\mathrm{e}^{-a}$ gives

\[ \mathrm{d}_\lambda (\mathrm{e}^{-a}\eta ) =\mathrm{e}^{-a}\mathrm{d}_{\lambda +\mathrm{d}a}\eta , \qquad D_\lambda (\mathrm{e}^{-a}u)=\mathrm{e}^{-a}D_{\lambda +\mathrm{d}a}u. \]

We use this identification when comparing classes; the additive constant in $a$ corresponds to a constant rescaling of the weight bundle.

Let $\omega _1$ be an LCK Gauduchon metric with Lee form $\lambda $, and let $\omega _2$ be an LCK metric with Lee form $\lambda +\mathrm{d}a$. If their twisted classes agree under the chosen identification, then

\[ \mathrm{e}^{-a}\omega _2=\omega _1+D_\lambda u=:A>0 \]

for a smooth real function $u$. Substituting $\omega _2=\mathrm{e}^ aA$ shows that $\omega _2$ has the same volume form as $\omega _1$ and is Gauduchon exactly when

29.11
\begin{equation} \label{eq:lck-coupled-equation} \begin{cases} A^ n=\mathrm{e}^{-na}\omega _1^ n,\\ \mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\bigl (\mathrm{e}^{(n-1)a}A^{n-1}\bigr )=0. \end{cases} \end{equation}

The scalar Hermitian equation of chapter 9 does not impose the second equation, and it uses $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u$ in place of $D_\lambda u$. The prescribed-volume Gauduchon theorem, theorem 9.9.1, does not assert that its solution is LCK or lies in the given twisted class.

Problem 29.4.1. Does a compact LCK manifold have at most one Gauduchon LCK metric with a given Lee class, a given twisted Bott–Chern class and a given smooth positive volume form?

This is the uniqueness problem posed in [Question 47.56(3), OV24LCK], with the identification of classes made explicit. The question concerns metrics: uniqueness of a scalar potential $u$ would additionally require control of the kernel of $D_\lambda $. The counterexamples with degenerate densities in chapter 11 concern ordinary potentials and do not decide this question.
