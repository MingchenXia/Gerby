From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 16.3.2

Proposition 16.3.2. There are two smooth decompositions of the same positive current for which the corresponding values of $W$ are different.

Proof. Use the Hermitian form $\omega $ and the smooth function $g=u_ t$ of example 2.3.1, with $t\ne 0$ sufficiently small. Both $\omega $ and $\omega '=\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}g$ are Hermitian, and

\[ \omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}0=\omega '+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(-g). \]

For a smooth potential in a Hermitian background, all multiplier ideals are trivial and zero is a competitor for its multiplier-ideal envelope, so that envelope is zero. Consequently

\[ W_\omega (0)=\int _ X\omega ^2,\qquad W_{\omega '}(-g)=\int _ X(\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}g)^2. \]

By the explicit formula (2.3), the two integrals differ. $\square$
