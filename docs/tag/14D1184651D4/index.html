Curves constant on a left half-line

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · 25.1 Curves constant on a left half-line

We restrict the envelope test curves of definition 18.1.1 by requiring that each slice be fixed by the multiplier-ideal envelope and that the curve be constant for $\tau \leq a$. Constancy identifies the initial potential explicitly as $\Gamma _ a$, and it confines the associated ray between the translates of this potential by $at$ and by $ct$.

Definition 25.1.1. Let $\mathscr T_{a,c}(\theta )$ be the set of families $\Gamma =(\Gamma _\tau )_{\tau <c}$ such that

  1. $\Gamma _\tau \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ and $P_\theta [\Gamma _\tau ]_{\mathcal I}=\Gamma _\tau $ for every $\tau <c$;

  2. the family is pointwise concave and decreasing in $\tau $;

  3. $\Gamma _\tau =\Gamma _ a$ for $\tau \leq a$.

The cutoff of $\Gamma $ is $c$, and we extend $\Gamma $ by $\Gamma _ c=\lim _{\tau \uparrow c}\Gamma _\tau $ and $\Gamma _\tau =-\infty $ for $\tau >c$. Such curves are ordered by the pointwise order of their slices $\Gamma _\tau $, $\tau <c$.

Every slice has supremum zero by theorem 16.2.2. Hence, by theorem 1.4.2, corollary 1.4.3, the decreasing limit $\Gamma _ c$ belongs to $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$; in particular, it is not identically $-\infty $. It is also $P_\theta [\cdot ]_{\mathcal I}$-fixed. Indeed, $\Gamma _ c\leq 0$ is a competitor for its own envelope, and $\Gamma _ c\leq \Gamma _\tau $, so the order preservation of proposition 16.2.6 gives

\[ \Gamma _ c\leq P_\theta [\Gamma _ c]_{\mathcal I}\leq \Gamma _\tau \quad (\tau <c). \]

Taking the infimum over $\tau $ proves equality.

Proposition 25.1.2. A curve $\Gamma \in \mathscr T_{a,c}(\theta )$ is an envelope test curve with initial potential $\varphi =\Gamma _ a$. Its Legendre transform

\[ U_ t=\Bigl (\sup _{\tau <c}(\Gamma _\tau +t\tau )\Bigr )^*\qquad (t>0) \]

is an envelope ray with initial potential $\varphi $ and slope $c$, in the sense of definition 18.1.2, and satisfies

\[ \varphi +at\leq U_ t\leq \varphi +ct\qquad (t>0). \]

In particular $U_ t\sim \varphi $ for every $t>0$.

Proof. By corollary 16.2.4, a $P_\theta [\cdot ]_{\mathcal I}$-fixed potential is also fixed by the ordinary envelope $P_\theta $. The regularized supremum of the curve is $\Gamma _ a$, since the curve is decreasing and constant for $\tau \leq a$. Hence all the conditions of definition 18.1.1 hold, and theorem 18.2.1 applies. The term $\tau =a$ in the transform gives the lower bound, and the inequalities $\Gamma _\tau \leq \varphi $ for $\tau <c$ give the upper bound. $\square$
