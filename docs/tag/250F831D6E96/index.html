Theorem 24.3.2250F831D6E96

Theorem 24.3.2. Let $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(Y,\theta )$ and fix a Hermitian form $\omega $ on $Y$. There are potentials $u_ m$ with analytic singularities, converging to $u$ in $L^1(Y)$, and positive numbers $\epsilon _ m\downarrow 0$, indexed by sufficiently large positive integers $m$, such that

\[ \theta _ m=\theta +2\epsilon _ m\omega , \qquad \theta _ m+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ m\geq \epsilon _ m\omega . \]

For each $m$, the family $\mathbb D_{\theta _ m}(u_ m)$ has a nef Cartier realization. On every fixed compact model $\pi :Z\to Y$, its class has the current representative

\[ C_{m,Z}=\pi ^*\theta +2\epsilon _ m\pi ^*\omega -D_{m,Z}, \qquad D_{m,Z}=\frac1m\sum _ E\operatorname {ord}_ E\mathcal I(mu)[E], \]

where $E$ ranges over the prime divisors on $Z$ and the order is computed after pulling the ideal back to $Z$. These representatives satisfy

\[ C_{m,Z}\longrightarrow \pi ^*\theta -N_ Z(u) \quad \hbox{weakly as currents}, \]

and the positive residual representatives satisfy

\[ R_{m,Z}:=C_{m,Z}+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(u_ m\circ \pi ) \longrightarrow \pi ^*\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(u\circ \pi )-N_ Z(u)\geq 0 \quad \hbox{weakly as currents}. \]

The exterior derivatives of both $C_{m,Z}$ and $R_{m,Z}$ converge smoothly to $\pi ^*\mathrm{d}\theta $.

Proof. Take $u_ m$ from corollary 1.15.2; they converge to $u$ in $L^1$. If $e_ m\to 0$ are their curvature errors, put $\epsilon _ m=\sup _{\ell \geq m}e_\ell +1/m$. These numbers are positive and decrease to zero, and the indices $m$ are unchanged. Adding $2\epsilon _ m\omega $ to the background turns the lower bound $-\epsilon _ m\omega $ into $\epsilon _ m\omega $. Since each $u_ m$ has analytic type $(1/m,\mathcal I(mu))$, proposition 24.3.1 gives a nef Cartier realization in the background $\theta _ m$.

On a fixed model, the generic divisorial Lelong numbers of $u_ m\circ \pi $ are exactly $m^{-1}\operatorname {ord}_ E\mathcal I(mu)$, so $N_ Z(u_ m)=D_{m,Z}$ and $C_{m,Z}=\pi ^*\theta _ m-N_ Z(u_ m)$ represents the class of $\mathbb D_{\theta _ m}(u_ m)$ on $Z$. The self-trace identity and theorem 23.1.1 give $D_{m,Z}\to N_ Z(u)$ in the trace mass of section 23.1, hence weakly as currents. Since the smooth error $2\epsilon _ m\pi ^*\omega $ tends to zero with all its derivatives, $C_{m,Z}$ converges as stated.

Divisorial removal gives $R_{m,Z}\geq 0$. All the $u_ m$ and $u$ have a common smooth lower curvature bound: choose $A>0$ with $\theta +\epsilon _ m\omega \leq A\omega $ for every index $m$ considered. By lemma 1.22.13, the convergence in $L^1(Y)$ implies $u_ m\circ \pi \to u\circ \pi $ in $L^1(Z)$. Applying $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}$ and adding the convergent currents $C_{m,Z}$ proves the convergence of the residual currents, and positivity persists in the weak limit. Finally, since each $D_{m,Z}$ is closed,

\[ \mathrm{d}R_{m,Z}=\mathrm{d}C_{m,Z} =\pi ^*\mathrm{d}\theta +2\epsilon _ m\pi ^*\mathrm{d}\omega , \]

which proves the smooth convergence of the exterior derivatives. $\square$
