The notion of b-divisors

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · 11.1 The notion of b-divisors

The b-divisors defined in this section are sometimes called b-divisor classes. We always omit the word classes to save space.

Let $X$ be a connected compact Kähler manifold of dimension $n$.

Let us recall the following elementary result regarding how cohomology behaves under blow-up.

Proposition 11.1.1. Let $\pi \colon Y\rightarrow X$ be a blow-up with connected smooth center of codimension at least $2$ with exceptional divisor $E$. Then there is a natural identification

11.1
\begin{equation} \label{eq:H11blowup} \mathrm{H}^{1,1}(Y,\mathbb {R})=\mathrm{H}^{1,1}(X,\mathbb {R})\oplus \mathbb {R}\{ E\} . \end{equation}
See [RYY19] for a much more general result.
