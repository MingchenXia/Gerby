Proposition 16.3.35ADA09DD223E

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Proposition 16.3.3

Proposition 16.3.3. Suppose that $\{ \theta \} $ is big, let $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$, and put $w=P_\theta [u]$. Then

\[ \mu _ w\leq \mathbf1_{\{ w=V_\theta \} }\mu _{V_\theta }, \]

and $\mu _ w$ is carried by $\{ w=0\} $.

Proof. Put $w_ k=P_\theta (u+k,V_\theta )$ for integers $k$; these are potentials by proposition 16.1.6. By corollary 16.2.6,

\[ \mu _{w_ k}\leq \mathbf1_{\{ w_ k=u+k\} }\mu _ u+\mathbf1_{\{ w_ k=V_\theta \} }\mu _{V_\theta } \leq \mathbf1_{\{ u+k\leq V_\theta \} }\mu _ u +\mathbf1_{\{ w=V_\theta \} }\mu _{V_\theta }; \]

for the second inequality we used $w_ k\leq V_\theta $ and $w_ k\leq w\leq V_\theta $. Since $V_\theta \leq 0$, the sets $\{ u+k\leq V_\theta \} $ decrease to the pole set of $u$, which is $\mu _ u$-null, and the first term tends to zero in total variation by dominated convergence. By proposition 16.3.2, $w_ k$ increases almost everywhere to $w$, so $\mu _{w_ k}\to \mu _ w$ weakly by corollary 15.7.6. Integrating a continuous function $\chi \geq 0$ and letting $k\to \infty $, we obtain $\int _ X\chi \, \mu _ w\leq \int _ X\chi \mathbf1_{\{ w=V_\theta \} }\, \mu _{V_\theta }$, and lemma 16.2.1 gives the asserted inequality. Finally, a potential lies below $0$ quasi-everywhere if and only if it is nonpositive, so $V_\theta =P_\theta (0)$, and theorem 16.2.5 shows that $\mu _{V_\theta }$ is carried by $\{ V_\theta =0\} $. Hence $\mu _ w$ is carried by $\{ w=V_\theta =0\} $. $\square$
