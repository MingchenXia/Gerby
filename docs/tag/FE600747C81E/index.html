From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Corollary 6.1.9

Corollary 6.1.9. Let $\varphi ,\psi \in \mathrm{QPSH}(X)$. Assume that $\varphi \preceq _ P \psi $. Then $\varphi \preceq _{\mathcal{I}}\psi $.

Proof. Thanks to 6.1.8, we have $\varphi \lor \psi \sim _ P \psi $. Take a closed smooth real $(1,1)$-form $\theta $ on $X$ such that $\varphi ,\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$. Then by proposition 3.2.13, we have

\[ \psi \sim _{\mathcal{I}} P_{\theta }[\psi ]= P_{\theta }\left[\varphi \lor \psi \right]\sim _{\mathcal{I}} \varphi \lor \psi . \]

Our assertion follows from the $\mathcal{I}$-version of 6.1.8. $\square$
