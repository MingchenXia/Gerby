From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Corollary 6.2.21

Corollary 6.2.21. Let $(\varphi _ i)_{i\in I}, (\psi _ i)_{i\in I}, (\eta _ i)_{i\in I}$ be three nets in $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ and $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$. Assume that

  1. $\psi _ i\preceq _ P \varphi _ i\preceq _ P \eta _ i$ for each $i\in I$;

  2. $\eta _ i\xrightarrow {d_ S} \varphi $, $\psi _ i\xrightarrow {d_ S} \varphi $.

Then $\varphi _ i\xrightarrow {d_ S}\varphi $.

Proof. By corollary 6.2.19, we may replace $\theta $ by $\theta +\omega $, where $\omega $ is a Kähler form on $X$. In particular, we may assume that $\varphi _ i,\psi _ i,\eta _ i\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ for all $i\in I$. By proposition 6.2.4, we may assume that $\varphi _ i,\psi _ i,\eta _ i$ are model potentials for all $i\in I$ and hence $\psi _ i \leq \varphi _ i\leq \eta _ i$ for all $i\in I$.

It follows from theorem 2.4.8 that for each $k=0,\ldots ,n$, we have

\[ \int _ X \theta _{\psi _ i}^ k\wedge \theta _{V_{\theta }}^{n-k}\leq \int _ X \theta _{\varphi _ i}^ k\wedge \theta _{V_{\theta }}^{n-k}\leq \int _ X \theta _{\eta _ i}^ k\wedge \theta _{V_{\theta }}^{n-k} \]

for all $i\in I$. By theorem 6.2.17, the limits, with respect to $i\in I$, of the two outer terms are $\int _ X \theta _{\varphi }^ k\wedge \theta _{V_{\theta }}^{n-k}$. It follows that

6.67
\begin{equation} \lim _{i\in I}\int _ X \theta _{\varphi _ i}^ k\wedge \theta _{V_{\theta }}^{n-k}=\int _ X \theta _{\varphi }^ k\wedge \theta _{V_{\theta }}^{n-k}. \end{equation}

By corollary 6.2.18, it remains to prove that $\varphi _ i \lor \varphi \xrightarrow {d_ S}\varphi $. By corollary 6.2.18 and proposition 6.1.20, up to replacing $\psi _ i$ (resp. $\varphi _ i$, $\eta _ i$) by $\psi _ i\lor \varphi $ (resp. $\varphi _ i\lor \varphi $, $\eta _ i\lor \varphi $), we may assume from the beginning that $\psi _ i,\varphi _ i,\eta _ i\geq \varphi $. Now $\varphi _ i\xrightarrow {d_ S}\varphi $ by (6.67) and 6.2.5. $\square$
