Normalization mass quotient

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Theorem 15.10.1

Theorem 15.10.1. For every such pair $(\theta ,\varphi )$ and every bounded measurable $F$, the equation in the ordinary type of $\varphi $ has a unique constant and a solution unique modulo constants. Its normalization scalar is

\[ B_{\theta ,\varphi }(F)=\log m_\varphi -\log Z_ F. \]

For continuous real $F$, its supporting set is

\[ \mathcal A_{\theta ,\varphi }(F)=\{ \mathrm{e}^ F\mathrm{d}V/Z_ F\} . \]

For two such pairs on the same $X$, with the same $\mathrm{d}V$ and possibly in different classes,

15.37
\begin{equation} \label{eq:normalization-mass-distance} \sup _{F\in L^\infty }|B_{\theta ,\varphi }(F)-B_{\theta ',\psi }(F)| =|\log m_\varphi -\log m_\psi |. \end{equation}

Thus an invariant of these pairs is determined by the whole functional if and only if it is constant on every fiber of the map $(\theta ,\varphi )\mapsto m_\varphi $.

Proof. Existence follows from corollary 32.8.3. Indeed, a Kähler manifold has the bounded mass property by lemma 21.4.1, and since in a closed background all potentials of the ordinary type of $\varphi $ have the same mass $m_\varphi >0$, the reference satisfies the positive-volume model condition (30.10). Uniqueness modulo constants is the classical theorem on equations with prescribed singularities in closed big classes, which applies under the model and small-unbounded-locus hypotheses; see the notes in section 34.11.

Every potential of the ordinary type of $\varphi $ has mass $m_\varphi $, so integrating the equation forces $cZ_ F=m_\varphi $, which is the scalar formula. For continuous $F$, Jensen's inequality shows, as for (15.25), that the normalized exponential measure is a supporting probability, and differentiating at zero in both signs of every continuous direction shows that it is the only one. Subtracting the scalar formulas gives (15.37). Finally, $m_\varphi =\mathrm{d}V(X)\exp B_{\theta ,\varphi }(0)$, so the functional and the mass determine each other, and an invariant factors through either one exactly when it is constant on the fibers of the mass map. $\square$
