Bounded exact regularization

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Theorem 1.11.1

Theorem 1.11.1. For every $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\omega )\cap L^\infty (X)$, there are smooth functions $u_ j\downarrow u$ such that

\[ \omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ j>0. \]

The functions $u_ j$ are uniformly bounded.

Proof. Subtracting a constant, we may assume $u\leq -2$. Apply proposition 1.10.1 with $\theta =\omega $, obtaining smooth functions $v_ j\downarrow u$ and positive numbers $\varepsilon _ j\downarrow 0$ with $(1+\varepsilon _ j)\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v_ j\geq 0$. The compact sets $\{ v_ j\geq -1\} $ decrease to the empty set, since $u\leq -2$. After discarding finitely many terms, we may therefore assume $v_ j<0$ everywhere. Put

\[ u_ j=\frac{v_ j}{1+2\varepsilon _ j}. \]

Then

\[ \omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ j \geq \frac{\varepsilon _ j}{1+2\varepsilon _ j}\omega >0. \]

Since $v_ j$ is negative and decreases, while the positive denominator decreases to one, $u_ j$ also decreases, and it converges to $u$. Moreover, $u\leq v_ j\leq u_ j\leq u_1$, which gives uniform boundedness. Adding back the subtracted constant proves the theorem. $\square$
