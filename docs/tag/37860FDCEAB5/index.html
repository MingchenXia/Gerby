Proposition 3.1.39

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 3.1.39

Proposition 3.1.39. Let $(\varphi _ i)_{i\in I}$ be a decreasing net in $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ;\phi )$ with

\[ \varphi =\inf _{i\in I}\varphi _ i\not\equiv -\infty . \]

Then

3.89
\begin{equation} \label{eq:E_cont_decnet} \lim _{i\in I} E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _ i)=E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi ). \end{equation}

Proof. We first handle the case where $I=\mathbb {Z}_{>0}$. Namely, $(\varphi _ i)_ i$ is a decreasing sequence. Thanks to 3.1.36, we know that $E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _ i)$ is decreasing in $i$. So the limit in (3.89) exists and the $\geq $ inequality holds. Conversely, let $\psi \in \mathcal{E}^{\infty }(X,\theta ;\phi )$ and $\varphi \leq \psi $. We need to show that

\[ E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\psi )\geq \lim _{i\to \infty } E_{\theta }^{\phi }\left(\varphi _ i\lor \psi \right). \]

Replacing the sequence $(\varphi _ i)_ i$ by $(\varphi _ i\lor \psi )_ i$, we are reduced to proving the $\leq $ direction of (3.89) when $\varphi \in \mathcal{E}^{\infty }(X,\theta ;\phi )$.

In this case, thanks to 3.1.37, we have

\[ E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _ i)-E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi )\leq \int _ X (\varphi _ i-\varphi )\, \theta _{\varphi }^ n. \]

Our assertion follows from the monotone convergence theorem.

We now treat the general net case. By 3.1.36, the net $E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _ i)$ is decreasing and bounded below by $E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi )$. Thus

\[ L\coloneqq \lim _{i\in I}E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _ i) \]

exists and satisfies $L\geq E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi )$. It remains to prove the reverse inequality.

Since $\varphi \leq \varphi _ i$ for every $i\in I$, the hypotheses of corollary 1.5.6 are satisfied, and hence $\varphi _ i\xrightarrow {L^1}\varphi $. For each $k\geq 1$, choose $a_ k\in I$ such that

\[ \| \varphi _ i-\varphi \| _{L^1}<k^{-1},\quad i\geq a_ k. \]

We can recursively choose an increasing sequence $(i_ k)_{k\geq 1}$ in $I$ such that $i_ k\geq a_ k$ for all $k$. Then $(\varphi _{i_ k})_ k$ is a decreasing sequence and $\varphi _{i_ k}\to \varphi $ in $L^1$. Its pointwise decreasing limit is therefore equal to $\varphi $ almost everywhere, hence everywhere by proposition 1.2.6. Applying the sequence case to $(\varphi _{i_ k})_ k$, we get

\[ \lim _{k\to \infty }E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _{i_ k})=E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi ). \]

Since $L\leq E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _{i_ k})$ for every $k$, we conclude $L\leq E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi )$. $\square$
