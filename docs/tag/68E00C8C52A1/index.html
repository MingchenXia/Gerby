Theorem 6.1.17

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Theorem 6.1.17

Theorem 6.1.17. Let $\varphi ,\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$, $\varphi \preceq _ P \psi $. Then for any

6.16
\begin{equation} \label{eq:arangetemp_reproduce} a\in \left( 1, \left(\frac{\int _ X \theta _{\psi }^ n}{\int _ X \theta _{\psi }^ n-\int _ X \theta _{\varphi }^ n}\right)^{1/n} \right), \end{equation}

the function

\[ \eta \coloneqq P_{\theta }\Bigl (a\varphi +(1-a)\psi \Bigr ) \]

lies in $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$, satisfies $\eta \preceq _ P \varphi $ and

6.17
\begin{equation} \label{eq:path_env_reproduce} a^{-1}\eta +\left(1-a^{-1}\right)\psi \leq \varphi . \end{equation}

If in addition, $\varphi \sim _ P \psi $, then $\eta \sim _ P \varphi $ as well.

Proof. By our assumption and (3.5), we have $\varphi \preceq P_{\theta }[\varphi ]\leq P_{\theta }[\psi ]$. Due to proposition 3.1.6, the interval (6.16) remains unchanged after replacing $\psi $ by $P_{\theta }[\psi ]$. Fix $a$ in this interval. So by 2.4.15 and the discussions afterwards,

\[ \eta '\coloneqq P_{\theta }\Bigl (a\varphi +(1-a)P_{\theta }[\psi ]\Bigr )\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}. \]

We first check that $\eta $ is a genuine $\theta $-psh function. Choose a closed non-pluripolar set $K\subseteq X$ on which $\varphi $ and $\psi $ are bounded. Then the obstacle $a\varphi +(1-a)\psi $ is bounded above on $K$. Every candidate in the definition of $\eta $ is therefore uniformly bounded above on $X$ by 1.5.5, so $\eta \not\equiv \infty $ and hence $\eta \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )\cup \{ -\infty \} $. On the other hand, since $\psi \preceq P_{\theta }[\psi ]$, we can find $C>0$ such that $\psi \leq P_{\theta }[\psi ]+C$. Then

\[ \eta '-(a-1)C\leq a\varphi +(1-a)\psi \]

quasi-everywhere, hence $\eta \geq \eta '-(a-1)C$. Thus $\eta '\preceq \eta $, and the monotonicity theorem theorem 2.4.8 gives $\eta \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$.

By (2.32),

\[ \eta \leq a\varphi +(1-a)\psi \]

quasi-everywhere. Equivalently, (6.17) holds quasi-everywhere. Since both sides of (6.17) are $\theta $-psh, proposition 1.2.6 upgrades the inequality to an everywhere inequality.

It remains to prove the assertion about the $P$-order. Using proposition 3.1.24, we get

\[ a^{-1} P_{\theta }[\eta ]+\left(1-a^{-1}\right)P_{\theta }[\psi ] \leq P_{\theta }[\varphi ]. \]

By the assumption $\varphi \preceq _ P\psi $, we also have $P_{\theta }[\varphi ]\leq P_{\theta }[\psi ]$. Therefore,

\[ a^{-1} P_{\theta }[\eta ]\leq P_{\theta }[\varphi ]-\left(1-a^{-1}\right)P_{\theta }[\psi ]\leq a^{-1} P_{\theta }[\varphi ] \]

quasi-everywhere and hence everywhere by proposition 1.2.6. It follows that $P_{\theta }[\eta ]\leq P_{\theta }[\varphi ]$, namely $\eta \preceq _ P\varphi $.

Finally assume that $\varphi \sim _ P\psi $. Then $\int _ X\theta _{P_{\theta }[\psi ]}^ n=\int _ X\theta _{\varphi }^ n$ by proposition 3.1.6. Applying corollary 2.4.16 gives

\[ \int _ X\theta _{\eta '}^ n=\int _ X\theta _{\varphi }^ n. \]

Since $\eta '\preceq \eta $, the monotonicity theorem theorem 2.4.8 gives

\[ \int _ X\theta _{\eta }^ n\geq \int _ X\theta _{\varphi }^ n. \]

The reverse inequality follows from $\eta \preceq _ P\varphi $ and proposition 6.1.16. Hence $\eta \sim _ P\varphi $ by proposition 6.1.4. $\square$
