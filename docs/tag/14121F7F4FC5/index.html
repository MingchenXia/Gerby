Note. According to Ya Deng, the definition (19.5.42) of $\Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(\alpha )$ was what Demailly originally proposed for Deng's thesis. Due to the lack of the techniques of the trace operators, Deng had to work with analytic singularities instead. As a consequence, the transcendental analogue of proposition 19.4.32 is not obvious. This is one of the two main technical difficulties of theorem 19.5.4. This problem also led me to finally develop the theory of trace operators, a notion that had been on the author's mind for several years.
