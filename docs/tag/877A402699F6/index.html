Proposition 10.8.2877A402699F6

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 10.8.2

Proposition 10.8.2. Every solution satisfies $0\leq u\leq C$.

Proof. Put $\theta =\chi +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\underline u$ and $w=u-\underline u-\sup _ X(u-\underline u)\leq 0$. Since $\Gamma \subseteq \Gamma _1$, we have $\operatorname {tr}_\omega \mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}w\geq -\operatorname {tr}_\omega \theta \geq -C$. The Green-kernel argument in the proof of proposition 9.10.1, applied to the operator $\operatorname {tr}_\omega \mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}$, therefore bounds $\| w\| _{L^1}$ uniformly.

Center a holomorphic coordinate ball of fixed size at a minimum point of $w$, and rescale it to the real unit ball. For a fixed sufficiently small $\varepsilon >0$, set $q=w+\varepsilon |x|^2$ and $m=\min _ Xw$. The real contact-set construction of (9.22) gives a compact set $E$ on which

\[ D^2q\geq 0,\qquad q\leq m+\varepsilon /2, \qquad |B_{\varepsilon /2}|\leq \int _ E\det D^2q\, \mathrm dx. \]

On $E$, $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}w\geq -C\varepsilon \omega $. Choose $\varepsilon $ so that $C\varepsilon <r$. Then $P=\omega ^{-1}(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}w+r\omega )\geq 0$ and

\[ F(A_{\underline u}-r\operatorname {Id}+P)=F(A_ u)=h\leq H. \]

Lemma 10.8.1 bounds $P$, and hence the complex trace of the Hessian of $q$. The nonnegative real Hessian of $q$ on $E$ therefore has bounded trace, and hence bounded determinant, so the contact inequality implies $|E|\geq c>0$. Since $w\leq m+\varepsilon /2$ on $E$, the $L^1$ bound gives $c(-m-\varepsilon /2)\leq C$. This bounds the oscillation of $w$, hence that of $u$; with the normalization $\inf _ Xu=0$, this is the assertion. $\square$
