Chapter 20 · Hopf and locally conformally Kähler geometry · Tag 77FC30E16968

Hopf all metrics unbounded

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 8.6.3

Corollary 8.6.3. Every Hermitian metric on $H_ n$, $n\geq 3$, fails the bounded mass property.

Proof. For a Hermitian form $h$, compactness gives $h\geq c\omega _ n$ for some $c>0$. The potentials $cV_\eta $ satisfy $h+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(cV_\eta )\geq c(\omega _ n+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}V_\eta )>0$, and positivity of determinants bounds their masses below by $c^ n$ times the divergent masses in theorem 8.6.2. $\square$
