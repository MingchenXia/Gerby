From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Lemma 3.1.50

Lemma 3.1.50. Let $\varphi _ j\in \mathcal{E}(X,\theta ;\phi )$ ($j\in \mathbb {Z}_{>0}$) and $\gamma \in \mathcal{E}(X,\theta ;\phi )$. Assume that $\varphi _ j\leq \gamma $ for each $j$ and that $\varphi _ j$ converges to $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ with respect to the $L^1$-topology. Assume that there is a constant $A>0$ such that for any $j>0$,

\[ \int _ X (\varphi _ j-\gamma )\, \theta _{\varphi _ j}^ n\geq -A. \]

Then $\varphi \in \mathcal{E}(X,\theta ;\phi )$ and

3.163
\begin{equation} \int _ X (\varphi -\gamma )\, \theta _{\varphi }^ n\geq - 2^{n+3}A. \end{equation}

Proof. Replacing simultaneously $\gamma ,\varphi _ j,\varphi $ by $\gamma -B,\varphi _ j-B,\varphi -B$ for $B\gg 1$, we may assume that $\gamma \leq 0$. The assumptions and the conclusion are invariant under this translation. Since $\gamma \in \mathcal E(X,\theta ;\phi )$ and $P_\theta [\phi ]=\phi $, we then have $\gamma \leq \phi $.

Step 1. Assume that $(\varphi _ j)_ j$ is decreasing. In this case, we prove

3.164
\begin{equation} \int _ X (\varphi -\gamma )\, \theta _{\varphi }^ n\geq -4A. \end{equation}

By 3.1.43, for any $j, k>0$,

\[ \int _ X (\varphi _ j-\gamma )\, \theta _{\varphi _ k}^ n\geq -4A. \]

For any $C>0$,

\[ \int _ X \Bigl (\varphi _ j \lor (\gamma -C)-\gamma \Bigr )\, \theta _{\varphi _ k}^ n\geq \int _ X (\varphi _ j-\gamma ) \, \theta _{\varphi _ k}^ n\geq -4A. \]

Letting $k\to \infty $, by theorem 2.4.7, we find

\[ \int _ X \Bigl (\varphi _ j \lor (\gamma -C)-\gamma \Bigr )\, \theta _{\varphi }^ n\geq -4A. \]

Letting $j\to \infty $, by the monotone convergence theorem, we get

\[ \int _ X \Bigl (\varphi \lor (\gamma -C)-\gamma \Bigr )\, \theta _{\varphi }^ n\geq -4A. \]

Then we let $C\to \infty $, again by the monotone convergence theorem, we conclude (3.164).

Step 2. In general, let

\[ \psi _ j=\operatorname *{sup}_{k\geq j}{}\! {}^{*} \varphi _ k. \]

Then $\varphi =\inf _{j>0}\psi _ j$ due to corollary 1.2.8.

For each $C>0$, let

\[ \psi _{j,C}=\psi _ j \lor (\gamma -C),\quad \varphi _ C=\varphi \lor (\gamma -C). \]

Then $\psi _{j,C},\varphi _ C\in \mathcal{E}(X,\theta ;\phi )$ since $\gamma -C\in \mathcal{E}(X,\theta ;\phi )$ thanks to the monotonicity theorem theorem 2.4.8. Observe that $\psi _{j,C}$ decreases to $\varphi _ C$ as $j\to \infty $. Moreover,

\[ \gamma \geq \psi _{j,C} \geq \psi _ j\geq \varphi _ j. \]

By 3.1.49,

\[ \int _ X (\psi _{j,C}-\gamma )\, \theta _{\psi _{j,C}}^ n\geq -2^{n+1}A. \]

By Step 1,

3.165
\begin{equation} \int _ X(\varphi _ C-\gamma )\, \theta _{\varphi _ C}^ n\geq -2^{n+3}A. \end{equation}

In particular, by the plurifine locality of the non-pluripolar product proposition 2.2.2 1,

\[ \int _{\{ \varphi >\gamma -C\} }(\varphi -\gamma )\, \theta _{\varphi }^ n\geq -2^{n+3}A. \]

Letting $C\to \infty $, we conclude (3.163). As a consequence,

\[ \int _ X (\gamma -\varphi )\, \theta _{\varphi }^ n<\infty . \]

We still have to prove that $\varphi \in \mathcal{E}(X,\theta ;\phi )$. In fact, by (3.165), for any $C>0$,

\[ \int _{\{ \varphi \leq \gamma -C\} }\, \theta _{\varphi _ C}^ n\leq \frac{1}{C}\int _ X (\gamma -\varphi _ C)\, \theta _{\varphi _ C}^ n\leq 2^{n+3}C^{-1}A. \]

Using the monotonicity theorem theorem 2.4.8 and the plurifine locality of the non-pluripolar product proposition 2.2.2 1, we find

\[ \int _ X \theta _{\phi }^ n=\int _ X \theta _{\varphi _ C}^ n=\int _{\{ \varphi \leq \gamma -C\} }\, \theta _{\varphi _ C}^ n+\int _{\{ \varphi >\gamma -C\} }\, \theta _{\varphi }^ n. \]

Letting $C\to \infty $, we conclude that

\[ \int _ X \theta _{\varphi }^ n=\int _ X \theta _{\phi }^ n. \]

This completes the proof. $\square$
