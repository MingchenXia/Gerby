11.6 Smooth nonuniqueness for a degenerate densityD85397FA2C2A

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · 11.6 Smooth nonuniqueness for a degenerate density

The first example places the freedom in the potential inside an open region where the density vanishes. Three independent semipositive forms of rank one reduce equality of the Monge–Ampère measures to equality of products of three scalar coefficients. We construct two semipositive forms with the same cube, and then add the same exact correction to both to obtain a strictly positive background.

In this section and the following ones, $X=\mathbb P(\mathbb C^2\oplus \mathbb C^2)=\mathbb P^3$, with block homogeneous coordinates $[A:B]$, where $A,B\in \mathbb C^2$, and $\omega _ X$ is its Fubini–Study form. Here $A$ and $B$ denote coordinates, not the forms of (11.1); the two solution forms will be written $A_0$ and $B_0$.

Theorem 11.6.1. On $X=\mathbb P^3$ there exist a smooth Hermitian form $\omega $ with $\mathrm{d}\omega \ne 0$, a smooth nonnegative nonzero function $f$, and distinct smooth functions $u,v\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\omega )$ such that

\[ \sup _ Xu=\sup _ Xv=0,\qquad (\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u)^3=f\omega _ X^3=(\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v)^3. \]

The density vanishes on a nonempty open set, and both potentials have minimal singularities.
