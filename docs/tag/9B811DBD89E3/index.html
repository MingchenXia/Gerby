Chapter 19 · Non-Archimedean pluripotential theory · Tag 9B811DBD89E3

Theorem 13.4.24

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Theorem 13.4.24

Theorem 13.4.24. The map (13.46) is an order preserving bijection.

Proof. The map (13.46) is an order preserving injection by 13.4.21. It remains to prove that it is surjective. Let $\phi \in \mathrm{PSH}(L^{\mathrm{An}})$. We want to construct $\Gamma \in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )$ with $\Gamma ^{\mathrm{An}}=\phi $.

Let $H$ be an ample line bundle and $(\epsilon _ i)_ i$ be a decreasing sequence of rational numbers with limit $0$, $\phi _ i\in \mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{gf}}_{\mathbb {Q}}((L+\epsilon _ i H)^{\mathrm{An}})$ such that

\[ \phi =\inf _{i>0}\phi _ i. \]

The existence of these data is guaranteed by theorem 13.4.6. Fix a Kähler form $\omega \in c_1(H)$.

Thanks to 13.4.22, we can find $\Gamma ^ i\in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta +\epsilon _ i \omega )$ with $(\Gamma ^ i)^{\mathrm{An}}=\phi _ i$. It follows from 13.4.21 that

\[ \Gamma ^ i\geq P_{\theta +\epsilon _ i\omega }\left[ \Gamma ^{i+1} \right]_{\mathcal{I}} \geq \Gamma ^{i+1}. \]

Therefore, for any $\omega '\in \textup{Käh}(X)$, after discarding finitely many $i$ so that $\omega '\geq \epsilon _ i\omega $, the sequence $(P_{\theta +\omega '}\left[ \Gamma ^{i} \right]_{\mathcal{I}})_ i$ is decreasing. This does not change its infimum. We let

\[ \Gamma ^{\theta +\omega '}=\inf _{i\gg 1}P_{\theta +\omega '}\left[ \Gamma ^{i} \right]_{\mathcal{I}}\in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta +\omega '). \]

Note that the infimum is defined thanks to (13.48). It follows from proposition 13.4.23 that

\[ \left(\Gamma ^{\theta +\omega '} \right)^{\mathrm{An}}=\phi . \]

From this, we conclude that for $\omega ',\omega ''\in \textup{Käh}(X)$ with $\omega '\leq \omega ''$, we have

\[ P_{\theta +\omega ''}\left[ \Gamma ^{\theta +\omega '}\right]_{\mathcal{I}}=\Gamma ^{\theta +\omega ''}. \]

Indeed, both sides have analytification $\phi $, by (13.47) and (13.46). The injectivity part of 13.4.21 gives our assertion.

It follows that $(\Gamma ^{\theta +\omega '})_{\omega '\in \textup{Käh}(X)}$ defines an element $\Gamma $ in $\mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )$ and $\Gamma ^{\mathrm{An}}=\phi $. $\square$
