Hirzebruch non two convex complement

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 4.6.40

Corollary 4.6.40. For every $e\geq 0$, there is an ample rank-two bundle $E$ on the Hirzebruch surface $X=\mathbb F_ e$ such that $\mathbb P(\mathcal O_ X\oplus E)\setminus \mathbb P(\mathcal O_ X)$ is not $2$-convex. The bundle may be chosen with the slope-stability and Chern classes of theorem 4.6.30.

Proof. Use theorem 4.6.30 to choose $E$ whose dual Dolbeault operator lies in the $C^4$ neighborhood of theorem 4.6.4; the split dual bundle satisfies the hypotheses of that theorem by proposition 4.6.22. Thus $E^*$ admits no smooth complex norm with plurisubharmonic square, and corollary 4.6.39 with $r=2$ gives the assertion. $\square$
