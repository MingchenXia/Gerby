Corollary 6.1.10564E8A0E68F9

Corollary 6.1.10. Let $\theta $ be a closed smooth real $(1,1)$-form on $X$ representing a big cohomology class. Assume that $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$. Then

\[ \begin{aligned} P_{\theta }[\varphi ]=& \sup \left\{ \psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ): \psi \leq 0, \psi \sim _ P \varphi \right\} \\ =& \sup \left\{ \psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ): \psi \leq 0, \psi \preceq _ P \varphi \right\} . \end{aligned} \]

Proof. Note that $\psi \sim _ P \varphi $ implies that $\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ by proposition 3.1.6. We observe that

\[ \begin{split} & \sup \left\{ \psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ): \psi \leq 0, \psi \sim _ P \varphi \right\} \\ =& \sup \left\{ \psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ): \psi \leq 0, \varphi \preceq \psi , \psi \sim _ P \varphi \right\} \end{split} \]

since for any candidate $\psi $ for the first supremum, $\psi \lor (\varphi -\sup _ X \varphi )$ is larger than $\psi $, and is a candidate for the second supremum thanks to 6.1.8. So the first equality is a direct consequence of proposition 6.1.4 and theorem 3.1.9.

Next we prove the second equality. We only need to show that for any $\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ with $\psi \leq 0$ and $\psi \preceq _ P \varphi $, we have $\psi \leq P_{\theta }[\varphi ]$.

By 6.1.8 and example 6.1.3, we know that $P_{\theta }[\varphi ]\lor \psi \sim _ P \varphi $ and $P_{\theta }[\varphi ]\lor \psi \leq 0$. It follows from the first equality that $\psi \leq P_{\theta }[\varphi ]$. $\square$
