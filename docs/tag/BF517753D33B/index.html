Corollary 11.1.21BF517753D33B

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Corollary 11.1.21

Corollary 11.1.21. Let $T$ and $T'$ be non-divisorial closed positive $(1,1)$-currents on $X$. Suppose that $\{ T\} =\{ T'\} $. Then the following are equivalent:

  1. $\mathbb {D}(T)\leq \mathbb {D}(T')$;

  2. $T\preceq _{\mathcal{I}}T'$.

Proof. Let $E$ be a prime divisor over $X$. Choose a sequence of blow-ups with smooth connected centers as in the proof of theorem 11.1.15, so that $E$ appears as one of the exceptional prime divisors on the resulting model $\pi \colon Y\rightarrow X$. By (11.13), the difference

\[ \mathbb {D}(T')_ Y-\mathbb {D}(T)_ Y \]

is represented in the relative cohomology by

\[ \sum _ i\left(\nu (T,E_ i)-\nu (T',E_ i)\right)\{ E_ i\} . \]

Using proposition 11.1.1 inductively, or equivalently the negativity lemma for exceptional classes, pseudo-effectivity of this exceptional class is equivalent to the non-negativity of all coefficients. Hence $\mathbb {D}(T)\leq \mathbb {D}(T')$ is equivalent to

\[ \nu (T,E)\geq \nu (T',E) \]

for every prime divisor $E$ over $X$, which is exactly $T\preceq _{\mathcal{I}}T'$ by proposition 6.1.5. $\square$
