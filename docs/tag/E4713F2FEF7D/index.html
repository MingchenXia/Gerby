Proposition 15.15.5E4713F2FEF7D

Proposition 15.15.5. One has $E_ F^ H=\operatorname {pr}_1^*E_ F$ and

\[ \operatorname{MA}_ H(E_ F^ H)=\binom Nk\, \operatorname{MA}_\kappa (E_ F)\otimes h_0^ m, \qquad \operatorname{MA}_ H(E_ F^ H)(X)=\binom Nk V_0. \]

The pulled-back residual is globally $C^{1,1}$, and $E_ F^ H$ belongs to $\mathcal E_{\mathrm{env}}(X,H,u_\alpha )$. On each fixed product the uniform bounds and derivative stability of theorem 15.15.4 hold for these residuals.

Proof. Every competitor restricts to a projective slice as a $\kappa $-plurisubharmonic function or as $-\infty $ identically. To check the exact lower bound when $H$ is nonclosed, choose a smooth quadratic correction $q$ with $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}q\geq H$ on a coordinate polydisk and let $v_\epsilon =(v+q)*\eta _\epsilon -q$ on a smaller polydisk, where $\eta _\epsilon $ is the rescaling of a fixed smooth nonnegative radial probability kernel. Radial plurisubharmonic convolutions decrease to the canonical $v+q$, and

\[ \mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v_\epsilon \geq (\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}q-H)*\eta _\epsilon -\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}q \geq -H-o(1)\omega _{\mathrm{Eucl}}. \]

Restrict this smooth inequality to a fixed slice. The corrected restrictions decrease either to $-\infty $ or to a plurisubharmonic function in local $L^1$; in the latter case the background error tends to zero distributionally. Subtracting $q$ gives the exact $\kappa $ bound, and these canonical restrictions patch on the connected slice. The everywhere obstacle constraint now gives $v\leq \operatorname {pr}_1^*E_ F$ on every slice. Upper regularization preserves this bound. Conversely every base competitor lifts, since $h_0>0$, proving equality of the full envelopes.

The bounded truncations $\max (E_ F^ H,-j)$ are pullbacks. By proposition 1.7.1, lemma 1.7.3, dimensional multilinearity leaves only the term with $k$ projective factors and $m$ factor entries. Restrict to strict superlevel sets and let $j\to \infty $ to obtain the stated non-pluripolar measure and its finite mass. The residual regularity and uniform estimates pull back in a fixed smooth product atlas. Finally $E_ F^ H\geq u_\alpha -K_ F$. At $t=1$ take the stretching witness $v_1=E_ F^ H$; for $t>1$ take $v_ t=u_\alpha -tK_ F$, so that

\[ t^{-1}v_ t+(1-t^{-1})u_\alpha =u_\alpha -K_ F\leq E_ F^ H. \]

Together with ordinary domination this is definition 13.4.1, including the canonical values at the common pole. $\square$
