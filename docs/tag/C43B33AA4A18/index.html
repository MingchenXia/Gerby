Proposition 26.21.1C43B33AA4A18

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 26.21.1

Proposition 26.21.1. The following are equivalent:

  1. there is $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ with $\nu (u,D_ j)\geq a_ j$ for every $j$;

  2. the residual background $\beta _ D$ is pseudoeffective;

  3. for every Gauduchon metric $\gamma $,

    \[ \int _ X\theta \wedge \gamma ^{n-1}\geq \deg _\gamma D. \]

The first condition does not require $u-\sigma _ D$ to be bounded, nor the Lelong numbers of $u$ to equal the prescribed ones.

Proof. Suppose first that $u$ satisfies the first condition. By lemma 1.17.1, these Lelong numbers can be subtracted: $\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u-[D]\geq 0$. Consequently

\[ \beta _ D+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(u-\sigma _ D)=\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u-[D]\geq 0, \]

where the difference $u-\sigma _ D$ is first taken in $L^1$ and then replaced by its quasi-plurisubharmonic representative. This proves the second condition. Conversely, if $v\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\beta _ D)$, then $u=\sigma _ D+v$ satisfies

\[ \theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u=[D]+\beta _ D+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v\geq 0, \]

so its generic Lelong numbers along the $D_ j$ are at least $a_ j$. For $n\geq 2$, Lamari's duality, theorem 4.3.1, says that a smooth background is pseudoeffective exactly when all its Gauduchon integrals are nonnegative; applied to $\beta _ D$, together with the degree identity, it gives the equivalence of the second and third conditions. For $n=1$ the same criterion reads $\int _ X\beta _ D\geq 0$: necessity follows from Stokes' theorem, and for sufficiency one solves $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}f=(\int _ X\beta _ D)\mu -\beta _ D$ for a smooth real function $f$ with the scalar Green operator. $\square$
