From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Lemma 16.2.18

Lemma 16.2.18. Let $D'\Subset D$ be open subsets of $\mathbb R^ d$, let $e$ be a coordinate unit vector, and put $s_0=\operatorname {dist}(D',\mathbb R^ d\setminus D)$, with $s_0=\infty $ if $D=\mathbb R^ d$.

  1. Let $1\leq p<\infty $, and let $Z\in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(D)$ be such that the distribution $\partial _ eZ$ belongs to $L^ p(D)$. Then, for $0<|s|<s_0$, $\| \delta _ sZ\| _{L^ p(D')}\leq \| \partial _ eZ\| _{L^ p(D)}$, and $\delta _ sZ\to \partial _ eZ$ in $L^ p(D')$ as $s\to 0$.

  2. Let $2\leq p<\infty $, let $Z\in L^ p(D)$, and suppose that $\| \delta _ sZ\| _{L^ p(D')}\leq M$ for $0<|s|<s_1$, where $0<s_1\leq s_0$. Then $\partial _ eZ\in L^ p(D')$ and $\| \partial _ eZ\| _{L^ p(D')}\leq M$.

Proof. (1) Fix $s$ with $0<|s|<s_0$. We claim that $\delta _ sZ(x)=\int _0^1\partial _ eZ(x+tse)\, \mathrm dt$ for almost every $x\in D'$. For smooth $Z$ this is the fundamental theorem of calculus. In general, let $K\subset D'$ be compact; the set $K'=\{ x+tse:x\in K,\ 0\leq t\leq 1\} $ is a compact subset of $D$. The mollifications $Z_\varepsilon =Z*\rho _\varepsilon $ are smooth near $K'$ for small $\varepsilon $, they converge to $Z$ in $L^1(K')$, and $\partial _ eZ_\varepsilon =(\partial _ eZ)*\rho _\varepsilon \to \partial _ eZ$ in $L^1(K')$, by proposition 1.4.3(1) and lemma 1.4.1(2). Hence the identity for $Z_\varepsilon $ passes to the limit in $L^1(K)$; for the right side we use $\int _ K\int _0^1|F(x+tse)|\, \mathrm dt\, \mathrm dx\leq \int _{K'}|F|$, by Tonelli's theorem. This proves the claim. By Hölder's inequality on $[0,1]$ and Tonelli's theorem,

\[ \int _{D'}|\delta _ sZ|^ p\leq \int _0^1\int _{D'}|\partial _ eZ(x+tse)|^ p\, \mathrm dx\, \mathrm dt\leq \| \partial _ eZ\| _{L^ p(D)}^ p. \]

Similarly, since $\delta _ sZ(x)-\partial _ eZ(x)=\int _0^1\bigl (\partial _ eZ(x+tse)-\partial _ eZ(x)\bigr )\, \mathrm dt$ for almost every $x\in D'$, Hölder's inequality and Tonelli's theorem give, with $F$ the extension of $\partial _ eZ$ by zero to $\mathbb R^ d$,

\[ \| \delta _ sZ-\partial _ eZ\| _{L^ p(D')}\leq \sup _{|r|\leq |s|}\| F(\cdot +re)-F\| _{L^ p(\mathbb R^ d)}, \]

which tends to zero as $s\to 0$ by the continuity of translation in $L^ p(\mathbb R^ d)$ (lemma 1.4.1(2)).

(2) Let $\chi \in C_ c^\infty (D')$ with $0\leq \chi \leq 1$, extend $\chi Z$ by zero, and let $0<|s|<\min \{ s_1,\operatorname {dist}(\operatorname{Supp}\chi ,\mathbb R^ d\setminus D')\} $. Then $\delta _ s(\chi Z)$ vanishes outside $D'$, and on $D'$ it equals $(\tau _ s\chi )\delta _ sZ+(\delta _ s\chi )Z$. Since $D'$ is bounded and $p\geq 2$, Hölder's inequality gives

\[ \| \delta _ s(\chi Z)\| _{L^2(\mathbb R^ d)}\leq |D'|^{1/2-1/p}\bigl (M+\| \nabla \chi \| _\infty \| Z\| _{L^ p(D)}\bigr )=:M'. \]

The function $\chi Z$ lies in $L^1\cap L^2$, so its $L^2$ Fourier transform is given by the integral (theorem 6.1.2), and $\widehat{\delta _ s(\chi Z)}=m_ s\widehat{\chi Z}$ with $m_ s(\xi )=(\mathrm e^{2\pi \mathrm is\xi _ e}-1)/s$, where $\xi _ e$ is the component of $\xi $ in the direction $e$. By Plancherel's theorem, $\int |m_ s|^2|\widehat{\chi Z}|^2\leq M'^2$, and as $m_ s(\xi )\to 2\pi \mathrm i\xi _ e$, Fatou's lemma gives $\int 4\pi ^2\xi _ e^2|\widehat{\chi Z}|^2\leq M'^2$. Let $F\in L^2$ be the inverse $L^2$ transform of $2\pi \mathrm i\xi _ e\widehat{\chi Z}$. For a test function $\phi $ we have $\widehat{\partial _ e\phi }=2\pi \mathrm i\xi _ e\widehat\phi $, by integration by parts, so Parseval's identity, which extends to $L^2$ because the transform is unitary, gives

\[ \int F\, \overline\phi =\int 2\pi \mathrm i\xi _ e\widehat{\chi Z}\, \overline{\widehat\phi }=-\int \widehat{\chi Z}\, \overline{\widehat{\partial _ e\phi }}=-\int \chi Z\, \overline{\partial _ e\phi }. \]

Thus $\partial _ e(\chi Z)=F\in L^2$. Since $\chi $ may equal $1$ near any given compact subset of $D'$, it follows that $\partial _ eZ\in L^2_{\mathrm{loc}}(D')$.

Now let $D''\Subset D'''\Subset D'$ be open. By (1), applied with $D'''$ in place of $D$ and $p=2$, we have $\delta _ sZ\to \partial _ eZ$ in $L^2(D'')$, so along a sequence $s_ j\to 0$ the functions $\delta _{s_ j}Z$ converge to $\partial _ eZ$ almost everywhere on $D''$. Fatou's lemma gives $\| \partial _ eZ\| _{L^ p(D'')}\leq \varliminf _ j\| \delta _{s_ j}Z\| _{L^ p(D'')}\leq M$. Exhausting $D'$ by such sets $D''$ and using monotone convergence, we obtain $\| \partial _ eZ\| _{L^ p(D')}\leq M$. $\square$
