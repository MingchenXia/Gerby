Proposition 9.4.26

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 9.4.26

Proposition 9.4.26. Let $(\Gamma ^ i)_{i\in I}$ be a decreasing net in $\mathrm{TC}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ satisfying (9.96) and (9.97). Then $\inf _{i\in I} \Gamma ^{i}\in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta )_{>0}$.

Moreover, if $\Gamma ^ i\in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ for all $i\in I$, then so is $\inf _{i\in I} \Gamma ^{i}$.

Proof. The first assertion is an immediate consequence of proposition 3.1.25, except for the positivity of the mass at $-\infty $. Choose $\tau _0$ as in (9.97). Applying proposition 3.1.26 gives

\[ \int _ X \left(\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\inf _{i\in I}\Gamma ^ i_{\tau _0}\right)^ n =\lim _{i\in I}\int _ X\left(\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\Gamma ^{i}_{\tau _0}\right)^ n>0. \]

Hence the value at $-\infty $ has positive mass by monotonicity. The last assertion follows from proposition 3.2.18. $\square$
