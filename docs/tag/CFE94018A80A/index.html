From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 10.3.19

Proposition 10.3.19. Let $\pi \colon Y\rightarrow X$ be a modification. Then

\[ \Delta _{\nu }(\pi ^*L,\pi ^*h)=\Delta _{\nu }(L,h). \]

Proof. Thanks to proposition 3.2.7, we may assume that $\varphi $ is $\mathcal{I}$-model. By theorem 7.1.1, we can find a sequence $(\varphi _ j)_ j$ with analytic singularities in $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ such that $\varphi _ j\xrightarrow {d_ S}\varphi $. It is clear that $\pi ^*\varphi _ j\xrightarrow {d_ S}\pi ^*\varphi $. By theorem 10.3.18, we may then reduce to the case where $\varphi $ has analytic singularities. In this case, it suffices to apply 10.3.15. $\square$
