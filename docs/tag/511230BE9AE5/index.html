Inclusion of Okounkov bodies

The same corollary appears in the main chapter and the appendix. The appendix proof is retained.

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Corollary C.2.16

Corollary C.2.16. Suppose that $S,S'\in \overline{\mathcal{S}'(C)}_{>0}$ with $S\subseteq S'$. Then

C.12
\begin{equation} \label{eq:Deltacontainapp} \Delta (S)\subseteq \Delta (S'). \end{equation}

Proof. Let $S^ j,S'^ j\in \mathcal{S}'(C)$ be elements such that $S^ j\to S$, $S'^ j\to S'$. Then it follows from C.2.2 that $S^ j\cap S'^ j \to S$. Since $\operatorname{vol}$ is continuous, for large $j$, $S^ j\cap S'^ j$ has positive volume and hence lies in $\mathcal{S}'(C)$ by C.2.7. We may therefore replace $S^ j$ by $S^ j\cap S'^ j$ and assume that $S^ j\subseteq S'^ j$. Hence, (C.12) follows from the continuity of $\Delta $ proved in theorem C.2.15. $\square$
