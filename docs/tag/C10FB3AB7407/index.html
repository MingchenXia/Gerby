Radial selection for integrable densities

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · 14.35 Radial selection for integrable densities

The scalar and measure limits in corollary 14.34.2 do not by themselves determine a limit potential. For the sublogarithmic densities of lemma 14.29.1, the radial flux supplies the missing information. It also distinguishes convergence after subtracting the supremum from convergence after subtracting $\lambda ^{-1}\log b$, where $b$ is the critical scalar: the latter depends on a first moment of the limiting measure.

Use the product $X=X_0\times Y$, $Y=\mathbb P^ k$, $H=h_0+\kappa $, and the probability volume $\mu =V_0^{-1}h_0^ m\wedge \kappa ^ k$ of section 14.29, with $\dim X_0=m$, $v_-(h_0)=0$ and $k\geq 2$. Put $b=\binom NmV_0$, where $N=m+k$. For $0<\alpha <1$, let $U_{\lambda ,\alpha }$ be the greatest solution of

\[ \mathrm{MA}^{\mathrm{ext}}_ H(U_{\lambda ,\alpha }) =\mathrm e^{\lambda U_{\lambda ,\alpha }}q_\alpha \mu , \qquad \lambda >0. \]

The threshold of this forcing measure is $b>0$, by theorem 14.29.3, so the solutions exist by corollary 14.34.2.
