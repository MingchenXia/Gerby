Definition 11.1.3E6F6681E7170

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Definition 11.1.3

Definition 11.1.3. The volume of a pseudo-effective Weil b-divisor $\mathbb {D}$ over $X$ is

\[ \operatorname{vol}\mathbb {D}\coloneqq \lim _{\pi \colon Y\rightarrow X} \operatorname{vol}\mathbb {D}_ Y. \]

The right-hand side is a decreasing net due to proposition 3.2.12, hence the limit always exists. The volume of a non-pseudo-effective Weil b-divisor is defined to be $0$.

We say $\mathbb {D}$ is big if $\operatorname{vol}\mathbb {D}>0$.
