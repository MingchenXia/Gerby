Chapter 9 · Cohomology, positivity, and geometric loci · Tag D5CAAE8D5B60

Hermitian metrics whose ddc has a sign

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · 28.19.4 Hermitian metrics whose ddc has a sign

Finally, we show that no Hermitian metric on these manifolds has a $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}$ of constant sign, so that curvature criteria based on such metrics cannot settle the boundedness of the masses.

Proposition 28.19.6. Let $X$ be an Oeljeklaus–Toma manifold of type $(s,1)$ with $s\geq 2$. Then $X$ admits no Hermitian form $\widetilde\omega $ such that $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\widetilde\omega $ is semipositive, or seminegative, on every complex $2$-plane.

Proof. Suppose $\widetilde\omega $ is such a form, with $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\widetilde\omega \geq 0$ on every complex $2$-plane; the other case is analogous. Average the lift of $\widetilde\omega $ over the translations $(w,z)\mapsto (w+r,z+\zeta )$, $(r,\zeta )\in (\mathbb R^ s\times \mathbb C)/(\sigma _{\mathbb R},\sigma _{\mathbb C})(\mathcal O_ K)$, as in the definition of $Au$ above. The averaged form $\overline\omega $ is again $\Gamma $-invariant and Hermitian, its coefficients in the frame $\mathrm dw_1,\ldots ,\mathrm dw_ s,\mathrm dz$ depend only on $y$, and $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\overline\omega \geq 0$ on complex $2$-planes, because translations are holomorphic and positivity on $2$-planes is preserved by averaging.

Let $h(y)>0$ be the coefficient of $\mathrm i\, \mathrm dz\wedge \mathrm d\overline z$ in $\overline\omega $. Invariance under a unit $\varepsilon $ gives $h(\sigma (\varepsilon )y)|\sigma _{s+1}(\varepsilon )|^2=h(y)$, that is, $h(\sigma (\varepsilon )y)=\sigma _1(\varepsilon )\cdots \sigma _ s(\varepsilon )\, h(y)$, since $\varepsilon $ has norm one. Hence $h/(y_1\cdots y_ s)$ is invariant under $U$, and since $\Lambda _ U$ is a lattice it is bounded between two positive constants:

28.72
\begin{equation} \label{eq:ot-h-growth} c\, y_1\cdots y_ s\leq h(y)\leq C\, y_1\cdots y_ s. \end{equation}

Let $v$ be a nonzero vector in the span of $\partial /\partial w_1,\ldots ,\partial /\partial w_ s$, and let $P$ be the complex $2$-plane spanned by $v$ and $\partial /\partial z$. Write $\overline\omega =\sum _{a,b}g_{ab}(y)\, \mathrm i\, \xi _ a\wedge \overline\xi _ b$ with $\xi =(\mathrm dw_1,\ldots ,\mathrm dw_ s,\mathrm dz)$. Since the forms $\xi _ a$ are closed, $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\overline\omega =\sum _{a,b}\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}g_{ab}\wedge \mathrm i\, \xi _ a\wedge \overline\xi _ b$, and each $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}g_{ab}$ has only $w$-components. A term restricted to $P$ is nonzero only if it contains both $\mathrm dz$ and $\mathrm d\overline z$, which happens only for $\xi _ a=\xi _ b=\mathrm dz$. Hence the restriction of $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\overline\omega $ to $P$ equals the restriction of $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}h\wedge \mathrm i\, \mathrm dz\wedge \mathrm d\overline z$, and its sign is that of the value of $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}h$ on $v$. Thus $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}h\geq 0$ in the $w$-variables, and $h$ is convex in $y$.

Let $R>1$ and put $A=(R,R^{-1},1,\ldots ,1)$, $B=(R^{-1},R,1,\ldots ,1)$ and $M=(A+B)/2$. By (28.72), $h(A),h(B)\leq C$, whereas $h(M)\geq c(R+R^{-1})^2/4$, which contradicts convexity for large $R$. In the seminegative case, $h$ is concave, so $t\mapsto h(t,\ldots ,t)$ grows at most linearly as $t\to \infty $; this contradicts the lower bound $h(t,\ldots ,t)\geq ct^ s$ with $s\geq 2$. $\square$

For $s=t=1$ the same averaging produces a coefficient $h$ comparable to $y$, which may be affine; indeed the Tricerri metric is pluriclosed. In dimension three, the curvature criteria for finite upper and positive lower mass recalled in section 34.16 require a metric with $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\omega \geq 0$, respectively $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\omega \leq 0$, and proposition 28.19.6 excludes both on threefolds of type $(2,1)$.
