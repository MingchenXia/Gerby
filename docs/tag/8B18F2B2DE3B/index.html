Chapter 18 · Toric and convex geometry · Tag 8B18F2B2DE3B

Proposition A.2.9

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition A.2.9

Proposition A.2.9. Let $F\in \mathrm{Conv}^{\mathrm{prop}}(N)$. Then

\[ \nabla F(N)\subseteq \operatorname{Dom}F^*. \]

If moreover $F$ is closed, we have

A.10
\begin{equation} \label{eq:relintdomFstar} \operatorname{RelInt}\operatorname{Dom}F^*\subseteq \nabla F(N). \end{equation}

In particular, if $F$ is a proper closed convex function on $N$, then

\[ \overline{\nabla F(N)}=\overline{\operatorname{Dom}F^*}. \]

Proof. Suppose that $m\in \nabla F(n)$ for some $n\in N$. Then (A.9) holds. In particular,

\[ \langle m,n'\rangle -F(n')\leq \langle m,n\rangle -F(n). \]

It follows that

\[ F^*(m)\leq \langle m,n\rangle -F(n)<\infty . \]

(A.10) is proved in [Corollary 23.5.1, Roc70]. For the last assertion, it suffices to observe that $\overline{\operatorname{RelInt}\operatorname{Dom}F^*}=\overline{\operatorname{Dom}F^*}$. $\square$
