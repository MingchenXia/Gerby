Proposition 9.4.17

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 9.4.17

Proposition 9.4.17. Let $(\Gamma ^ i)_{i\in I}$ be a non-empty family in $\mathrm{TC}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ satisfying (9.87). Then $\operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma ^ i\in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta )_{>0}$. Moreover, if $\Gamma ^ i\in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ for all $i\in I$, then so is $\operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma ^ i$.

Finally, we have

9.91
\begin{equation} \label{eq:supsminfty} \left( \operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma ^ i \right)_{-\infty }=P_{\theta }\left[ \operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma ^ i_{-\infty } \right]. \end{equation}

Proof. The first assertion and the second follow from proposition 9.4.14 and corollary 9.4.11.

It remains to argue (9.91). For this purpose, it suffices to show that

\[ \left( \operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma ^ i \right)_{-\infty }\sim _ P \operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma ^ i_{-\infty }. \]

For any $J\in \operatorname{Fin}(I)$, it follows from corollary 9.4.11 and proposition 6.1.20 that

\[ \left(\bigvee _{j\in J}\Gamma ^ j\right)_{-\infty }\sim _ P \bigvee _{j\in J}\Gamma ^ j_{-\infty }. \]

From this, applying proposition 3.1.28, proposition 6.1.20 and proposition 9.4.14, we conclude our assertion. $\square$
