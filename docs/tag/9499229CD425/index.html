From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Definition 14.2.6

Definition 14.2.6. Let $\varphi ,\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$. Recall from section 13.5 that $\varphi $ is more singular than $\psi $, written $\varphi \preceq \psi $, if $\varphi \leq \psi +C$ on $X$ for some constant $C$; we then also say that $\psi $ is less singular than $\varphi $. A potential $\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ has minimal singularities if every $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ is more singular than $\psi $. If $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )\ne \varnothing $, put

\[ V_\theta =\bigl (\sup \{ \varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ):\varphi \leq 0\} \bigr )^*, \]

where ${}^*$ denotes the upper semicontinuous regularization.
