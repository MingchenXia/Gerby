Chapter 19 · Non-Archimedean pluripotential theory · Tag 492A145E9067

Proposition 13.3.3

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 13.3.3

Proposition 13.3.3. Let

\[ (\Gamma ^ i)_{i\geq 1}\subseteq \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )_{>0},\quad \Gamma \in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )_{>0}. \]

Assume one of the following conditions holds:

  1. The sequence $(\Gamma ^ i)_{i\geq 1}$ is decreasing and $\Gamma =\inf _{i\geq 1}\Gamma ^ i$. Assume that

    13.33
    \begin{equation} \label{eq:volGammalim} \operatorname{vol}\Gamma =\lim _{i\to \infty }\operatorname{vol}\Gamma ^ i. \end{equation}

  2. The sequence $(\Gamma ^ i)_{i\geq 1}$ is increasing and $\Gamma =\operatorname *{sup}_{i\geq 1}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma ^ i$.

Then

13.34
\begin{equation} \label{eq:contDHm} \operatorname{DH}(\Gamma ^ i)\rightharpoonup \operatorname{DH}(\Gamma ). \end{equation}

Proof. We may assume that $X$ admits a smooth flag $Y_{\bullet }$.

Assume 1. Let

\[ \eta \coloneqq \inf _{i\geq 1}\Gamma ^ i_{-\infty }. \]

Since $\Gamma _\tau \leq \Gamma ^ i_{-\infty }$ for every $i$ and every $\tau <\Gamma _{\max }$, we have

\[ \Gamma _{-\infty }\leq \eta . \]

By proposition 3.1.26, the decreasing limit $\eta $ is model and

\[ \int _ X\theta _\eta ^ n=\lim _{i\to \infty }\int _ X\theta _{\Gamma ^ i_{-\infty }}^ n=\lim _{i\to \infty }\operatorname{vol}\Gamma ^ i. \]

Using (13.33), this equals

\[ \operatorname{vol}\Gamma =\int _ X\theta _{\Gamma _{-\infty }}^ n. \]

Since $\Gamma _{-\infty }\leq \eta $ and both potentials are model, proposition 6.1.4 gives

\[ \Gamma _{-\infty }=\eta . \]

Thus

\[ \Gamma _{-\infty }=\inf _{i\geq 1}\Gamma ^ i_{-\infty }. \]

We want to derive (13.34) from 10.5.11. It boils down to prove the following: For any $\tau <\Gamma _{\max }$, we have

\[ \Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(\theta ,\Gamma ^ i_{\tau })\xrightarrow {d_{\mathrm{Haus}}} \Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(\theta ,\Gamma _{\tau }). \]

For fixed $\tau <\Gamma _{\max }$, the potentials $\Gamma ^ i_\tau $ decrease to $\Gamma _\tau $. By proposition 3.1.26, their masses converge to the mass of $\Gamma _\tau $; hence $\Gamma ^ i_\tau \xrightarrow {d_ S}\Gamma _\tau $ by corollary 6.2.11. Applying the continuity of the partial Okounkov body map, theorem 10.4.13 4, gives

\[ \Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(\theta ,\Gamma ^ i_{\tau })\xrightarrow {d_{\mathrm{Haus}}}\Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(\theta ,\Gamma _{\tau }). \]

The proof under the assumption 2 is similar. We only need to apply 10.5.12 instead of 10.5.11. $\square$
