From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Theorem 21.4.2

Theorem 21.4.2. If $X$ fails the bounded mass property, then for every complete flag on $X$, every holomorphic line bundle $L$ and every graded subalgebra $W_\bullet $ of its section ring as above,

\[ \operatorname {vol}_{\mathbb R^ n}\Delta (W_\bullet )=0, \qquad \dim W_ m=o(m^ n). \]

Proof. If $\Delta (W_\bullet )$ had nonempty interior, $X$ would have $n$ algebraically independent meromorphic functions by proposition 21.3.2. Moishezon's theorem would then give a modification of $X$ from a smooth projective, hence Kähler, manifold, contradicting lemma 21.4.1. The body therefore has empty interior, and proposition 21.2.1 gives both conclusions. $\square$
