Lemma 23.5.1691315D9AA1C

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 23.5.1

Lemma 23.5.1. Let $w$ be a quasi-plurisubharmonic function on $Y$, and take the ambient manifold in (19.1) to be $Y$ itself. For every prime divisor $E$ on a smooth compact modification $\pi :Z\to Y$, the resulting self-trace satisfies

\[ \tau _{Y,w}(E)=\nu (w\circ \pi ,E). \]

In particular, for the potential $w$ of the closed positive current $T$, one has $N_ Z(T)=S_ Z(w;Y)$.

Proof. Since $w$ is not identically $-\infty $, its Lelong number vanishes at almost every point; hence $\nu (w,Y)=0$, and the self-trace is defined. Fix a divisor $E$ on a compact modification and apply lemma 1.17.8 on a coordinate neighborhood of a point of its image in $Y$. With $\nu _ E=\nu (w\circ \pi ,E)$ and $A_ E$ the log discrepancy of $E$, it gives

\[ \max \{ \nu _ E-A_ E/m,0\} \leq \frac1m\operatorname {ord}_ E\mathcal I(mw) \leq \nu _ E. \]

The limit along the integers defining $\tau _{Y,w}(E)$ is therefore $\nu _ E$. Since all coefficients agree, the two divisorial currents are equal. $\square$
