Chapter 10 · Hermitian geometry, torsion, and Gauduchon duality · Tag E2EF3C8673FC

Hirzebruch exact complement index

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 4.6.45

Corollary 4.6.45. For every $e\geq 0$, there is a stable ample rank-two bundle $E$ on $X=\mathbb F_ e$ as in corollary 4.6.40 for which $U_ E$ is $3$-convex but not $2$-convex. Its least smooth convexity index is three.

Proof. Use the ruling section $S$ and fiber $f$ of lemma 4.6.19, and put $H=S+(3e+1)f$ and $P=4H-f$. Choose $E$ as in corollary 4.6.40, $\mu _ P$-stable and with $c_1(E)=4H$. The intersection numbers of lemma 4.6.19 give

\[ c_1(E)\cdot P=4H\cdot (4H-f)=80e+28>0. \]

By the same lemma, $P=4S+(12e+3)f$ is very ample. Take for $\omega $ the pulled-back Fubini–Study Kähler form representing $c_1(\mathcal O_ X(P))$. Degrees with respect to $\omega $ are intersection numbers with $P$, so $E$ is $\omega $-slope-stable with $\deg _\omega E>0$, and corollary 4.6.44 gives $3$-convexity, since $n=r=2$. By the choice of $E$, the complement is not $2$-convex. Finally, $1$-convexity would imply $2$-convexity, so the least index is three. $\square$
