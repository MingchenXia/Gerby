Chapter 5 · Plurisubharmonic functions and positive currents · Tag 6C81352D9EE7

Interior mixed background

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 1.7.6

Lemma 1.7.6. Let $T_ i=\vartheta _ i+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ i\geq 0$, $i=1,2$, where the $\vartheta _ i$ are smooth real $(1,1)$-forms on $U$ and the $u_ i$ are locally bounded quasi-plurisubharmonic functions on $U$. If

\[ T_ i^ n\geq F_ i\beta ^ n, \qquad F_ i\geq 0\text{ measurable}, \]

then, for every integer $0\leq k\leq n$,

1.22
\begin{equation} \label{eq:interior-mixed-background} T_1^ k\wedge T_2^{n-k} \geq F_1^{k/n}F_2^{(n-k)/n}\beta ^ n. \end{equation}

Consequently, for $a,b\geq 0$,

1.23
\begin{equation} \label{eq:interior-minkowski-background} (aT_1+bT_2)^ n \geq \bigl (aF_1^{1/n}+bF_2^{1/n}\bigr )^ n\beta ^ n. \end{equation}

Proof. Fix $W\Subset U$. Since the forms $\vartheta _ i$ are smooth, there is a constant $C$ such that, for every $x\in W$ and every small ball $B_ r(x)$, the constant forms

\[ A_ i=\vartheta _ i(x)+Cr\beta \]

satisfy $0\leq S_ i\leq 2Cr\beta $ on $B_ r(x)$, where $S_ i=A_ i-\vartheta _ i$. If $q_ i$ is a quadratic potential for $A_ i$, then $u_ i+q_ i$ is plurisubharmonic on $B_ r(x)$ and

\[ \bigl (\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(u_ i+q_ i)\bigr )^ n=(T_ i+S_ i)^ n\geq F_ i\beta ^ n. \]

Apply theorem 1.6.4 to these two closed Hessians. After expansion, every term other than $T_1^ k\wedge T_2^{n-k}$ contains some $S_ i$ and is therefore bounded by $C'r\mu $, where

\[ \mu =\sum _{\ell +m\leq n-1}T_1^\ell \wedge T_2^ m\wedge \beta ^{n-\ell -m} \]

is a locally finite measure.

A finite cover and a partition of unity give, on $W$,

\[ T_1^ k\wedge T_2^{n-k} \geq F_1^{k/n}F_2^{(n-k)/n}\beta ^ n-C'r\mu . \]

Letting $r\downarrow 0$ proves (1.22), and the binomial expansion then gives (1.23). $\square$
