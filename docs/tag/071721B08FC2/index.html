Corollary 21.2.2071721B08FC2

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 21.2.2

Corollary 21.2.2. If the subgroup generated by $S$ is all of $\mathbb Z^{n+1}$, then

\[ \lim _{m\to \infty }m^{-n}\dim W_ m =\operatorname {vol}_{\mathbb R^ n}\Delta (W_\bullet ). \]

Proof. The semigroup $S$ satisfies the cone conditions established above and, by hypothesis, generates $\mathbb Z^{n+1}$. The semigroup counting theorem therefore applies to it. By lemma 21.1.1, the number of points of $S$ at height $m$ is $\dim W_ m$, and the height-one slice of the closed convex cone generated by $S$ is $\Delta (W_\bullet )$. $\square$
