From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Problem 16.6.79

Problem 16.6.79. Extend the complete topology of theorem 30.11.3 to ordinary models of zero lower volume and to backgrounds without bounded mass, in such a way that the closure of the analytic models is exactly the locus of ideal models. The extension should recover the curve topology of corollary 30.12.3 and the background compatibility of section 30.9, section 30.10, including higher-dimensional limits that lose bigness or positive lower volume.
