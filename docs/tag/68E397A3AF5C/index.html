Example 16.6.1868E397A3AF5C

Example 16.6.18. There is a Hermitian background $\Theta $ on a compact fourfold that fails the bounded mass property and admits a potential $u$ without an analytic ordinary type such that

\[ P_\Theta [u]=P_\Theta [u]_{\mathcal I}=\Psi , \qquad u\not\sim \Psi . \]

Both envelopes agree below every smooth obstacle, and their common model for the zero obstacle has both singularity volumes zero.

Proof. Let $(Y,\omega )$ be the Hopf threefold of theorem 8.0.1. On $\mathbb P^1$, let $\sigma $ be the probability Fubini–Study form, choose a point $D$ and a logarithmic potential $\psi $ with $\sigma +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\psi =[D]$ and $\sup \psi =0$. On $M=Y\times \mathbb P^1$, with projections $p,r$, put

\[ \Theta =p^*\omega +r^*\sigma ,\qquad H=Y\times D,\qquad \Psi =\psi \circ r. \]

Take the potential $U\in \mathcal E_{\mathrm{env}}(Y,\omega ,0)$ of infinite mass from proposition 13.6.1, which satisfies $P_\omega [U]=0$. Its multiplier ideals are trivial, so also $P_\omega [U]_{\mathcal I}=0$. Set $u=\Psi +U\circ p$. Here $\widehat0=0$, so proposition 16.6.13, corollary 16.6.15 give the envelope identities, the equality below all obstacles and the vanishing of the singularity volumes. Moreover $u\in \mathcal E_{\mathrm{env}}(M,\Theta ,\Psi )$ by proposition 16.6.14. Since $U$ has infinite extended mass, it is unbounded; restriction to a section outside $H$ therefore proves $u\not\sim \Psi $. If a potential $a$ with analytic singularities were ordinarily equivalent to $u$, proposition 16.2.7 would give $u\sim a\sim P_\Theta [a]=P_\Theta [u]=\Psi $, a contradiction.

Finally, let $V_ j$ be the smooth potentials of theorem 8.0.1. Their pullbacks are bounded and $\Theta $-plurisubharmonic, and proposition 19.9.1 gives

\[ \int _ M\mathrm{MA}_\Theta (V_ j\circ p) =4\int _ Y\mathrm{MA}_\omega (V_ j)\longrightarrow +\infty . \]

Thus $\Theta $ itself fails the bounded mass property. $\square$
