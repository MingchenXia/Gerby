Chapter 13 · Singularity types, envelopes, and models · Tag DDC2E6D4F75F

Affine transverse contact defect

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 13.1.28

Proposition 13.1.28. For every point of $K$ and every neighborhood $W$ of it, the set $K\cap W$ is non-pluripolar. For $\delta >0$ and $a=(2,w)$ with $w\in \overline D^{\, n-1}$,

13.22
\begin{equation} \label{eq:affine-contact-defect} V_{\delta ,K}|_ K=0,\qquad F_{\delta ,K}(a)\geq \frac{4\delta }{9}\log 2>0. \end{equation}

For every $r>0$, the same lower bound holds for $F_{\delta ,K\cap \overline B(a,r)}(a)$. At $a_0=(2,0,\ldots ,0)$, every plurisubharmonic function $f$ defined near $a_0$ satisfies

13.23
\begin{equation} \label{eq:transverse-psh-no-jump} f(a_0)\leq \limsup _{K\setminus \{ a_0\} \ni z\to a_0}f(z). \end{equation}

Thus $K$ is not plurithin at $a_0$, although the regularized envelope does not touch the obstacle at $a_0$ for any positive growth parameter.

Proof. By the preceding lemma, every neighborhood of a point of $K$ contains a nonempty open subset of $K$, namely a product of open disks. This proves the first assertion, which applies in particular to each $K\cap \overline B(a,r)$, $r>0$.

Put $b(\zeta )=\log |\zeta -2|-\log 3$. Since $|\zeta -2|\leq 3$ on $E$, we have $b\leq 0$ there, and $b(2)=-\infty $. For $\varepsilon >0$, the plurisubharmonic function

\[ u_\varepsilon (z)=\frac{v(z_1)+\varepsilon b(z_1)}{1+\varepsilon } \]

belongs to $\mathcal L_1$ and satisfies $u_\varepsilon \leq 0$ at every point of $K$, including the points with $z_1=2$, where it equals $-\infty $. Thus $u_\varepsilon \leq F_{1,K}$. Letting $\varepsilon \downarrow 0$ gives $v(z_1)\leq F_{1,K}(z)$ off the hyperplane $\{ z_1=2\} $, and lemma 1.3.3 extends this inequality everywhere. The value $v(2)=4\log 2/9$ gives the lower bound for $\delta =1$. Since $0$ is a competitor, $V_{\delta ,K}|_ K=0$, and scaling of competitors gives $F_{\delta ,K}=\delta F_{1,K}$. Replacing $K$ by a smaller compact set only increases the envelope, which proves the local assertion.

Finally, let $f$ be plurisubharmonic near $a_0$. If $f(a_0)=-\infty $, (13.23) is automatic. Otherwise, restrict $f$ to the complex line through $a_0$ in which only the second coordinate varies. Small circles in this line centered at $a_0$ lie in $K\setminus \{ a_0\} $, and the submean inequality on these circles gives (13.23). $\square$
