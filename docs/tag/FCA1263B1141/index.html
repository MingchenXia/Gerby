Chapter 9 · Cohomology, positivity, and geometric loci · Tag FCA1263B1141

Bc zero class infinite error volume

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 28.5.1

Proposition 28.5.1. Suppose there are smooth real functions $v_ j$ on $X$, for positive integers $j$, such that

\[ \omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v_ j>0, \qquad \int _ X(\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v_ j)^ n\longrightarrow +\infty . \]

Then for every $\varepsilon >0$,

28.2
\begin{equation} \label{eq:bc-zero-class-infinite-error-volume} \sup _{\substack {T\in \{ 0\} \ \mathrm{smooth,\ closed}\\ T\geq -\varepsilon \omega }} \int _ X(T+\varepsilon \omega )^ n=+\infty . \end{equation}

This occurs for the Gauduchon form on the Hopf threefold in theorem 8.0.1.

Proof. Fix $\varepsilon >0$ and take $T_ j=\varepsilon \mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v_ j$. Each $T_ j$ is smooth and closed and represents the zero Bott–Chern class. Moreover,

\[ T_ j+\varepsilon \omega =\varepsilon (\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v_ j)>0, \]

and its top mass $\varepsilon ^ n\int _ X(\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v_ j)^ n$ tends to $+\infty $. The functions $v_ j$ on the Hopf threefold are provided by theorem 8.0.1. $\square$
