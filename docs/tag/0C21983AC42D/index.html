Chapter 10 · Hermitian geometry, torsion, and Gauduchon duality · Tag 0C21983AC42D

Normalization dual

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 15.3.1

Proposition 15.3.1. The functional $H_\varphi $ is convex and weakly lower semicontinuous. For every $F\in C(X)$,

15.21
\begin{equation} \label{eq:normalization-dual-minimum} B_\varphi (F) =\min _{\nu \in \mathcal P(X)} \left(H_\varphi (\nu )-\int _ XF\, \mathrm{d}\nu \right). \end{equation}

The minimizers are exactly $\mathcal A_\varphi (F)$.

Proof. Each function of $\nu $ in the supremum defining $H_\varphi $ is affine and weakly continuous, so their supremum is convex and lower semicontinuous. By definition, $H_\varphi (\nu )-\int _ XF\, \mathrm{d}\nu \geq B_\varphi (F)$, with equality precisely when

\[ B_\varphi (G)+\int _ XG\, \mathrm{d}\nu \leq B_\varphi (F)+\int _ XF\, \mathrm{d}\nu \quad \text{for every }G\in C(X). \]

This is the defining inequality of $\mathcal A_\varphi (F)$, and the minimum is attained because $\mathcal A_\varphi (F)$ is nonempty by theorem 15.2.1. $\square$
