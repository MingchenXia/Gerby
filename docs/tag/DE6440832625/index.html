Corollary 32.7.5DE6440832625

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 32.7.5

Corollary 32.7.5. For fixed $\lambda >0$, write $u\star _\lambda v$ for the solution $a$ in the theorem. This operation on $\mathcal E_\varphi $ is commutative and associative, and, for $c\in \mathbb R$,

\[ (u+c)\star _\lambda (v+c)=(u\star _\lambda v)+c, \qquad u\star _\lambda u=u-\lambda ^{-1}\log 2. \]

Proof. Put $F_\lambda (u)=\mathrm{e}^{-\lambda u}\mu _ u$, with zero mass on the poles. The theorem says that $F_\lambda (u\star _\lambda v)=F_\lambda (u)+F_\lambda (v)$. By corollary 30.3.2, the map $F_\lambda $ is injective on $\mathcal E_\varphi $. Commutativity and associativity therefore follow from those of addition of positive measures, including measures of infinite total mass. Finally, $F_\lambda (u+c)=\mathrm{e}^{-\lambda c}F_\lambda (u)$, which together with injectivity gives both identities. $\square$
