Proposition 9.4.14

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 9.4.14

Proposition 9.4.14. Let $(\Gamma ^ i)_{i\in I}$ be an increasing net in $\mathrm{TC}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ satisfying (9.87). Then $\operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*} \Gamma ^{i}$ as defined in definition 9.4.13 lies in $\mathrm{TC}(X,\theta )_{>0}$. Moreover, if $\Gamma ^ i\in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ for all $i\in I$, then $\operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*} \Gamma ^{i}$ lies in $\mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ as well.

Moreover, we have

9.88
\begin{equation} \label{eq:Gammiminf} \left( \operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*} \Gamma ^{i} \right)_{-\infty }=\operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma ^ i_{-\infty }. \end{equation}

Proof. Fix $\tau <\sup _ i\Gamma ^ i_{\max }$. Choose $i_0$ such that $\tau <\Gamma ^{i_0}_{\max }$. On the tail $i\geq i_0$, the potentials $\Gamma ^ i_\tau $ form an increasing net of model potentials of positive mass, uniformly bounded above by $0$. Hence proposition 3.1.28 shows that

\[ \operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma ^ i_\tau = \operatorname *{sup}_{i\geq i_0}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma ^ i_\tau \]

is model. Its mass is positive by monotonicity, since it dominates $\Gamma ^{i_0}_\tau $. The decreasingness and concavity in $\tau $ follow from the directedness of the net and the corresponding properties of the curves $\Gamma ^ i$. Thus $\operatorname *{sup}_{}{}\! {}^{*}_ i\Gamma ^ i$ is a test curve. If all $\Gamma ^ i$ are $\mathcal I$-model, the same tail argument and proposition 3.2.19 show that the supremum is $\mathcal I$-model.

It remains to argue (9.88). Fix any $i_0\in I$. Since the curves are decreasing and are extended as in 9.1.2, the value at $-\infty $ can be computed by taking the supremum over rational $\tau <\Gamma ^{i_0}_{\max }$.

By proposition 1.2.5, there is a pluripolar set $Z\subseteq X$ such that for any $x\in X\setminus Z$,

\[ \left( \operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*} \Gamma ^{i} \right)_{-\infty }(x)=\sup _{\mathbb {Q}\ni \tau < \Gamma ^{i_0}_{\max }}\left(\operatorname *{sup}_{i\in I}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma ^ i_{\tau }\right)(x)=\sup _{\mathbb {Q}\ni \tau < \Gamma ^{i_0}_{\max },i\in I}\Gamma ^ i_{\tau }(x)=\sup _{i\in I}\Gamma ^ i_{-\infty }(x). \]

So they are equal everywhere by proposition 1.2.6. $\square$
