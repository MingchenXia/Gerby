Proposition 24.3.1A8BE1F55208F

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 24.3.1

Proposition 24.3.1. If $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(Y,\theta )$ has analytic singularities, then $\mathbb D_\theta (u)$ has a nef Cartier realization on a smooth compact modification.

Proof. Resolve the analytic singularity ideal as in the proof of theorem 23.6.2. On a suitable model $\pi :Z\to Y$ we obtain

\[ \pi ^*\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(u\circ \pi )=[D]+R, \qquad R=\beta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v\geq 0, \]

where $D$ is a finite effective rational divisor, $\beta $ is smooth with $\mathrm{d}\beta =\pi ^*\mathrm{d}\theta $, and $v$ is globally bounded after the choice of metrics on the line bundles of the components of $D$. Positivity of $R$ follows from divisorial removal.

Fix a Hermitian form $\gamma $ on $Z$ and apply proposition 1.10.1 to $v$ in the smooth background $\beta $. It gives smooth functions $v_ j$ with $\beta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v_ j\geq -\epsilon _ j\gamma $ and $\epsilon _ j\downarrow 0$. These forms represent the same affine class and have exterior derivative $\pi ^*\mathrm{d}\theta $, which proves nefness.

On every further modification $g:Z'\to Z$, the current $g^*\beta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(v\circ g)$ is positive by lemma 1.22.1. Its bounded potential has zero Lelong numbers, so the divisorial part of the full pullback is exactly $g^*D$. The residual affine class on $Z'$ is therefore $g^*\{ R\} $, which proves the Cartier realization. $\square$
