From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 3.2.7

Proposition 3.2.7. Let $\pi \colon Y\rightarrow X$ be a proper bimeromorphic morphism from a Kähler manifold $Y$ to $X$. Then for $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$, we have

\[ P_{\pi ^*\theta }[\pi ^*\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}=\pi ^* P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}. \]

Proof. Let

\[ \eta =P_{\pi ^*\theta }[\pi ^*\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}. \]

First take a candidate $\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ in (3.182), so that $\psi \leq 0$ and $\psi \sim _{\mathcal{I}}\varphi $. Then $\pi ^*\psi \leq 0$ and $\pi ^*\psi \sim _{\mathcal{I}}\pi ^*\varphi $ by proposition 3.2.3; hence $\pi ^*\psi \leq \eta $. Taking the upper envelope over all such $\psi $ gives

\[ \pi ^* P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}\leq \eta . \]

Conversely, let $\chi \in \mathrm{PSH}(Y,\pi ^*\theta )$ be a candidate in the definition of $\eta $, so that $\chi \leq 0$ and $\chi \sim _{\mathcal{I}}\pi ^*\varphi $. By proposition 1.5.8, there is a unique $\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ such that $\chi =\pi ^*\psi $. Since $\pi $ is surjective, $\psi \leq 0$. By proposition 3.2.3, the relation $\chi \sim _{\mathcal{I}}\pi ^*\varphi $ implies $\psi \sim _{\mathcal{I}}\varphi $. Thus $\psi \leq P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}$, and hence

\[ \chi \leq \pi ^* P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}. \]

Taking the upper envelope over all such $\chi $ gives the reverse inequality. $\square$
