Remark 10.4.17

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Remark 10.4.17

Remark 10.4.17. More generally, the same argument shows the following result: Let $k=0,\ldots ,n$ and $T\in \mathcal{Z}_+(X,\alpha )$ such that $\nu (T,Y_ k)=0$. Assume that $\operatorname{Tr}^{\alpha |_{Y_ k}}_{Y_ k}(T)$ is defined. Then

10.100
\begin{equation} \left\{ y\in \mathbb {R}^{n-k}: (0,\ldots ,0,y)\in \Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(T)\right\} =\Delta _{Y_ k\supseteq \cdots \supseteq Y_ n}\left(\operatorname{Tr}^{\alpha |_{Y_ k}}_{Y_ k}(T)\right). \end{equation}

Also note that this result extends [Theorem 3.4, Jow10] and hence gives simpler proofs of [Theorem A, Theorem B, Jow10].
