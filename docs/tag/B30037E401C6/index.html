Theorem 4.3.20B30037E401C6

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Theorem 4.3.20

Theorem 4.3.20. The function $d_1$ defined in definition 4.3.19 is a metric on $\mathcal{R}^1(X,\theta ;\phi )$.

Proof. Symmetry and the triangle inequality follow from the corresponding pointwise properties of $d_1$ on $\mathcal{E}^1(X,\theta ;\phi )$.

We next show that $d_1(\ell ,\ell ')<\infty $ for any $\ell ,\ell '\in \mathcal{R}^1(X,\theta ;\phi )$. Let $C_\ell $ be the constant in proposition 4.2.9, and set $\tilde{\ell }_ t=\ell _ t-C_\ell t$. Then $\tilde{\ell }$ is again a geodesic ray, $\sup _ X\tilde{\ell }_ t=0$, and hence $\tilde{\ell }_ t\leq \phi $ by proposition 3.1.13. Thus, by proposition 4.3.16,

\[ d_1(\tilde{\ell }_ t,\phi )=-E_\theta ^\phi (\tilde{\ell }_ t)=-tE_\theta ^\phi (\tilde{\ell }_1). \]

Moreover, by (3.71), $d_1(\ell _ t,\tilde{\ell }_ t)=|C_\ell |t\int _ X\theta _{\phi }^ n$. Hence $d_1(\ell _ t,\phi )$ grows at most linearly in $t$. The same argument applies to $\ell '$, and the triangle inequality gives $d_1(\ell ,\ell ')<\infty $.

It remains to prove separation: suppose that $\ell ,\ell '\in \mathcal{R}^1(X,\theta ;\phi )$ and $d_1(\ell ,\ell ')=0$.

Fix $s>0$. Then it follows from proposition 4.3.18 that

\[ \frac{d_1(\ell _ s,\ell '_ s)}{s}\leq \lim _{t\to \infty }\frac{d_1(\ell _ t,\ell '_ t)}{t}=d_1(\ell ,\ell ')=0. \]

Therefore, $\ell _ s=\ell '_ s$ and hence $\ell =\ell '$. $\square$
