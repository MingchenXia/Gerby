Example 1.7.6AAD0C8775B34

Example 1.7.6. As an important example of 1.7.5, given $T=\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi \in \mathcal{Z}_+(X)$ and $x\in X$, we define

1.43
\begin{equation} \nu (T,x)=\nu (\varphi ,x). \end{equation}

Again, as in 1.4.2, this differs from the definitions in some literature by a factor of $2$. But given our normalization

\[ \mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}=\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2\pi }\partial \overline{\partial }, \]

(1.43) seems to be the most natural choice.

The key example to keep in mind is the following:

\[ \nu ([0],0)=1, \]

where $[0]$ is the current of integration at $0\in \mathbb {P}^1$. In fact, as a simple application of the Green's second identity, one can verify that

\[ \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2\pi }\partial \overline{\partial }\log |z|^2=\delta _0, \]

where the right-hand side is the Dirac delta distribution at $0\in \mathbb {C}$.
