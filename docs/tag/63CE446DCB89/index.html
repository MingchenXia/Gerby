Proposition 10.3.23

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 10.3.23

Proposition 10.3.23. As $\mathbb {Q}_{>0}\ni \delta \searrow 0$, the convex bodies $\Delta _{\nu }(\theta +\delta \omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )$ are decreasing and

\[ \Delta _{\nu }(\theta +\delta \omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )\xrightarrow {d_{\mathrm{Haus}}} \Delta _{\nu }(\theta _{\varphi }). \]

Proof. Let $0\leq \delta <\delta '$ be two rational numbers. Take $C\in \mathbb {N}_{>0}$ divisible enough, so that $C\delta $ and $C\delta '$ are both integers. Then by proposition 10.3.20,

\[ \Delta _{\nu }(C\theta +C\delta \omega +C\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )\subseteq \Delta _{\nu }(C\theta +C\delta '\omega +C\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi ). \]

It follows that

\[ \Delta _{\nu }(\theta +\delta \omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )\subseteq \Delta _{\nu }(\theta +\delta '\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi ). \]

On the other hand,

\[ \operatorname{vol}\Delta _{\nu }(\theta +\delta \omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )=\frac{1}{n!}\operatorname{vol}(\theta +\delta \omega )_{\varphi }. \]

As $\delta \to 0+$, the right-hand side converges to

\[ \operatorname{vol}\Delta _{\nu }(\theta ,\varphi )=\frac{1}{n!}\operatorname{vol}\theta _{\varphi }, \]

thanks to proposition 7.3.4. Our assertion therefore follows. $\square$
