Lemma 28.18.11959B582751F

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 28.18.1

Lemma 28.18.1. The family is smooth and projective with rational surface fibers, and $L$ is effective on $\mathcal X$. For every $t\ne 0$, $L_ t$ is generated by global sections, has square zero and is nef. For every integer $m\geq 1$,

\[ H^0(X_ t,K_{X_ t}+mL_ t)=0\qquad (t\ne 0). \]

Proof. Near one of the sections, coordinates centered on it identify the blowup with $\operatorname {Bl}_0(\mathbb C^2)\times \Delta $. Since the four centers are disjoint, these charts prove smoothness and the submersion property. The blowup of the projective morphism $\mathbb P^2\times \Delta \to \Delta $ is projective and proper, and each fiber $X_ t$ is the blowup of $\mathbb P^2$ at the four points $p_ i(t)$.

The quadratic forms

\[ F_1=x(z-ty),\qquad F_2=y(z-tx) \]

vanish along all four sections and therefore lift to sections of $L$. They are nonzero, which proves that $L$ is effective. For $t\ne 0$, their common zeros in $\mathbb P^2$ are exactly the four points. In suitable affine coordinates $(u,v)$ centered at $p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4$, their linear initial terms are

\[ (v-tu,-tu),\quad (-tu,v-tu),\quad (v-tu,v),\quad (u,v). \]

Each pair is linearly independent. Hence, after the exceptional divisors are subtracted, the transformed sections have no common zero on an exceptional curve. The two transformed sections therefore generate $L_ t$, and $L_ t$ has nonnegative degree on every irreducible curve.

On a fiber, $H^2=1$, $H\cdot E_ i=0$, and $E_ i\cdot E_ j=-\delta _{ij}$. Hence $L_ t^2=0$, and $K_{X_ t}=-3H+\sum _ iE_ i$ gives $K_{X_ t}\cdot L_ t=-2$. A nonzero section of $K_{X_ t}+mL_ t$ would have an effective zero divisor whose intersection with the nef line bundle $L_ t$ is $-2<0$, a contradiction. This argument also excludes a nowhere-vanishing section, whose zero divisor would have intersection zero. $\square$
