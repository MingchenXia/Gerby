Chapter 9 · Cohomology, positivity, and geometric loci · Tag C15ADAE71322

Positive trace bounded type obstruction

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Example 28.7.5

Example 28.7.5. Let $\mu :Y\to \mathbb P^2$ be the blowup of a point, with exceptional curve $E$, let $\omega _{\mathrm{FS}}$ be the Fubini–Study form on $\mathbb P^2$ with total volume one, and put $\alpha _ Y=\mu ^*\omega _{\mathrm{FS}}$ and $v=0$. The decomposition (28.10) holds on $Z=Y$ with no divisor, the residual class $\{ \alpha _ Y\} $ is nef and big, and

\[ \mathfrak r(0;Y)=\mathcal R(\{ \alpha _ Y\} ;Y)=1. \]

Nevertheless, there is no bounded $q\in \mathrm{PSH}(Y,\alpha _ Y)$ for which $\alpha _ Y+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}q\geq \delta \kappa $ with $\delta >0$ and $\kappa $ Kähler. Indeed, since $q$ is bounded, its restriction to $E$ is defined, and the disk criterion of lemma 1.22.1 gives

\[ \mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(q|_ E)\geq \delta \kappa |_ E, \]

because $\alpha _ Y|_ E=0$. Integration over the compact curve $E$ contradicts Stokes' theorem. Thus positive trace mass gives the one-sided comparison of proposition 28.7.3, but a Kähler current in the class may need additional singularities.
