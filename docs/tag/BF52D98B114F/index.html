Chapter 10 · Hermitian geometry, torsion, and Gauduchon duality · Tag BF52D98B114F

Gaud determinant subcritical

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Theorem 26.8.1

Theorem 26.8.1. For every $T\in \mathscr G_\omega $, the measure $\Gamma _0(T)$ is absolutely continuous with respect to $\mathrm{d}V$; we write $\Gamma _0(T)=k_ T\, \mathrm{d}V$ and call $k_ T\geq 0$ the determinant density of $T$. For every $1<q<n/(n-1)$ there is $C_ q>0$ such that

\[ \| k_ T\| _{L^ q(\mathrm{d}V)}\leq C_ q \qquad (T\in \mathscr G_\omega ). \]

Proof. Fix $p>1$ and a smooth strictly positive function $h$. By theorem 9.1.1 there are a smooth form $\Omega _ h=\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ h>0$ and a constant $c_ h>0$ such that

\[ \Omega _ h^ n=c_ hh^ n\, \mathrm{d}V. \]

The lower-density estimate of theorem 10.3.3 gives a constant $c_ p>0$, independent of $h$, such that

\[ c_ h\| h^ n\| _{L^ p(\mathrm{d}V)}\geq c_ p. \]

The pointwise matrix arithmetic–geometric mean inequality, applied to $\Omega _ h$ and the coefficient density of $T$, gives the inequality of measures

\[ c_ h^{1/n}h\, \Gamma _0(T)\leq \Omega _ h\wedge T. \]

Integrating and using $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}T=0$ yields

\[ \int _ Xh\, \mathrm{d}\Gamma _0(T) \leq c_ h^{-1/n}\int _ X\Omega _ h\wedge T =c_ h^{-1/n} \leq c_ p^{-1/n}\| h\| _{L^{np}(\mathrm{d}V)}. \]

Approximation by smooth strictly positive functions extends this bound to every continuous nonnegative $h$, and applied to $|h|$ it bounds the integral of every continuous function against $\Gamma _0(T)$ by its $L^{np}$ norm. Since continuous functions are dense in $L^{np}$, the duality between $L^{np}$ and $L^{np/(np-1)}$ shows that $\Gamma _0(T)$ has a density in $L^{np/(np-1)}$, with norm at most $c_ p^{-1/n}$. As $p$ ranges over $(1,+\infty )$, the exponent $np/(np-1)$ ranges over $(1,n/(n-1))$. This proves the theorem. $\square$
