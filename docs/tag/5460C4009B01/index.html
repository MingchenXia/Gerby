Corollary 12.12.105460C4009B01

Corollary 12.12.10. Under the hypotheses of 12.7.3, every bounded admissible solution with values in $[A,B]$ is its smooth solution. In particular, for every $a>0$ and smooth real $H$, the equation $H_ m(u)=e^{au+H}\omega ^ n$ has exactly one bounded admissible solution, and that solution is smooth and strictly admissible.

Proof. For a continuous admissible potential, smooth decreasing admissible approximations converge uniformly on compact $X$ by Dini's theorem. Thus 12.10.8, 12.6.1 define the same Hessian measure on the continuous class. The smooth solution of 12.7.3 therefore solves the bounded equation as well. Apply 12.12.9 with $\nu =\omega ^ n$ and $h_1=h_2=0$ to identify any other bounded solution in $[A,B]$ with it. For the final assertion, smooth existence is already 12.5.1, and 12.12.8 with $\nu =e^ H\omega ^ n$ gives uniqueness among all bounded solutions. $\square$
