21 Flags, convex bodies and section growth926A00196D5E

Let $X$ be a connected compact complex manifold of dimension $n$, and let $L$ be a holomorphic line bundle on $X$. Along a complete flag of compact complex submanifolds, successive vanishing orders turn each section of $L^ m$ into a lattice point, and the Gauduchon degree bounds of chapter 20 keep the normalized points in a compact convex body. For a graded algebra of sections with some nonzero piece of positive degree, counting these points gives the growth exponent and the leading coefficient of its graded pieces: the exponent is the transcendence degree of the field of section ratios, and the coefficient is an intrinsic lattice volume of the body. A smooth metric on $L$ supplies an additional bound: its maximal curvature rank on the semipositive locus bounds the growth dimension, even if its curvature has no global sign.

Not every compact complex manifold carries such a flag, but the blowup of a point does, and Hartogs extension identifies the sections on the blowup with those on $X$; the count therefore applies to every compact complex manifold. Growth of the maximal order $m^ n$ forces $X$ to admit a modification from a projective manifold, and hence to have the bounded mass property. On the Hopf threefold, where Hermitian masses are unbounded, positive-degree line bundles still have bodies that are triangles: their area detects quadratic section growth, although their three-dimensional volume vanishes.
