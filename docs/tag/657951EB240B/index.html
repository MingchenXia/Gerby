Definition 22.1.2657951EB240B

Definition 22.1.2.657951EB240B We define

\[ \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )=\varprojlim _{\omega \in \textup{Käh}(X)} \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta +\omega )_{>0}\footnotemark \]
Note. The cumbersome projective limit can be avoided if instead of relying on the language of quasi-plurisubharmonic functions, we use that of augmented nef b-divisors developed in definition 20.1.32 instead. But given the fact that these two formulations are completely equivalent to each other by corollary 20.1.34, we just stick to the slightly more traditional language here.
in the category of sets, where the transition maps are given as follows: Suppose that $\omega ,\omega '\in \textup{Käh}$ and $\omega \geq \omega '$. Then the transition map is defined in proposition 17.14.6:
A99876729E5F 22.1.3
\begin{equation} P_{\theta +\omega }[\bullet ]_{\mathcal{I}}\colon \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta +\omega ')_{>0}\rightarrow \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta +\omega )_{>0}. \end{equation}

Note that the transition maps are compatible:

\[ P_{\theta +\omega ''}[P_{\theta +\omega '}[\varphi ]_\mathcal {I}]_\mathcal {I}=P_{\theta +\omega ''}[\varphi ]_\mathcal {I},\quad \forall \, \varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta +\omega )_{>0} \]

for $\omega \leq \omega '\leq \omega ''$, since both sides are $\mathcal{I}$-model and are $\mathcal{I}$-equivalent to $\varphi $.
