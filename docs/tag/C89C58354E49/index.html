Theorem 4.3.13

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Theorem 4.3.13

Theorem 4.3.13. The metric space $(\mathcal{E}^1(X,\theta ;\phi ),d_1)$ is complete.

Proof. As we have seen in 4.3.3 and proposition 4.3.2, the triple

\[ \left(\mathcal{E}^1(X,\theta ;\phi ),d_1,\land \right) \]

is a rooftop metric space. Hence thanks to proposition 4.3.12, it remains to show that each increasing or decreasing Cauchy sequence in $\mathcal{E}^1(X,\theta ;\phi )$ converges.

We first consider an increasing sequence $(\varphi _ i)_{i>0}$ in $\mathcal{E}^1(X,\theta ;\phi )$. The Cauchy property simply means that

\[ \sup _{i>0} E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _ i)<\infty . \]

We claim that

\[ \varphi \coloneqq \operatorname *{sup}_{i>0}{}\! {}^{*}\varphi _ i \]

is the $d_1$-limit of the sequence. We first observe that $(\sup _ X \varphi _ i)_{i>0}$ is bounded, as a consequence of 4.3.8. Therefore, proposition 3.1.53 guarantees that $\varphi \in \mathcal{E}^1(X,\theta ;\phi )$, and hence our assertion follows from proposition 3.1.47.

Next we consider a decreasing sequence $(\varphi _ i)_{i>0}$ in $\mathcal{E}^1(X,\theta ;\phi )$. The Cauchy property simply means that

\[ \inf _{i>0} E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _ i)>-\infty . \]

We claim that

\[ \varphi \coloneqq \inf _{i>0}\varphi _ i \]

is the $d_1$-limit of the sequence. Equivalently, we need to show that $\varphi \in \mathcal{E}^1(X,\theta ;\phi )$ and

4.62
\begin{equation} \label{eq:E_cont_decnet_temp1} E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi )=\lim _{i\to \infty } E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _ i). \end{equation}

We first observe that $(\sup _ X \varphi _ i)_{i>0}$ is bounded, as a consequence of 4.3.8. Therefore, $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ;\phi )$ and $\varphi _ i\xlongrightarrow {L^1} \varphi $, see corollary 1.5.6. We claim that $\varphi \in \mathcal{E}^1(X,\theta ;\phi )$. For this purpose, we may assume that $\varphi _1\leq 0$. Since $(\varphi _ i)_ i$ is decreasing, $\varphi _ i\leq \varphi _1\leq 0$ for all $i>0$. As $\varphi _ i\preceq \phi $ and $\phi $ is model, each $\varphi _ i$ is a candidate for $P_\theta [\phi ]=\phi $, so $\varphi _ i\leq \phi $. Thus, using the energy formula (3.69) and the fact that all mixed terms are non-positive, we get for each $i>0$,

\[ \frac{1}{n+1}\int _ X \left(\varphi _ i-\phi \right)\, \theta _{\varphi _ i}^ n\geq E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _ i)\geq \inf _{j>0} E_{\theta }^{\phi }(\varphi _ j)>-\infty . \]

We can then apply 3.1.50, with $\gamma =\phi $, to conclude that $\varphi \in \mathcal{E}^1(X,\theta ;\phi )$. Our assertion (4.62) then follows from proposition 3.1.39. We conclude the proof. $\square$
