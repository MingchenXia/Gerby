Proposition 11.2.9

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 11.2.9

Proposition 11.2.9. The product in definition 11.2.3 is continuous along decreasing nets in each variable. In other words, if $(\mathbb {D}_ i^ j)_{j\in J}$ ($i=1,\ldots ,n$) are decreasing nets of nef b-divisors over $X$ with limits $\mathbb {D}_ i$, then

\[ \lim _{j\in J} \left(\mathbb {D}_1^ j,\ldots ,\mathbb {D}_ n^ j\right)=\left(\mathbb {D}_1,\ldots ,\mathbb {D}_ n\right). \]

Proof. This is a straightforward consequence of 11.2.2 4 and proposition 11.2.8. $\square$
