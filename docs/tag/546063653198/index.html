Zero boundary truncation mass

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Lemma 9.10.7

Lemma 9.10.7. Let $\varphi \in \mathcal E_0(B)$ and $T>0$, and put $\varphi _ T=\max \{ \varphi ,-T\} $. Then $\varphi _ T\in \mathcal E_0(B)$, and for every $p\geq 1$,

\[ \int _ B(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi _ T)^ n=\int _ B(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )^ n, \qquad e_ p(\varphi _ T)\leq e_ p(\varphi ), \]
9.56
\begin{equation} \label{eq:zero-boundary-truncation-variation} \| (\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi _ T)^ n-(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )^ n\| _{\mathrm{TV}} \leq 2e_ p(\varphi )T^{-p}. \end{equation}

Moreover, for every Borel set $E\subset B$,

9.57
\begin{equation} \label{eq:energy-measure-capacity-modulus} \int _ E(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )^ n \leq T^ n\operatorname {Cap}_ B(E)+e_ p(\varphi )T^{-p}. \end{equation}

Proof. Since $\varphi $ tends to zero uniformly at $\partial B$, there is an open neighborhood $V$ of $\partial B$ such that $\varphi >-T$ on $V\cap B$; there $\varphi _ T=\varphi $. Thus $\varphi _ T$ is bounded, nonpositive and plurisubharmonic, it has zero boundary values, and it coincides with $\varphi $ outside the compact set $\overline B\setminus V\subset B$. By lemma 9.10.6, the measures $(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi _ T)^ n$ and $(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )^ n$ have the same total mass, which is finite; in particular $\varphi _ T\in \mathcal E_0(B)$.

Put $F_ T=\{ \varphi \leq -T\} $. By maximum locality (proposition 9.2.7), the two measures agree on the complement $\{ \varphi >-T\} $. Since their total masses are equal and finite, they also assign the same mass $m_ T$ to $F_ T$, and Chebyshev's inequality gives

\[ m_ T\leq T^{-p}\int _{F_ T}(-\varphi )^ p(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )^ n\leq e_ p(\varphi )T^{-p}. \]

As $\varphi _ T=-T$ on $F_ T$, we get

\[ e_ p(\varphi _ T)=\int _{\{ \varphi >-T\} }(-\varphi )^ p(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )^ n+T^ pm_ T \leq e_ p(\varphi ). \]

The two measures differ only on $F_ T$, where each has mass $m_ T$; this proves (9.56). Finally, $(\varphi _ T+T)/T$ is plurisubharmonic with values in $[0,1]$, so it is a competitor for $\operatorname {Cap}_ B$ (definition 9.3.2), and therefore $\int _ E(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi _ T)^ n\leq T^ n\operatorname {Cap}_ B(E)$. Since the two measures agree on $E\setminus F_ T$, we conclude

\[ \int _ E(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )^ n \leq \int _{E\setminus F_ T}(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi _ T)^ n+m_ T \leq T^ n\operatorname {Cap}_ B(E)+e_ p(\varphi )T^{-p}. \]
$\square$
