Chapter 5 · Plurisubharmonic functions and positive currents · Tag 3B857D475432

Closed null current trace

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 1.25.4

Lemma 1.25.4. Let $T,S$ be closed positive $(1,1)$-currents on a complex manifold of dimension $n\geq 1$, both carried coefficientwise by a Borel set of Lebesgue measure zero. For any Hermitian form $\eta $,

\[ T\wedge \eta ^{n-1}=S\wedge \eta ^{n-1} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad T=S. \]

Proof. On a small coordinate ball, take plurisubharmonic local potentials $a,b$ with $T=\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}a$ and $S=\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}b$, by [Chapter III, Proposition 1.19, p. 135, DemCADG]. Put $h=a-b\in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}$. The trace identity gives $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}h\wedge \eta ^{n-1}=0$ in the sense of distributions. Dividing by the smooth positive volume form $\eta ^ n$ gives a scalar second-order operator with smooth coefficients whose principal matrix is a positive multiple of the inverse of the Hermitian matrix of $\eta $; it is uniformly elliptic on every smaller ball. Local elliptic regularity therefore makes $h$ smooth. Thus $T-S=\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}h$ is smooth and carried by a Lebesgue null set, so its coefficient measures vanish. This proves equality locally, and hence globally. $\square$
