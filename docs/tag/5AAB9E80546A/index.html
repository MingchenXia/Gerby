Proposition 1.25.55AAB9E80546A

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 1.25.5

Proposition 1.25.5. Let $\pi :Y\to X$ be a modification of connected compact complex manifolds, let $\theta $ be a smooth real $(1,1)$-form on $X$, and let $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$. Put $S=\{ u=-\infty \} $ and

\[ T=\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u,\qquad T_ Y=\pi ^*\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(u\circ \pi ). \]

Then

1.82
\begin{equation} \pi _*\bigl (\mathbf1_{\pi ^{-1}(S)}T_ Y\bigr )=\mathbf1_ ST. \end{equation}

Proof. By proposition 1.22.12, $u\circ \pi \in \mathrm{PSH}(Y,\pi ^*\theta )$. Change of variables on the locus where $\pi $ is an isomorphism, whose complement has Lebesgue measure zero in both manifolds, gives $\pi _*(u\circ \pi )=u$ as distributions and $\pi _*\pi ^*\theta =\theta $ as currents. Since pushforward by a proper map commutes with $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}$, we get $\pi _*T_ Y=T$.

For any Borel set $B\subset X$, the coefficient measures of $\pi _*T_ Y$ are pushforwards of the coefficient measures of $T_ Y$, multiplied by the smooth coefficients that arise from pulling back a test form. Restriction therefore gives $\pi _*(\mathbf1_{\pi ^{-1}(B)}T_ Y)=\mathbf1_ B\pi _*T_ Y$. Applying this to the Borel set $S$ proves (1.82); closedness of the restricted currents is not used. $\square$
