Conditional i characterization

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 30.8.2

Proposition 30.8.2. Let $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$, put $q=P_\theta [u]_{\mathcal I}$, and suppose that $\underline{\operatorname {vol}}_\theta (q)>0$. Then the following conditions are equivalent:

  1. $P_\theta [u]=P_\theta [u]_{\mathcal I}$;

  2. $\overline{\operatorname {vol}}_\theta (u)=\mathcal V^+_{\mathcal I,\theta }(u)$;

  3. $u\in \mathcal E_{\mathrm{env}}(X,\theta ,q)$;

  4. $P_{\theta ,F}[u]=P_{\theta ,F}[u]_{\mathcal I}$ for every $F\in C^\infty (X,\mathbb R)$.

If they hold, then also $\underline{\operatorname {vol}}_\theta (u)=\mathcal V^-_{\mathcal I,\theta }(u)$.

Proof. Recall that $q$ is an ordinary model and $u\preceq P_\theta [u]\leq q$. If (i) holds, then $P_\theta [u]=q$ has positive lower volume, and theorem 30.3.4 shows that both volumes of $u$ equal those of $q$; in particular (ii) holds, together with the final assertion.

Conversely, (ii) and $u\preceq q$ give $u\in \mathcal E_{\mathrm{vol}}(X,\theta ,q)$, and corollary 30.3.6 gives $P_\theta [u]=q$, which is (i). The same corollary shows that (i) is equivalent to (iii). By proposition 16.6.8, (iii) implies (iv), and (iv) with $F=0$ is (i). $\square$
