Corollary 10.4.22

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Corollary 10.4.22

Corollary 10.4.22. Assume that $T\in \mathcal{Z}_+(X,\alpha )_{>0}$. We have

10.108
\begin{equation} \label{eq:minOkounkov3} \min _{\mathrm{lex}} \Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(T)=\nu _{Y_{\bullet }}(T). \end{equation}

Proof. By theorem 10.4.20, it is clear that

\[ \min _{\mathrm{lex}} \Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(T)\leq _{\mathrm{lex}}\nu _{Y_{\bullet }}(T). \]

On the other hand, we clearly have

\[ \Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(T)\subseteq \Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(T+\omega ) \]

for any Kähler form $\omega $ on $X$. It follows that

\[ \min _{\mathrm{lex}} \Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(T)\geq _{\mathrm{lex}} \min _{\mathrm{lex}} \Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(T+\omega ). \]

By theorem 10.4.18, the right-hand side is just $\nu _{Y_{\bullet }}(T+\omega )=\nu _{Y_{\bullet }}(T)$. We conclude the proof. $\square$
