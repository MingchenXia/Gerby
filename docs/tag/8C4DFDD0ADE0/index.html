Corollary A.2.4

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Corollary A.2.4

Corollary A.2.4. Let $F\colon (0,\infty )\rightarrow [-\infty ,\infty )$ be a convex function. If we define $G\colon \mathbb {R}\rightarrow (-\infty ,\infty ]$ by

\[ G(\tau )=\sup _{t>0} \Bigl ( t\tau -F(t) \Bigr ), \]

then $G$ is a convex function and

A.7
\begin{equation} \label{eq:Leginvpartdef} F(t)=G^*(t),\quad \forall t>0. \end{equation}

Moreover,

A.8
\begin{equation} \label{eq:LegratQ} G(\tau )=\sup _{t\in \mathbb {R}_{>0}} \Bigl ( t\tau -F(t) \Bigr )=\sup _{t\in \mathbb {Q}_{>0}} \Bigl ( t\tau -F(t) \Bigr ). \end{equation}

Proof. We distinguish two cases.

First suppose that $F(t)=-\infty $ for some $t>0$. Then $F(t)=-\infty $ for all $t>0$ by the convexity of $F$. Our assertions are clear in this case.

Next assume that $F(t)\neq -\infty $ for all $t>0$. In this case, theorem A.1.15 guarantees that $F$ admits a closed proper extension $\tilde{F}\in \mathrm{Conv}(\mathbb {R})$ with

\[ \tilde{F}(t)=\infty ,\quad \forall t<0. \]

It follows from (A.6) that

\[ G(\tau )=\tilde{F}^*(\tau ),\quad \forall \tau \in \mathbb {R}. \]

Now theorem A.2.2 implies (A.7). Finally (A.8) follows from the continuity of $F$. $\square$
