Lemma 6.2.22

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Lemma 6.2.22

Lemma 6.2.22. Let $(\varphi _ i)_{i\in I}$ be a net in $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$, and $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$. Fix $\lambda >0$. Then the following are equivalent:

  1. $\varphi _ i\xrightarrow {d_{S,\theta }}\varphi $;

  2. $\lambda \varphi _ i\xrightarrow {d_{S,\lambda \theta }}\lambda \varphi $.

Proof. We have $V_{\lambda \theta }=\lambda V_{\theta }$. The middle expression in (6.24) for the pair $(\lambda \varphi _ i,\lambda \varphi )$ in the class $\lambda \theta $ is therefore $\lambda ^ n$ times the corresponding expression for $(\varphi _ i,\varphi )$ in the class $\theta $. The equivalence follows from corollary 6.2.7. $\square$
