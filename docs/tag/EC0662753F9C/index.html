Thin

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Definition 1.3.4
Definition 1.3.4. A subset $E\subseteq \Omega $ is thin
Note. A more proper name would be plurithin. But since we will never need the classical notion of thin sets à la Cartan in this book, we prefer omitting the prefix pluri-.
at $x\in \Omega $ if one of the following conditions holds:

  1. $x\not\in \overline{E}$;

  2. $x\in \overline{E}$ and there is an open neighborhood $U\subseteq \Omega $ of $x$ and $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(U)$ such that

    \[ \varlimsup _{(U\cap E)\setminus \{ x\} \ni y\to x} \varphi (y)<\varphi (x). \]

We say $E$ is thin if it is thin at all $x\in \Omega $.
