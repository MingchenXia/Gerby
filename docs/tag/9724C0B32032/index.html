Chapter 15 · Multiplier-ideal models and partial equilibrium · Tag 9724C0B32032

Proposition 14.2.7

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 14.2.7

Proposition 14.2.7. Let $(K,v)$ be a weighted subset of $X$. Let $(\varphi _ j)_ j$ be a sequence in $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ and $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$. Then the following hold:

  1. If $(\varphi _ j)_ j$ is decreasing, and $\varphi _ j\xrightarrow {d_ S}\varphi $, then

    14.44
    \begin{equation} \label{eq:PthetaKvarphij1} P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi _ j]_{\mathcal{I}}(v) \searrow P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}(v),\quad P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi _ j](v) \searrow P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi ](v). \end{equation}

  2. If $\varphi _ j \nearrow \varphi $ almost everywhere then

    \[ P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi _ j]_{\mathcal{I}}(v) \nearrow P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}(v)\textup{ a.e.},\quad P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi _ j](v) \nearrow P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi ](v) \textup{ a.e.}. \]

Proof. We only give the proof for the relative $\mathcal{I}$-envelopes; the proof for the relative $P$-envelopes is identical, replacing $P_{\theta }[\bullet ]_{\mathcal{I}}$ by $P_{\theta }[\bullet ]$ and using corollary 14.1.8 for the final candidate condition.

Set

\[ \eta _ j\coloneqq P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi _ j]_{\mathcal{I}}(v),\quad \eta \coloneqq P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}(v). \]

By 14.1.10, $d_ S(\eta _ j,P_{\theta }[\varphi _ j]_{\mathcal{I}})=0$ and $d_ S(\eta ,P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}})=0$ for all $j$. Moreover, theorem 6.2.27 gives

\[ P_{\theta }[\varphi _ j]_{\mathcal{I}}\xrightarrow {d_ S}P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}} \]

in case 1, and the same convergence holds in case 2 by corollary 6.2.9 and theorem 6.2.27. Consequently, $\eta _ j\xrightarrow {d_ S}\eta $ in both cases.

Assume first that $(\varphi _ j)_ j$ is decreasing. Then $(\eta _ j)_ j$ is decreasing and $\eta _ j\geq \eta $. Let $\eta _\infty \coloneqq \inf _ j \eta _ j$. The Monge–Ampère masses of $\eta _ j$ converge to the mass of $\eta $, by the preceding $d_ S$-convergence and theorem 6.2.17. Since $\eta _ j\geq \eta _\infty \geq \eta $, the monotonicity theorem theorem 2.4.8 gives

\[ \int _ X\theta _{\eta _\infty }^ n=\int _ X\theta _\eta ^ n. \]

Hence $\eta _ j\xrightarrow {d_ S}\eta _\infty $ by corollary 6.2.11. The uniqueness of the $d_ S$-limit gives $d_ S(\eta _\infty ,\eta )=0$. Since $\eta _\infty \geq \eta $, proposition 6.2.4 implies $\eta _\infty \sim _ P \eta $. In particular, $\eta _\infty \preceq _{\mathcal{I}}\varphi $ by corollary 6.1.9. The condition $\eta _\infty \leq v$ on $K$ quasi-everywhere is inherited from the $\eta _ j$'s, so $\eta _\infty $ is a candidate for $P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}(v)$. Thus $\eta _\infty \leq \eta $, and the reverse inequality was already observed. This proves the $\mathcal{I}$-part of 1.

Assume next that $\varphi _ j\nearrow \varphi $ almost everywhere. Then $(\eta _ j)_ j$ is increasing and $\eta _ j\leq \eta $. Put $\tilde\eta \coloneqq \operatorname *{sup}_{}{}\! {}^{*}_ j \eta _ j$. By corollary 14.1.6 and corollary 6.2.9, we have $\eta _ j\xrightarrow {d_ S}\tilde\eta $. The $d_ S$-convergence above therefore gives $d_ S(\tilde\eta ,\eta )=0$, hence $\tilde\eta \sim _ P \eta $ by proposition 6.2.4. In particular, $\tilde\eta \preceq _{\mathcal{I}}\varphi $ by corollary 6.1.9. Also $\tilde\eta \leq v$ on $K$ quasi-everywhere by proposition 1.2.5. Hence $\tilde\eta $ is a candidate for $P_{\theta ,K}[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}(v)$, so $\tilde\eta \leq \eta $. Since $\eta _ j\leq \eta $ for all $j$, this proves $\eta _ j\nearrow \eta $ almost everywhere. $\square$
