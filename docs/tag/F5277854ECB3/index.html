Chapter 5 · Plurisubharmonic functions and positive currents · Tag F5277854ECB3

Divisorial current ordinary type

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 1.17.5

Corollary 1.17.5. Let $Z$ be a connected compact complex manifold, let $(D_ j)$ be a finite or countable family of distinct prime divisors on $Z$, and let $c_ j>0$. Suppose that $S=\sum _ jc_ j[D_ j]$ is a closed positive current of finite mass. Then there are quasi-plurisubharmonic functions $v$ on $Z$ satisfying

\[ \mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v=S-\beta \]

for a smooth closed real $(1,1)$-form $\beta $, and any two such functions differ by a smooth function. In particular, they determine a canonical ordinary singularity type, which depends only on $S$.

Proof. Lemma 1.17.3, applied to $T=S$, gives the decomposition, with $v$ quasi-plurisubharmonic since $S\geq 0$, and shows that any two such potentials differ by a smooth function. By proposition 1.12.2, they have the same ordinary singularity type. $\square$
