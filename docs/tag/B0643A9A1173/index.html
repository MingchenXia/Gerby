Lemma 12.1.5B0643A9A1173

Lemma 12.1.5. If $0\leq r<m$, $\alpha _1,\ldots ,\alpha _ r\in \Gamma _ m(\omega )$, and $\Xi $ is a smooth complementary form, then

\[ |\alpha _1\wedge \cdots \wedge \alpha _ r\wedge \Xi | \leq C_{n,m,\Xi }(\alpha _1+\cdots +\alpha _ r)^ r\wedge \omega ^{n-r}. \]

For $r=0$ the right-hand side means $C_{n,m,\Xi }\omega ^ n$.

Proof. First take a single form $\alpha $, diagonal in an $\omega $-unitary frame, with ordered eigenvalues $\lambda \in \Gamma _ m$. For $1\leq r<m$, repeated splitting and deleted-coordinate positivity give $S_ r(\lambda )\geq \lambda _1\cdots \lambda _ r$. The sum of the last $n-r$ eigenvalues is positive, so each negative eigenvalue has modulus at most $(n-r)\lambda _{r+1}$. Thus every product of $r$ distinct eigenvalues has modulus at most

\[ (n-r)^ r\lambda _1\cdots \lambda _ r\leq (n-r)^ r S_ r(\lambda ). \]

Indeed, replace its negative factors by this bound and its positive factors by the corresponding largest eigenvalues. In the diagonal frame only complementary diagonal coefficients of $\Xi $ contribute to $\alpha ^ r\wedge \Xi $. This proves $|\alpha ^ r\wedge \Xi |\leq C_{n,m,\Xi }\alpha ^ r\wedge \omega ^{n-r}$.

Apply this bound to the polynomial $(\alpha _1+t_2\alpha _2+\cdots +t_ r\alpha _ r)^ r\wedge \Xi $, with $0\leq t_ i\leq 1$. Its absolute value is bounded by the stated right-hand side, by polarized positivity and monotonicity on the cone. A fixed invertible interpolation matrix on this finite-dimensional polynomial space bounds each coefficient by its supremum on the cube. The coefficient of $t_2\cdots t_ r$ is a fixed positive multiple of $\alpha _1\wedge \cdots \wedge \alpha _ r\wedge \Xi $. This proves the mixed bound. The closed-cone case follows by adding $\varepsilon \omega $ to each form and taking the limit. $\square$
