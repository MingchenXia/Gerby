From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 2.5.1

Proposition 2.5.1. If $\eta $ is another Hermitian form and $\omega \leq C\eta $ for a constant $C>0$, then

\[ \overline{\mathrm v}_{\mathrm{sm}}(\omega )\leq C^ n\overline{\mathrm v}_{\mathrm{sm}}(\eta ). \]

In particular, finiteness of $\overline{\mathrm v}_{\mathrm{sm}}$ is independent of the Hermitian form.

Proof. Let $u\in \mathcal H_\omega $. Since $C\eta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u\geq \omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u>0$, the function $u/C$ belongs to $\mathcal H_\eta $. The top exterior power is monotone on positive $(1,1)$-forms: for positive Hermitian matrices $A\leq B$, all eigenvalues of $A^{-1/2}BA^{-1/2}$ are at least one, so $\det A\leq \det B$. Applying this pointwise gives

\[ \int _ X(\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u)^ n \leq C^ n\int _ X\bigl (\eta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(u/C)\bigr )^ n \leq C^ n\overline{\mathrm v}_{\mathrm{sm}}(\eta ). \]

Taking the supremum over $u$ proves the inequality. Since any two Hermitian forms on the compact manifold $X$ are comparable in both directions, the last assertion follows. $\square$
