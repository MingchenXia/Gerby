Relative fixed measure continuity

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Theorem 31.3.3

Theorem 31.3.3. Suppose $u_ j\in \mathcal E_\varphi $, $u_ j\to u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ in $L^1(X)$, and $\mu _{u_ j}\leq \nu $ for a fixed finite non-pluripolar measure $\nu $. Then

\[ u\in \mathcal E_\varphi , \qquad u_ j\longrightarrow u\text{ in capacity}, \qquad \mu _{u_ j}\rightharpoonup \mu _ u\leq \nu . \]

Proof. Start with an arbitrary subsequence. By lemma 31.3.1, a further subsequence has a common lower envelope $h\in \mathcal E_\varphi $, and $u\in \mathcal E_\varphi $. Inequality (31.2) and Hartogs' lemma give a common upper bound $u_ j\leq \varphi +C$.

After a common translation, assume $u_ j,u\leq 0$. Since $\mu _{u_ j}\leq \nu $, lemma 1.23.1 gives

\[ \int _ X|\mathrm{e}^{u_ j}-\mathrm{e}^ u|\, \mathrm{d}\mu _{u_ j} \leq \int _ X|\mathrm{e}^{u_ j}-\mathrm{e}^ u|\, \mathrm{d}\nu \longrightarrow 0. \]

Thus proposition 31.3.2 gives convergence in capacity along this subsequence. For the measures themselves, the common lower bound gives

\[ \mu _{u_ j}(\{ u_ j\leq -T\} ) \leq \nu (\{ h\leq -T\} )\longrightarrow 0 \quad \hbox{uniformly in }j, \]

since $\nu $ is finite and does not charge the pole set of $h\not\equiv -\infty $. The tail criterion for smooth backgrounds, theorem 7.6.1, therefore gives $\mu _{u_ j}\rightharpoonup \mu _ u$, and weak limits preserve domination by the fixed finite measure $\nu $. Every subsequence of the original sequence has a further subsequence with these conclusions and the same limit $u$, so both convergences hold for the original sequence. $\square$
