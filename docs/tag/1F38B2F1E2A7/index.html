Chapter 10 · Hermitian geometry, torsion, and Gauduchon duality · Tag 1F38B2F1E2A7

Split elliptic cotangent cohomology

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 26.19.37

Lemma 26.19.37. There is a direct sum decomposition

\[ H^1(M,\Omega _ M^1)=\mathbb C\xi \oplus \mathbb C f^*\eta . \]

The restriction of a class $c=\rho \xi +Kf^*\eta $ to any ruling fiber is nonzero if and only if $\rho \ne 0$.

Proof. The relative cotangent sequence is

\[ 0\longrightarrow f^*\Omega _ E^1\longrightarrow \Omega _ M^1 \longrightarrow \Omega _{M/E}^1\longrightarrow 0. \]

The two affine charts of a projective line give the direct images

\[ f_*\mathcal O_ M=\mathcal O_ E,\quad R^1f_*\mathcal O_ M=0, \qquad f_*\Omega _{M/E}^1=0,\quad R^1f_*\Omega _{M/E}^1=\mathcal O_ E. \]

For the last identity, a Laurent cocycle $g(z,t)\, \mathrm{d}t$ can be reduced, by subtracting forms holomorphic on the two charts, to its coefficient of $\mathrm{d}t/t$. Its residue is unchanged by $t_ i=b_{ij}t_ j$, with differentials taken relative to the base, so the local generators glue to the trivial line bundle. For functions, the two halves of a Laurent series exhaust the cocycle, which gives $R^1f_*\mathcal O_ M=0$. The direct images in degree zero follow because $\mathbb P^1$ has no nonconstant holomorphic functions and no nonzero holomorphic one-forms.

The Leray spectral sequences and the fact that the base is a curve now give

\[ H^1(M,f^*\Omega _ E^1)\simeq H^1(E,\Omega _ E^1),\quad H^2(M,f^*\Omega _ E^1)=0,\quad H^1(M,\Omega _{M/E}^1)\simeq \mathbb C, \]

together with $H^0(M,\Omega ^1_{M/E})=0$. The cohomology sequence of the relative cotangent sequence is therefore the exact sequence

\[ 0\longrightarrow \mathbb C f^*\eta \longrightarrow H^1(M,\Omega _ M^1)\longrightarrow \mathbb C\longrightarrow 0. \]

On a fiber, the class $\xi $ is represented on the two affine charts by $\mathrm{d}\log v=-\mathrm{d}t/t$, whose residue is nonzero, so $\xi $ splits the sequence. The pulled-back class $f^*\eta $ restricts to zero on every fiber. This proves both assertions. $\square$
