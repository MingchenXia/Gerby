Hermitian morse program

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Problem 33.3.15

Problem 33.3.15. In the setting of (33.12), find geometric hypotheses that control the integral on the right-hand side, the cost of the subtraction, up to a nef endpoint, beyond the regime of a closed nef core treated in theorem 26.11.1. Determine when positive root volume forces bigness in the absence of such a core. For closed backgrounds, compare the resulting estimate with the classical Morse inequalities; for nonclosed backgrounds, the estimate should keep track of the differential data and of the dependence on $\mu $.
