Lemma 1.21.4A340B19CD0CC

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 1.21.4

Lemma 1.21.4. Let $\alpha $ be a smooth real $(1,1)$-form on $X$, and let $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\alpha )\cap C^0(X)$. There are smooth real functions $u_ j$ and numbers $\varepsilon _ j\downarrow 0$ such that $u_ j\to u$ uniformly and

\[ \alpha +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ j\geq -\varepsilon _ j\omega . \]

Proof. Use the Chern averaging operators and the classical estimate stated before lemma 1.21.3, with $\alpha $ in place of $\theta $. For some $K_0,A>0$ and $\epsilon _0(t)\to 0$, put $\lambda (x,t)=t\partial _ t(R_ tu(x)+K_0t^2)$. The estimate with vanishing radius component gives

\[ \alpha +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}R_ tu\geq -(A\lambda (\cdot ,t)+\epsilon _0(t))\omega . \]

The function $\lambda $ is nonnegative, continuous for $t>0$, and increasing in $t$. For fixed $x$, the convex function $s\mapsto R_{\mathrm{e}^ s}u(x)+K_0\mathrm{e}^{2s}$ has the finite limit $u(x)$ as $s\to -\infty $. Its nonnegative derivative must therefore tend to zero, since a positive limit of the derivative would make the function tend to $-\infty $. Thus $\lambda (x,t)\to 0$ as $t\to 0$, and for any sequence $t_ j\downarrow 0$, Dini's theorem gives $\| \lambda (\cdot ,t_ j)\| _\infty \to 0$.

The averaging kernels have mass one and only sample points at distance at most $Ct$ from the base point. Uniform continuity of $u$ therefore gives $\| R_ tu-u\| _\infty \to 0$. Set $u_ j=R_{t_ j}u$, and let $\varepsilon _ j\to 0$ be a decreasing positive majorant of $A\| \lambda (\cdot ,t_ j)\| _\infty +\epsilon _0(t_ j)$. $\square$
