Proposition 11.2.6

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 11.2.6

Proposition 11.2.6. Suppose that $\mathbb {D}$ is a nef b-divisor over $X$. Then

\[ \left( \mathbb {D},\ldots ,\mathbb {D} \right)=\operatorname{vol}\mathbb {D}. \]

Proof. Using 11.2.5 and 11.1.4, we may easily reduce to the case where $\mathbb {D}$ is nef and big. In this case, take a non-divisorial closed positive $(1,1)$-current $T$ in $\mathbb {D}_ X$ such that $\mathbb {D}(T)=\mathbb {D}$. Then we need to show that

\[ \operatorname{vol}\mathbb {D}=\operatorname{vol}T, \]

which is proved in theorem 11.1.10. $\square$
