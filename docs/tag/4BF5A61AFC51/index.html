Proposition 1.6.124BF5A61AFC51

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 1.6.12

Proposition 1.6.12. For each $2\leq p<\infty $,

\[ v\in W^{1,p}_{\mathrm{loc}}(U) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \int _{-\infty }^ A|\chi '(s)|^ p \mathrm e^{(2-p)s/m_0}\, \mathrm ds<\infty . \]

In this case, the weak gradient of $v$ is the zero extension of $\chi '(\log |f|)\nabla \log |f|$ from $U\setminus Z$.

Proof. At a smooth point of a component of multiplicity $m_0$, outside the other components, multiplying the defining function of the component by a holomorphic $m_0$-th root of the remaining unit gives a normal coordinate $w_1$ with $f=w_1^{m_0}$. Coordinate changes with bounded derivatives and Jacobians preserve local first-order Sobolev integrability. With $r=|w_1|$ and $s=m_0\log r$, polar integration of the classical gradient gives, up to fixed positive factors,

\[ \int _0^{r_0}|\chi '(m_0\log r)|^ p r^{1-p}\, \mathrm dr =\frac1{m_0}\int _{-\infty }^{m_0\log r_0} |\chi '(s)|^ p\mathrm e^{(2-p)s/m_0}\, \mathrm ds. \]

Such smooth points occur in every neighborhood of $0$. This proves necessity, since changing the finite upper endpoint does not affect convergence.

For sufficiency, put

\[ s_\varepsilon =\tfrac 12\log (|f|^2+\varepsilon ^2), \qquad v_\varepsilon =\chi (s_\varepsilon ). \]

For small $\varepsilon >0$, these are $C^2$ plurisubharmonic functions decreasing to $v$. By convexity, $|\chi (s)|\leq C(1+|s|)$ for $s\leq A$, so the local exponential integrability of $\log |f|$, recalled in [SGPT], gives convergence in every local $L^ p$ space with $p<\infty $. By the chain rule,

\[ |\nabla v_\varepsilon | =\chi '(s_\varepsilon ) \frac{|f|}{|f|^2+\varepsilon ^2}|\partial f|. \]

Apply lemma 1.6.11 with the coarea formula, keeping two powers of $|\partial f|$ for the level estimate and bounding the other $p-2$ powers by the first inequality of the lemma. The local integral of $|\nabla v_\varepsilon |^ p$ over $K\cap \{ 0<|f|<r_ K\} $ is then bounded by

\[ C\int _0^{r_ K} |\chi '(\tfrac 12\log (r^2+\varepsilon ^2))|^ p \frac{r^{p+(p-2)(1-1/m_0)+1}}{(r^2+\varepsilon ^2)^ p} \, \mathrm dr. \]

With $s=\tfrac 12\log (r^2+\varepsilon ^2)$, we have $\mathrm ds=r\, \mathrm dr/(r^2+\varepsilon ^2)$ and $r\leq (r^2+\varepsilon ^2)^{1/2}$, so this is at most

\[ C\int _{-\infty }^ A|\chi '(s)|^ p \mathrm e^{(2-p)s/m_0}\, \mathrm ds. \]

The integral over the part where $|f|\geq r_ K$ is uniformly bounded as well. Weak compactness in Sobolev spaces and the local $L^ p$ convergence therefore show that $v\in W^{1,p}_{\mathrm{loc}}$. Its weak gradient agrees with the classical one off $Z$, and $Z$ has Lebesgue measure zero, which proves the final assertion. $\square$
