Chapter 12 · Monge–Ampère equations and stability · Tag C3049F567279

Strict gap matrix bound

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 10.8.1

Lemma 10.8.1. There are a positive continuous function $H$ on $X$ and constants $r,R,\delta >0$ with the following properties. Every right-hand side in the family satisfies $h\leq H$; on $X$,

\[ \mu -2r\mathbf1\in \Gamma ,\qquad f_\infty (\mu -2r\mathbf1)\geq H+3\delta , \qquad \mu =\lambda (A_{\underline u}); \]

and at every point of $X$, every positive semidefinite Hermitian endomorphism $P$ satisfies

\[ F(A_{\underline u}-r\operatorname {Id}+P)\leq H+2\delta \quad \Longrightarrow \quad P\leq R\operatorname {Id}. \]

Proof. Each $f_{\infty ,i}$ is an increasing limit of positive concave functions, hence concave with values in $(0,+\infty ]$. Suppose it is infinite at one point and finite at another point of $\Gamma $. Extend the segment joining them slightly beyond the second point, inside the open cone. The second point is then an interior point of the longer segment, so concavity forces the value there to be infinite, a contradiction. Thus $f_{\infty ,i}$ is either finite everywhere, and then continuous, or infinite everywhere. By symmetry, the same alternative holds for every $i$.

Take $\delta =\frac18\min \{ \delta _0,\min _ Xf(\mu )\} >0$. In the finite case set $H=\min \{ H_0,f_\infty (\mu )-4\delta \} $; otherwise set $H=H_0$. Since $f_\infty (\mu )\geq f(\mu )\geq 8\delta $, this function is positive. The conditions on the family give $h\leq H$ and $f_\infty (\mu )\geq H+4\delta $. Since $\mu (X)$ is a compact subset of $\Gamma $ and the functions $f_{\infty ,i}$ are continuous (or identically infinite), a sufficiently small $r>0$ gives $\mu -2r\mathbf1\in \Gamma $ and $f_\infty (\mu -2r\mathbf1)\geq H+3\delta $ on $X$.

If the matrix bound failed, there would be points $x_ j\to x_*$ and $P_ j\geq 0$ with unbounded norms and $F(A_{\underline u}(x_ j)-r\operatorname {Id}+P_ j)\leq H(x_ j)+2\delta $. By monotonicity of eigenvalues, the ordered eigenvalues $\lambda ^{(j)}$ of $A_{\underline u}(x_ j)-r\operatorname {Id}+P_ j$ satisfy $\lambda _ i^{(j)}\geq \mu _ i(x_ j)-r$. Their sum tends to infinity, so some component $k$ tends to infinity along a subsequence. For every fixed $t>0$, eventually

\[ \lambda ^{(j)}\geq \mu (x_*)-2r\mathbf1+te_ k. \]

Since $f$ is increasing in each variable, letting $t\to \infty $ shows that the lower limit of $F(A_{\underline u}(x_ j)-r\operatorname {Id}+P_ j)$ is at least $f_\infty (\mu (x_*)-2r\mathbf1)\geq H(x_*)+3\delta $, which contradicts the upper bound $H(x_ j)+2\delta $. Adding positive semidefinite matrices preserves the cone, so all these comparisons take place in $\Gamma $. $\square$
