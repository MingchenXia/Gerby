Individual root completeness

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 14.31.4

Proposition 14.31.4. The norm $\rho _ h$ is complete on $\mathcal D_ h$ if and only if $v_+(h)<+\infty $. When the upper mass is finite,

\[ \| p\| _{L^ n}\leq v_+(h)^{1/n}\rho _ h(p)\qquad (p\in \mathcal D_ h), \]

so $\rho _ h$ and $\| \cdot \| _{\mathcal D_ h}$ are equivalent norms.

Proof. Suppose $v_+(h)<+\infty $. A continuous solution for a bounded density $q$ has mass $c_ h(q)\int _ Xq\, \mathrm{d}V\leq v_+(h)$. Applying this to the truncations of $|p|^ n$ and letting the truncation level increase gives $b_ h(|p|^ n)\| p\| _{L^ n}^ n\leq v_+(h)$, which is the asserted inequality. Hence $\rho _ h$ and $\| \cdot \| _{\mathcal D_ h}$ are equivalent, and completeness follows from proposition 14.31.3.

Suppose instead that $v_+(h)=+\infty $. By theorem 26.14.3, theorem 26.1.3, the normalization scalars of smooth strictly positive probability densities are unbounded above. For each $j$, choose such a density $f_ j$ with

\[ c_ h(f_ j)\geq (j2^ j)^ n, \qquad \text{and put}\qquad p_ j=jf_ j^{1/n}. \]

Then $\| p_ j\| _{L^ n}=j$ and $\rho _ h(p_ j)=jc_ h(f_ j)^{-1/n}\leq 2^{-j}$, so the partial sums $P_ N=\sum _{j\leq N}p_ j$ form a Cauchy sequence for $\rho _ h$.

Fix a Gauduchon form $\gamma $. Its coefficient $a_\gamma $ is bounded below by a positive constant, so (14.103) gives $\rho _ h(p)\geq c\| p\| _{L^1}$ with a constant $c>0$. Hence $\sum _ jp_ j$ converges in $L^1$ to a nonnegative function $P$. If $P_ N$ converged for $\rho _ h$ to an element of $\mathcal D_ h$, that element would also be the $L^1$ limit, hence equal to $P$ almost everywhere. But $P\geq p_ j$ gives $\| P\| _{L^ n}\geq j$ for every $j$, so $P\notin L^ n$. Therefore $\rho _ h$ is not complete. $\square$
