10.1 Continuous solvability49F0FF1AED46

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · 10.1 Continuous solvability

There are two natural ways to normalize the prescribed density: by an unknown positive constant, or by an exponential of the potential.

Theorem 10.1.1 (Kołodziej–Nguyen). There are $c>0$ and $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\omega )\cap C(X)$ such that

\[ (\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u)^ n=c f\, \mathrm{d}V. \]

The potential can be normalized by $\sup _ Xu=0$.

The constant $c$ is uniquely determined by $f$, by proposition 10.6.1. The normalized potential, however, need not be unique, even for a smooth density; see theorem 11.6.1.

Theorem 10.1.2 (Nguyen). For every $\lambda >0$, there is a unique continuous $\omega $-plurisubharmonic function $u_\lambda $ satisfying

10.1
\begin{equation} (\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_\lambda )^ n=\mathrm{e}^{\lambda u_\lambda }f\, \mathrm{d}V. \end{equation}
