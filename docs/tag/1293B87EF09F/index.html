Chapter 18 · Toric and convex geometry · Tag 1293B87EF09F

Theorem 12.2.12

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Theorem 12.2.12

Theorem 12.2.12. We have

12.36
\begin{equation} \label{eq:FVthetaE} F_{V_{\theta }}\in \mathcal{E}^{\infty }(N_{\mathbb {R}},P_ D).\footnotemark \end{equation}
Note. Initially I was only able to show $F_{V_{\theta }}\in \mathcal{E}(N_{\mathbb {R}},P_ D)$, the strengthened version was suggested by Robert Berman.
In particular,
12.37
\begin{equation} \label{eq:toricminsingmass} \int _ X \theta _{V_{\theta }}^ n=n!\operatorname{vol}P_ D. \end{equation}

Proof. Taking $\varphi =V_{\theta }$ in theorem 12.2.9, we find

\[ \Phi _{\mathbb {R}}\left( \Delta (\theta ,V_{\theta })\right)=\Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(\theta ,V_{\theta })=\Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(L)=\Phi _{\mathbb {R}}(P_ D), \]

where we applied proposition 12.2.5 in the last equality. Therefore, (12.39) follows. In particular, (12.37) follows from proposition 12.2.4.

Next we prove (12.38). Take a smooth function $\chi \colon N_{\mathbb {R}}\rightarrow [0,1]$ so that

  1. $\operatorname{Supp}\chi $ is compact;

  2. $\chi \equiv 1$ on a neighbourhood of $0$.

Fix a $T_ c$-invariant volume form $\mu $ on $T(\mathbb {C})$. Then $(\operatorname{Trop}^*\chi )\mu $ can be regarded as a $T_ c$-invariant smooth non-negative measure on $X$. After choosing a suitable normalizing constant $C>0$, there is a unique solution

12.40
\begin{equation} \label{eq:thetavarphinD_temp1} \theta _{\varphi }^ n=C\left(\operatorname{Trop}^*\chi \right)\mu ,\quad \sup _ X \varphi =0,\quad \varphi \in \mathcal{E}^{\infty }(X,\theta ). \end{equation}

This is proved in [Theorem B, BEGZ10]. The uniqueness of $\varphi $ further guarantees its $T_ c$-invariance. Since $\varphi $ has minimal singularities, $F_{\varphi }\sim F_{V_{\theta }}$. Next, using proposition 12.2.4, the Monge–Ampère equation (12.40) implies

\[ \operatorname{MA}_{\mathbb {R}}\left(F_{\varphi }\right)=C \chi \operatorname{Trop}_*\mu . \]

It follows from the regularity theorem [Theorem 2.19, BB13] and (12.39) that $F_{\varphi }\sim \operatorname{Supp}_{P_ D}$. Hence, (12.38) follows. $\square$
