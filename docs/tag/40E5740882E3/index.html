Hirzebruch fixed stein hypersurface

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 4.6.35

Corollary 4.6.35. For each $e\geq 0$, the bundle $E$ of corollary 4.6.31 can be chosen so that $E^\times $ contains a connected smooth Stein hypersurface on which the restriction of every smooth strongly pseudoconvex squared Finsler metric on $E$ has a nondegenerate critical point with at least two positive Levi eigenvalues and at most one negative Levi eigenvalue.

Proof. The bundle constructed in the proof of theorem 4.6.30 is a fiber of an algebraic family on a chart of the prioritary stack, so it is algebraic. Apply theorem 4.6.34 with $n=r=2$, using the curvature obstruction of corollary 4.6.31. $\square$
