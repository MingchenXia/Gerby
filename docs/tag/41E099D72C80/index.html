10.3.1 The spaces of sections41E099D72C80

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · 10.3.1 The spaces of sections

Definition 10.3.1. We will write

\[ \begin{aligned} \Gamma (\theta ,\varphi )\coloneqq & \left\{ (\nu (s),k) : k\in \mathbb {N}, s\in \mathrm{H}^0(X,L^ k\otimes \mathcal{I}(k\varphi ))^{\times } \right\} ,\\ \Delta _ k(\theta ,\varphi )\coloneqq & \mathrm{Conv}\left\{ k^{-1}\nu (s): s\in \mathrm{H}^0\left(X,L^ k\otimes \mathcal{I}(k\varphi )\right)^{\times } \right\} \subseteq \mathbb {R}^ n, \end{aligned} \]

where the second set is defined only for those $k$ such that $\mathrm{H}^0(X,L^ k\otimes \mathcal{I}(k\varphi ))\neq 0$.

When $\varphi =V_{\theta }$, we simply write $\Gamma (L)$ and $\Delta _ k(L)$ instead.

Here and in the sequel, the cross notation means excluding $0$. Here $\mathrm{Conv}$ denotes the convex hull. For large enough $k$, $\Delta _ k(\theta ,\varphi )$ is non-empty thanks to theorem 7.4.2.

Definition 10.3.2. Assume that $\varphi $ has analytic singularities. We define

10.35
\begin{equation} \Gamma ^{\infty }(\theta ,\varphi )\coloneqq \left\{ (\nu (s),k):k\in \mathbb {N}, s\in \mathrm{H}^0(X,L^ k\otimes \mathcal{I}_{\infty }(k\varphi ))^{\times } \right\} . \end{equation}
Recall that $\mathcal{I}_{\infty }$ is introduced in definition 1.6.12.

For later use, we introduce a twisted version as well.

Definition 10.3.3. If $T$ is a holomorphic line bundle on $X$, we introduce

\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta _{k,T}(\theta ,\varphi )\coloneqq & \mathrm{Conv}\left\{ k^{-1}\nu (s): s\in \mathrm{H}^0(X,T\otimes L^ k\otimes \mathcal{I}(k\varphi ))^{\times } \right\} \subseteq \mathbb {R}^ n,\\ \Delta _{k,T}(L)\coloneqq & \mathrm{Conv}\left\{ k^{-1}\nu (s): s\in \mathrm{H}^0(X,T\otimes L^ k)^{\times } \right\} \subseteq \mathbb {R}^ n \end{aligned} \]

for all $k\in \mathbb {Z}_{>0}$ such that the relevant spaces of sections are non-zero.
