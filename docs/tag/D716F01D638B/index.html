Lemma 14.2.1D716F01D638B

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 14.2.1

Lemma 14.2.1. Fix $p>1$ and $L>0$. There is $b>0$ such that, for every $f\geq 0$ with $\| f\| _{L^ p(\mathrm{d}V)}\leq L$, one can find $s\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ satisfying

\[ -1\leq s\leq 0,\qquad \mathrm{MA}_\theta (s)\geq b f\, \mathrm{d}V. \]

The constant $b$ depends only on the fixed geometry and on $\rho ,a,p,L$.

Proof. Choose $1<q<p$. Since a fixed quasi-plurisubharmonic function belongs to every finite $L^ r$ space, Hölder's inequality gives a uniform $L^ q$ bound for

\[ g=(-\rho )^{2n}f. \]

By theorem 10.1.1 there are $c>0$ and a continuous $\omega $-plurisubharmonic $v$, normalized by $\sup _ Xv=-1$, such that

\[ \omega _ v^ n=c(1+g)\, \mathrm{d}V. \]

The construction in that theorem gives a uniform upper bound for $c$, because in its Gauduchon trace estimate the root integral of $1+g$ is bounded below by the root integral of $1$. The lower-density estimate of theorem 10.3.3 gives $c\| 1+g\| _ q\geq c_ q>0$, so $c$ is also bounded below. The potential estimate of the same theorem then gives $-C\leq v\leq -1$ with a uniform $C$.

Put $h=\rho +av\leq -1$. Then $\theta _ h\geq a\omega _ v$. On $(-\infty ,-1]$ define

\[ \chi (t)=-t^{-1}-1,\qquad \chi '(t)=t^{-2}\in (0,1],\qquad \chi ''(t)=-2t^{-3}>0, \]

and set $s=\chi (h)$, with value $-1$ at the poles of $h$. To justify both the positivity of $\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}s$ and the computation of its Monge–Ampère measure, we first work with the bounded functions $h_ k=\max (h,-k)$, $k\geq 1$. The chain rule for bounded potentials and $\theta \geq 0$ give

\[ \theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\chi (h_ k) =(1-\chi '(h_ k))\theta +\chi '(h_ k)\theta _{h_ k} +\chi ''(h_ k)\, \mathrm{d}h_ k\wedge \mathrm d^{\mathrm c}h_ k\geq 0. \]

These bounded potentials decrease to $s$, so $s\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ and $-1\leq s\leq 0$. On $\{ h>-k\} $, locality and positivity of mixed products give

\[ \mathrm{MA}_\theta (s) \geq a^ n|h|^{-2n}\omega _ v^ n \geq a^ nc|h|^{-2n}(-\rho )^{2n}f\, \mathrm{d}V. \]

Letting $k\to \infty $, we obtain this inequality on the set where $h$ is finite; its complement is pluripolar and has zero volume. Since $|h|\leq (-\rho )+aC\leq (1+aC)(-\rho )$, the assertion follows with any common lower bound for $a^ nc(1+aC)^{-2n}$. $\square$
