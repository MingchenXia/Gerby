Kahler model capacity powers

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Proposition 16.9.6

Proposition 16.9.6. Let $\kappa $ be a Kähler form and $\phi $ a model potential for $\kappa $ of mass $m>0$. There is $A\geq 1$ such that every Borel set $E\subset X$ satisfies

16.85
\begin{equation} \label{eq:kahler-model-capacity-powers} A^{-1}\operatorname {Cap}_\kappa (E)^ n \leq \operatorname {Cap}_{\kappa ,\phi }(E) \leq A\operatorname {Cap}_\kappa (E)^{1/n}. \end{equation}

More generally, if $\theta \leq \kappa $, $\{ \theta \} $ is big and $\phi $ is a model potential for $\theta $ of mass $m>0$, then

16.86
\begin{equation} \label{eq:big-model-capacity-lower-power} \operatorname {Cap}_\kappa (E)^ n \leq \frac{C^ n}{m}\operatorname {Cap}_{\theta ,\phi }(E), \end{equation}

where $C$ depends only on $X$ and $\kappa $.

Proof. All capacities involved are inner regular (proposition 16.7.6), so it suffices to treat compact sets $K$: the inequalities for a Borel set $E$ follow by applying them to the compact subsets of $E$ and taking suprema. If $K$ is pluripolar, all capacities of $K$ vanish. Otherwise fix a smooth function $g\geq 0$ with zero set $K$, and apply lemma 16.9.3 with this $g$ to $(\theta ,\phi )$ and to $(\kappa ,0)$; here $0=V_\kappa $ is a model potential for $\kappa $ of mass $\Omega $, by proposition 16.3.2(4). Denote the resulting envelopes by $S_\phi $ and $S_0$ and their suprema by $L_\phi $ and $L_0$. Every competitor for $P_\theta (\phi +jg)$ is $\kappa $-plurisubharmonic, because $\theta \leq \kappa $, and it is at most $\phi +jg\leq jg$ quasi-everywhere, so $P_\theta (\phi +jg)\leq P_\kappa (jg)$ for every $j$; hence $S_\phi \leq S_0$ and $L_\phi \leq L_0$. The two estimates of lemma 16.9.3 give

\[ \operatorname {Cap}_\kappa (K)^ n \leq \frac{C^ n}{\max \{ 1,L_0\} ^ n} \leq \frac{C^ n}{\max \{ 1,L_\phi \} ^ n} \leq \frac{C^ n}{m}\operatorname {Cap}_{\theta ,\phi }(K). \]

This proves (16.86) and, for $\theta =\kappa $, the first inequality in (16.85).

For the upper bound let $\theta =\kappa $, and choose $b>1$ with $b^ n(\Omega -m)<\Omega $. By theorem 16.4.4, applied to $\phi \preceq 0$, where the mass of $0$ is $\int _ X\kappa ^ n=\Omega $, the envelope $\eta =E_ b(\phi ,0)=P_\kappa (b\phi )$ is a potential, and it satisfies $b^{-1}\eta \leq \phi $; in particular $\eta =-\infty $ at the poles of $\phi $. Write $c=1-b^{-1}>0$. For fixed $j$ and $\delta >0$ consider

\[ v_ j=c\, P_\kappa (jg)+b^{-1}\eta -\delta , \]

a convex combination of $\kappa $-plurisubharmonic functions minus a constant. The function $P_\kappa (jg)$ is upper semicontinuous and vanishes on $K$, because $0\leq P_\kappa (jg)\leq jg$. Hence $P_\kappa (jg)<\delta /c$ on a neighborhood $U$ of $K$, and there $v_ j<b^{-1}\eta \leq \phi $. Outside the poles of $\phi $, the difference $v_ j-\phi $ is bounded above by $cj\sup _ Xg$, and $g$ has a positive minimum on $X\setminus U$. Therefore $v_ j\leq \phi +lg$ for a sufficiently large integer $l$, so $v_ j\leq P_\kappa (\phi +lg)\leq S_\phi $. Letting $\delta \downarrow 0$ and then $j\to \infty $, and using that the increasing limit of $P_\kappa (jg)$ equals $S_0$ almost everywhere, we obtain

\[ cS_0+b^{-1}\eta \leq S_\phi \]

almost everywhere, hence everywhere.

Choose a Borel set $G$ of positive volume on which $b^{-1}\eta \geq -D$ for a constant $D$; such a set exists because $\eta $ is finite almost everywhere. By proposition 14.2.1, there is a constant $B$, independent of $K$, with $\int _ X|S_0-L_0|\, \kappa ^ n\leq B$. Hence $S_0\geq L_0-B/\int _ G\kappa ^ n$ at some point $x\in G$, for otherwise $\int _ G(L_0-S_0)\, \kappa ^ n>B$. At this point the last display gives $L_\phi \geq S_\phi (x)\geq cL_0-D'$ with $D'=cB/\int _ G\kappa ^ n+D$, which does not depend on $K$. If $L_0\geq \max \{ 1,2D'/c\} $, then $L_\phi \geq cL_0/2$, and lemma 16.9.3 gives

\[ \operatorname {Cap}_{\kappa ,\phi }(K)\leq \frac{2C}{cL_0} \leq \frac{2C}{c\, \Omega ^{1/n}}\operatorname {Cap}_\kappa (K)^{1/n}; \]

the last inequality is the lower bound (16.81) for the construction with reference potential $0$, which gives $\Omega /L_0^ n\leq \operatorname {Cap}_\kappa (K)$. In the remaining range $L_0<\max \{ 1,2D'/c\} $, the same lower bound shows that $\operatorname {Cap}_\kappa (K)$ is bounded below by a positive constant independent of $K$, and we use $\operatorname {Cap}_{\kappa ,\phi }(K)\leq m$, enlarging $A$ if necessary. The constant $A$ in the upper bound depends on $\phi $. $\square$
