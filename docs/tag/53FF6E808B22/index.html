Remark 10.2.14

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Remark 10.2.14

Remark 10.2.14. Suppose that $X$ is a normal projective variety. Consider a rank $n$ (surjective) valuation $\nu \colon \mathbb {C}(X)^{\times }\rightarrow \mathbb {Z}^ n$ and a closed positive $(1,1)$-current $T$ on $X$. Then we can always define $\nu (T)\in \mathbb {R}^ n$ as follows: Take a resolution $\pi \colon Y\rightarrow X$ such that there is a smooth flag $Y_{\bullet }$ on $Y$ and $g\in \operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb {Z}^ n_{\mathrm{lex}})$ such that

\[ \nu =\nu _{Y_{\bullet }}g. \]

Then we define

\[ \nu (T)\coloneqq \nu _{Y_{\bullet }}(\pi ^*T)g. \]

This definition does not depend on the choice of $\pi $, by passing to a common resolution and applying proposition 10.2.9, proposition 10.2.10.
