Chapter 17 · B-divisors and restriction · Tag AB54064E3778

Augmented nef b-divisor

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Definition 11.1.17

Definition 11.1.17. An augmented nef b-divisor over $X$ is a pair $(\mathbb {D},D)$, where

  1. $\mathbb {D}$ is a nef b-divisor over $X$;

  2. $D$ is a formal sum

    \[ D=\sum _{E} c_ E E,\quad c_ E\in \mathbb {R}_{\geq 0}, \]

    where $E$ runs over the set of prime divisors on $X$,

such that the following condition is satisfied:

\[ \sum _{E}c_ E \{ E\} \]

is convergent as a sum in $\mathrm{H}^{1,1}(X,\mathbb {R})$.

The cohomology class of $(\mathbb {D},D)$ is defined as

\[ \mathbb {D}_ X+\sum _{E}c_ E \{ E\} \in \mathrm{H}^{1,1}(X,\mathbb {R}). \]

The volume of $(\mathbb {D},D)$ is defined as

\[ \operatorname{vol}(\mathbb {D},D)=\operatorname{vol}\mathbb {D}. \]
