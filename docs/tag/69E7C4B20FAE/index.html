Corollary 6.1.11

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Corollary 6.1.11

Corollary 6.1.11. Let $\theta $ be a closed smooth real $(1,1)$-form on $X$ representing a pseudo-effective cohomology class. Assume that $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$. Then

\[ P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}=\sup \left\{ \psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ): \psi \leq 0, \psi \preceq _{\mathcal{I}} \varphi \right\} . \]

Proof. It suffices to show that for any $\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ with $\psi \leq 0$ and $\psi \preceq _{\mathcal{I}}\varphi $, we have $\psi \leq P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}$. By 6.1.8 and proposition 3.2.8, we know that $P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}\lor \psi \sim _{\mathcal{I}}\varphi $. Therefore, $P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}\lor \psi $ is a candidate in the definition of $P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}$ (cf. (3.182)), so

\[ \psi \leq P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}\lor \psi \leq P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}. \]

We conclude the proof. $\square$
