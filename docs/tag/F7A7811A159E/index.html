Corollary 7.3.11

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Corollary 7.3.11

Corollary 7.3.11. Let $\pi \colon X\rightarrow Z$ be a proper bimeromorphic morphism from $X$ to a Kähler manifold $Z$. Then

7.45
\begin{equation} \label{eq:volpushinc} \operatorname{vol}(T_1,\ldots ,T_ n)=\operatorname{vol}(\pi _*T_1,\ldots ,\pi _*T_ n). \end{equation}

Proof. Observe that we may assume that $T_ i=\operatorname{Reg}T_ i$ for all $i=1,\ldots ,n$. In fact, since the pushforward of the divisorial part of $T_ i$ is divisorial as well, hence by proposition 7.3.4 5, they do not contribute to the volumes.

Now by proposition 7.3.9, it remains to show that

\[ \operatorname{vol}(T_1,\ldots ,T_ n)=\operatorname{vol}(\pi ^*\pi _*T_1,\ldots ,\pi ^*\pi _*T_ n). \]

By 7.3.10, the difference $\pi ^*\pi _*T_ i-T_ i$ is divisorial, hence our desired equality follows from proposition 7.3.4 5. $\square$
