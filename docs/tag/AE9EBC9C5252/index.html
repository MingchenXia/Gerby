From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 10.3.10

Proposition 10.3.10. Let $L'$ be another big line bundle on $X$. Then

\[ \Delta _{\nu }(L)+\Delta _{\nu }(L')\subseteq \Delta _{\nu }(L\otimes L'). \]

Proof. Observe that for each $k\in \mathbb {N}$, we have

\[ \Delta _ k(L)+\Delta _ k(L')\subseteq \Delta _ k(L\otimes L'). \]

So our assertion follows immediately from theorem 10.1.3. $\square$
