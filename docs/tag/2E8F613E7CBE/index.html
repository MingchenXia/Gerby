Proposition 6.1.12

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 6.1.12

Proposition 6.1.12. Let $\theta $ be a closed real smooth $(1,1)$-form on $X$ and $\varphi ,\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$. Then the following are equivalent:

  1. $\varphi \preceq _{\mathcal{I}}\psi $;

  2. $P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}\leq P_{\theta }[\psi ]_{\mathcal{I}}$.

Proof. 1 $\implies $ 2. This follows immediately from corollary 6.1.11.

2 $\implies $ 1. It follows from proposition 3.2.8 that

\[ \varphi \sim _{\mathcal{I}} P_{\theta }[\varphi ]_{\mathcal{I}}\leq P_{\theta }[\psi ]_{\mathcal{I}} \sim _{\mathcal{I}} \psi . \]

We conclude 1. $\square$
