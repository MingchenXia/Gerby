15.13 Selection for logarithmic models on productsA167FA614566

For a logarithmic reference with a positive residual background, removing the divisor turns the singular exponential equation into a bounded equation. On a product with a curve, the selected residual can then be determined by the Green operator. This also controls simultaneous changes of the reference, background and density, for which the centering constant must follow the changing data.

Let $(Y,\kappa )$ be a connected compact Hermitian manifold of dimension $k\geq 1$, with $\int _ Y\kappa ^ k=1$, and put $X=\mathbb P^1\times Y$, $N=k+1$. Write $\omega _1$ for the mass-one Fubini–Study form on $\mathbb P^1$ and $\mathrm{d}W=\omega _1\wedge \kappa ^ k$; pullbacks to $X$ are implicit. Fix $a,d>0$, a smooth positive nonconstant function $b$ on $\mathbb P^1$, and a continuous positive function $H$ on $\mathbb P^1$. Set

\[ \begin{aligned} \theta & =(a+d)\omega _1+b\kappa ,& \eta & =a\omega _1+b\kappa ,\\ f& =N b^ kH,& m& =\int _{\mathbb P^1}H\omega _1,\qquad c_0=\frac am. \end{aligned} \]

Both forms are positive and nonclosed: the component $\mathrm{d}b\wedge \kappa $ of horizontal degree one cannot cancel the vertical component $b\, \mathrm{d}\kappa $. For homogeneous coordinates $[Z_0:Z_1]$, put

\[ \ell =\frac{|Z_0|^2}{|Z_0|^2+|Z_1|^2},\qquad D=\{ Z_0=0\} \times Y,\qquad \psi =d\log \ell . \]

The Poincaré–Lelong formula gives $\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\psi =\eta +d[D]$.
