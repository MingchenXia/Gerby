Chapter 17 · B-divisors and restriction · Tag D7373D3639FA

Closed current modification continuity

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Proposition 23.7.2

Proposition 23.7.2. Let $g:Z\to Y$ be a smooth compact modification, and fix a Gauduchon metric $\gamma '$ on $Z$. Pullback of positive closed $(1,1)$-currents, defined through their local potentials, is weakly continuous. Moreover, there is a constant $C_ g$, depending only on the modification and the fixed metrics, such that

23.4
\begin{equation} \label{eq:modification-linear-mass-bound} \int _ Zg^*T\wedge (\gamma ')^{n-1} \leq C_ g\int _ YT\wedge \gamma ^{n-1} \qquad (T\geq 0,\ \mathrm{d}T=0). \end{equation}

Proof. Write $T=B_\gamma (T)+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v_\gamma (T)$. The pullback is

\[ g^*T=g^*B_\gamma (T)+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(v_\gamma (T)\circ g). \]

This current is positive and closed, since it is locally $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}$ of plurisubharmonic functions, and the composed potential is not identically $-\infty $ because $g$ is surjective. The formula agrees with the definition through local potentials, so it does not depend on the decomposition.

If $T_ j\to T$, the smooth parts converge in $C^\infty $, and the potentials converge in $L^1(Y)$ with a common lower curvature bound. By lemma 1.22.13, their compositions with $g$ converge in $L^1(Z)$, and taking $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}$ proves the weak convergence of the pullbacks.

Integration by parts on $Z$ gives

\[ \int _ Zg^*T\wedge (\gamma ')^{n-1} =\int _ Zg^*B_\gamma (T)\wedge (\gamma ')^{n-1}. \]

The absolute value of the right-hand side is at most a fixed constant times $\| B_\gamma (T)\| _{C^0}$. Since the left-hand side is nonnegative, (23.3) gives (23.4). $\square$
