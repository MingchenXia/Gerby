Invariant singular coefficient zero mass

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 26.5.8

Corollary 26.5.8. If $A\geq 0$ is singular, then every bounded $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta _ A)$ satisfies

\[ \int _ X(\theta _ A+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u)^3=0. \]

For every $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta _ A)$, bounded or not, the non-pluripolar top power of $\theta _ A+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u$ is also zero. Thus the nonzero semipositive boundary forms $\theta _ A$ of proposition 26.5.3 have zero non-pluripolar top mass.

Proof. For $\varepsilon >0$, the same bounded $u$ is $\theta _{A+\varepsilon I}$-plurisubharmonic, so the preceding proposition bounds its mass by $H\det (A+\varepsilon I)$, which tends to zero with $\varepsilon $. By the multilinear construction of bounded products in proposition 1.7.1,

\[ (\theta _{A+\varepsilon I}+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u)^3 =\bigl (\theta _ A+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u+\varepsilon h_0\bigr )^3 \]

is a polynomial in $\varepsilon $ whose coefficients are mixed products of finite mass. Its total mass therefore converges to $\int _ X(\theta _ A+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u)^3$ as $\varepsilon \downarrow 0$, so this mass is zero, and positivity makes the measure zero.

For unbounded $u$, we subtract a constant so that $u\leq 0$. Since $\theta _ A\geq 0$, the truncations $u_ k=\max (u,-k)$ are bounded and $\theta _ A$-plurisubharmonic, so their top products vanish by the bounded case. Restricting them to $\{ u>-k\} $ and passing to the limit that defines the non-pluripolar product gives zero as well. $\square$
