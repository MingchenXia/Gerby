Corollary 10.4.1468273CCE4042

Corollary 10.4.14. Assume that $L$ is a big line bundle on $X$ and $h$ is a plurisubharmonic metric on $L$ with positive volume. Then

10.93
\begin{equation} \Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}h)=\Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(L,h). \end{equation}

Proof. We may assume that $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}h$ has positive mass and is $\mathcal{I}$-good. By the $d_ S$-continuity of both sides of (10.93) as proved in theorem 10.4.13 and theorem 10.3.18, together with theorem 7.1.1, we may assume that $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}h$ has analytic singularities.

In this case, using the birational invariance of both sides of (10.93) as proved in proposition 10.3.19 and theorem 10.4.13, we may assume that $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}h$ has log singularities. Finally, after all these reductions, the equality (10.93) holds by construction. $\square$
