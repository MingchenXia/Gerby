Theorem A.4.5CD69C52538E4

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Theorem A.4.5

Theorem A.4.5. For $m\in \sigma ^\vee \cap M$, the following are equivalent:

\[ \chi ^ m\in \mathcal I_ D^{\mathrm{ad}}(u)_ o, \qquad \chi ^ m\in \Gamma (X_\sigma ,\mathcal I_ D^{\mathrm{ad}}(u)), \qquad m+w_ D\in \operatorname {int}P(u). \]

Equivalently, $\langle m+w_ D,v\rangle <(\operatorname {rec}g)(v)$ for every nonzero $v\in -\sigma $.

Proof. On the torus, polar coordinates turn the weighted monomial integral into a positive constant times

\[ \int _ Q\exp \bigl (2\langle m+w_ D,x\rangle -2g(x)\bigr )\, \mathrm dx, \]

where $Q$ is the logarithmic image of the domain. The omitted toric divisors have measure zero on the regular locus. A basis of neighborhoods of $o$ is given by $|\chi ^{b_ j}|<\epsilon $ for every $j$, with $0<\epsilon <1$. Their logarithmic domains satisfy

A.6
\begin{equation} -\sigma -b\ \subset Q_\epsilon =\{ x:\langle b_ j,x\rangle <\log \epsilon \text{ for all }j\} \ \subset \ -\sigma , \end{equation}

for $b\in \operatorname {int}\sigma $ sufficiently far along an interior ray. Indeed, every $\langle b_ j,b\rangle $ is strictly positive, and the $b_ j$ generate the dual cone. The convex-cone lemma gives necessity and sufficiency at $o$, including divergence when a recession inequality is an equality.

For a compact subset of $X_\sigma $, the embedding coordinates have absolute value at most some $R>1$. Its logarithmic image is contained in $\{ x:\langle b_ j,x\rangle \leq \log R\} $, which is contained in $b_ R-\sigma $ for a sufficiently large $b_ R\in \operatorname {int}\sigma $. Under the same strict condition the integral over this translated cone is finite. This proves local integrability everywhere. The converse follows by testing at $o$, and the interior formulation follows from the lemma. $\square$
