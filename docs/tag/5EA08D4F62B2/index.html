Theorem 1.20.65EA08D4F62B2

Theorem 1.20.6. For the preceding family $(\Gamma _\tau )_{\tau <c}$, the pointwise supremum in (1.70) is already upper semicontinuous on $X\times (0,\infty )$. Its complexification $U(x,z)=U_{\operatorname{Re}z}(x)$ is quasi-plurisubharmonic on $X\times \{ \operatorname{Re}z>0\} $ and satisfies $\pi ^*\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}U\geq 0$, where $\pi :X\times \{ \operatorname{Re}z>0\} \to X$ is the projection. Each slice belongs to $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$.

Proof. Each term $(x,z)\mapsto \Gamma _\tau (x)+\tau \operatorname{Re}z$ of the supremum satisfies the current inequality on the product, since $\operatorname{Re}z$ is pluriharmonic. The bound $\Gamma _\tau \leq 0$ gives $U_ t\leq ct$, while any fixed $\tau _0<c$ gives the minorant $\Gamma _{\tau _0}+t\tau _0$. By the local upper-envelope theorem, as in section 1.19, the upper semicontinuous regularization $V$ of $U$ on the product is quasi-plurisubharmonic, satisfies $\pi ^*\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}V\geq 0$, and agrees with $U$ almost everywhere. It does not depend on $\operatorname {Im}z$ and satisfies $V_ t\leq ct$. The pointwise supremum $U$ is measurable, since by the left continuity in lemma 1.20.5 the parameters may be restricted to rational numbers.

By Fubini's theorem, there is a set $Z\subset X$ of zero smooth volume, containing the pole set of $\Gamma _{\tau _0}$, such that $U_ t(x)=V_ t(x)$ for almost every $t>0$ whenever $x\notin Z$. For such $x$, both sides are finite convex functions of $t$, by lemma 1.20.4, lemma 1.20.1, so by continuity the equality holds for every $t>0$.

For each $\tau <c$, proposition 1.20.3 gives

\[ \chi _\tau =\inf _{t>0}(V_ t-t\tau ) \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )\cup \{ -\infty \} . \]

Off $Z$ it equals $\Gamma _\tau $ by lemma 1.20.5. In particular, $\chi _\tau $ is not identically $-\infty $, and lemma 1.3.3 gives $\chi _\tau =\Gamma _\tau $ everywhere. At each $x$, the convex function $t\mapsto V_ t(x)$ can be recovered from these inverse transforms by lemma 1.20.4, also when it is identically $-\infty $. We obtain

\[ V_ t(x)=\sup _{\tau <c}\bigl (\chi _\tau (x)+t\tau \bigr )=U_ t(x). \]

Finally, the minorant $\Gamma _{\tau _0}+t\tau _0$ shows that no slice $U_ t$ is identically $-\infty $, so every slice belongs to $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$. $\square$
