27.16 Newton selection and moment-shell limits5CEF27CB9AAA

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · 27.16 Newton selection and moment-shell limits

On a product of projective lines, selecting the monomials whose exponents lie in the dilates of a fixed polytope gives a graded family of section spaces for which the lower bound for the potentials can be proved directly, without a general approximation theorem. The limit of the kernel measures depends on the dimension of the polytope. When this dimension is smaller than that of the manifold, the limit is carried by a family of real tori and differs from the mixed curvature measures of the envelope.

Let $X=(\mathbb P^1)^ n$ with $n\geq 1$, and let $L=\mathcal O(k_1,\ldots ,k_ n)$, where each $k_ j$ is a positive integer. Equip $L$ with its product Fubini–Study metric $h$. On the torus $T=(\mathbb C^*)^ n$, in the standard frame $e$, write

\[ r_ j=\log |z_ j|^2,\qquad \varphi _0(r)=\sum _ jk_ j\log (1+\mathrm e^{r_ j}),\qquad \theta =c_1(L,h)=\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi _0. \]

Put $P=\prod _ j[0,k_ j]$. Fix a nonempty rational polytope $Q$ contained in the interior of $P$ whose affine span has the form

\[ \operatorname {aff}Q=b+W,\qquad b\in \mathbb Z^ n, \]

with $W\subseteq \mathbb R^ n$ a rational linear subspace. Set $d=\dim Q$ and $\Lambda =W\cap \mathbb Z^ n$. Let $\mathrm dq_\Lambda $ be the Lebesgue measure on $b+W$ normalized so that a fundamental domain of the lattice $\Lambda $ has volume one; when $d=0$, it is the unit point mass at $b$. Write $\operatorname {vol}_\Lambda (Q)$ for the volume of $Q$ with respect to $\mathrm dq_\Lambda $. In this section the selected spaces are defined directly by monomials, without a selecting potential: we select exactly the monomials with exponents in $mQ$,

27.116
\begin{equation} V_ m=\operatorname {span}\{ z^\alpha e^ m: \alpha \in mQ\cap \mathbb Z^ n\} . \end{equation}

Each of these monomials is a global section of $L^ m$, since $0\leq \alpha _ j\leq mk_ j$. Since $Q$ is convex, products of selected sections are selected, so these spaces form a graded family.

Choose a smooth real torus-invariant function $w$ on $X$ such that the function $F=\varphi _0+w$ of $r$ has positive definite Hessian $D^2F(r)$ for every $r\in \mathbb R^ n$. Choose a smooth strictly positive torus-invariant probability measure $\nu $ on $X$. On $T$ we then have $\mathrm d\nu =a(r)\, \mathrm dr\, \mathrm d\tau $, where $a>0$, $\int a\, \mathrm dr=1$, and $\mathrm d\tau $ is the Haar probability measure on $(S^1)^ n$ in the angular variables. Give $V_ m$ the Hilbert norm $\int _ X|s|_{h^ m}^2\mathrm e^{-mw}\, \mathrm d\nu $, and for an orthonormal basis put

\[ \mathcal B_ m=\sum _ j|s_{m,j}|_{h^ m}^2,\qquad B_ m=\mathrm e^{-mw}\mathcal B_ m,\qquad U_ m=m^{-1}\log \mathcal B_ m. \]

As in section 27.1, the density $B_ m$ includes the continuous weight.
