Chapter 12 · Monge–Ampère equations and stability · Tag C9E4D8D9901A

Projective exponential barrier growth

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 14.34.4

Lemma 14.34.4. As functions of $L$,

\[ q_\alpha =Q^{k-1}D\, \mathrm e^{k(L-1)},\qquad q_\alpha \geq b_0, \]

and $W(L)\sim k\alpha ^ k(1-\alpha )L^{-1-k(1-\alpha )}$ as $L\to +\infty $. For every $\tau >0$, $\mathrm e^{\tau (L^\alpha -1)}W(L)\to +\infty $ as $L\to +\infty $. In particular, $B_\tau \kappa ^ k$ has infinite mass on every punctured neighborhood of $o$.

Proof. In the notation of the proof of lemma 14.29.1, $t=r/(1+r)$ and $s(r)=L$. Since the mass of $q_\alpha \kappa ^ k$ on $\{ r_1<r<r_2\} $ is $Q(r_2)^ k-Q(r_1)^ k$, this measure has radial distribution $-\mathrm{d}(Q^ k)/\mathrm{d}L=W(L)\, \mathrm{d}L$. The Fubini–Study measure $\kappa ^ k$ corresponds to $Q=t$, so its radial distribution is $k\mathrm e^{-k(L-1)}\mathrm{d}L$. Both measures are invariant under the unitary group of the chart, so $q_\alpha $ is the ratio of the two distributions, which is the stated formula. The lower bound $q_\alpha \geq b_0$ follows from $\kappa +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_\alpha \geq (1-\alpha )\kappa $ off $o$. As $L\to +\infty $, $t$ tends to zero exponentially, so $Q\sim \alpha L^{\alpha -1}$ and $D\sim \alpha (1-\alpha )L^{\alpha -2}$; this gives the asymptotics of $W$. Consequently,

\[ \log \bigl (\mathrm e^{\tau (L^\alpha -1)}W(L)\bigr )=\tau L^\alpha -[1+k(1-\alpha )]\log L+O(1)\longrightarrow +\infty , \]

and the mass of $B_\tau \kappa ^ k$ on $\{ L>T\} $, which equals $\int _ T^\infty \mathrm e^{\tau (L^\alpha -1)}W(L)\, \mathrm{d}L$, is infinite for every $T$. $\square$
