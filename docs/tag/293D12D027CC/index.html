Chapter 11 · Non-pluripolar measures and mass · Tag 293D12D027CC

Mass bound exact trace

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 30.5.5

Corollary 30.5.5. Suppose that $Y$ has the bounded mass property. Choose a Hermitian form $\Omega _0\geq \theta |_ Y$ on $Y$ and a Hermitian form $\Omega \geq 2\Omega _0$ on $Y$ with $v_-(\Omega )>0$. Then

\[ \nu \bigl (\mathcal T_\Omega (u;Y)\circ \pi ,E\bigr )=\tau _{Y,u}(E) \]

for every prime divisor $E$ on every smooth compact modification $\pi :Z\to Y$.

Proof. Put $T=\mathcal T_\Omega (u;Y)$ and $r=\dim Y$. Normalize the majorant $v$ of proposition 19.6.1 by $\sup _ Yv=0$. It belongs to $\mathrm{PSH}(Y,\theta |_ Y)\subset \mathrm{PSH}(Y,\Omega /2)$ and is a competitor for $T$, so $v\leq T$. For every $w\in \mathrm{PSH}(Y,\Omega )$ with $w\sim T$, a constant translate of $v$ lies below $w$, so $P_{\Omega /2}(w)\not\equiv -\infty $. By lemma 13.5.2, applied on $Y$ with $a=1/2$,

\[ \int _ Y\mathrm{MA}_\Omega (w)\geq 2^{-r}v_-(\Omega ), \qquad \underline{\operatorname {vol}}_\Omega (T) \geq 2^{-r}v_-(\Omega )>0. \]

Since $\Omega -\theta |_ Y\geq \Omega _0$ is Hermitian and the curvature errors of the approximants tend to zero, the restrictions $u_ m|_ Y$ belong to $\mathrm{PSH}(Y,\Omega )$ for all sufficiently large $m$. By theorem 19.6.2, their dyadic ordinary envelopes $q_ j=P_\Omega [u_{2^ j}|_ Y]$ decrease to $T$. Theorem 30.5.2, applied on $Y$ in the fixed background $\Omega $, and the analytic type of $u_{2^ j}|_ Y$ give

\[ \nu (T\circ \pi ,E) =\lim _ j\nu (q_ j\circ \pi ,E) =\lim _ j2^{-j}\operatorname {ord}_ E\mathfrak a_{2^ j}(u;Y) =\tau _{Y,u}(E), \]

as required. $\square$
