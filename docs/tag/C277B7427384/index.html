15.2.5 Products with a Hermitian factorC277B7427384

An explicit product construction exhibits the criterion for densities with an arbitrary continuous modulus. Let $(Y,\kappa )$ be a connected compact Hermitian $k$-fold, $k\geq 1$, with $\int _ Y\kappa ^ k=1$. Denote by $\nu _\kappa $ the smooth stationary probability of $L_\kappa =\operatorname {tr}_\kappa \mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}$, given by the Gauduchon multiplier when $k\geq 2$; for $k=1$ it is $\kappa $. Write $w$ for the Fubini–Study area of mass one on $\mathbb P^1$. All factor forms and functions below are pulled back to $X=\mathbb P^1\times Y$.

Proposition 15.2.7. Let $a>0$, let $b$ be smooth and positive on $\mathbb P^1$, and let $A$ be continuous and positive there, with $\int _{\mathbb P^1}Aw=a$. For

\[ \omega =aw+b\kappa ,\qquad \mathrm dV=w\wedge \kappa ^ k, \qquad F=\log ((k+1)Ab^ k), \]

one has

\[ c_0(F)=1,\qquad \mathcal A_0(F)=\left\{ \frac Aa\, w\otimes \nu _\kappa \right\} . \]

Proof. The Green solution of $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u=(A-a)w$ on $\mathbb P^1$ has continuous complex Hessian and is bounded. Its pullback yields $\sigma =Aw+b\kappa >0$, and $\sigma ^{k+1}=(k+1)Ab^ kw\wedge \kappa ^ k$. Thus scalar uniqueness gives $c_0(F)=1$, and theorem 15.2.6 gives a unique support. For every smooth $h$ depending on all variables, the block decomposition

\[ L_\sigma h=A^{-1}L_ wh+b^{-1}L_\kappa h \]

shows that $(A/a)w\otimes \nu _\kappa $ is stationary: the first term integrates to zero by Stokes on $\mathbb P^1$, and the second by stationarity on $Y$ and Fubini. This candidate has a bounded density with respect to $\mathrm dV$. The one-dimensional annihilator of lemma 4.9.1 and probability normalization therefore identify it with the supporting probability. $\square$

Example 15.2.8. There is a non-Kähler threefold with a nonclosed Hermitian background and a positive continuous density whose logarithm is neither Dini continuous nor Hölder continuous of any positive order, but whose supporting probability is explicit and unique.

Proof. Take the Hopf surface $Y=(\mathbb C^2\setminus \{ 0\} )/\langle z\mapsto 2z\rangle $ and its unit-volume metric $\kappa =c|z|^{-2}\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}|z|^2$, where $c>0$ is chosen so that $\int _ Y\kappa ^2=1$. Put $s=\log |z|^2$, $\alpha =\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}s$ and $\beta =\mathrm ds\wedge \mathrm d^{\mathrm c}s$, so that $\kappa =c(\alpha +\beta )$. The differentiation in (8.1) applies in this dimension. Here $\alpha $ has rank one, so $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\kappa =-c\alpha ^2=0$ and $\nu _\kappa =\kappa ^2$. Polar coordinates identify $Y$ with $S^3\times S^1$, hence $H^2(Y,\mathbb R)=0$. A closed positive form would be exact and have positive square integral, contradicting Stokes. Thus $Y$ is non-Kähler, and so is $\mathbb P^1\times Y$, by restriction.

Let $\tau \in [-1,1]$ be the height on $\mathbb P^1$, set $b=2+\tau $, and put

\[ f(t)=\begin{cases} 1/\log (e/|t|),& 0<|t|\leq 1,\\ 0,& t=0, \end{cases} \]
\[ A=a+\varepsilon \left(f(\tau )-\int _{\mathbb P^1}f(\tau )w\right), \quad 0<\varepsilon <a. \]

Since $0\leq f\leq 1$, this is a positive continuous function of mean $a$. Along a normal segment to the equator, $|\tau |$ is comparable to the distance $r$. The oscillation of $A$ is therefore bounded below by a positive multiple of $1/\log (e/r)$. Its Dini integral $\int _0^{r_0}\mathrm dr/(r\log (e/r))$ diverges, and this modulus is larger than every $r^\gamma $, $\gamma >0$, as $r\downarrow 0$. The logarithm of $A$ has the same lower bound, because $A$ is uniformly positive and bounded. The smooth term $2\log b$ contributes only $O(r)$, so $F=\log (3Ab^2)$ has neither regularity. The mixed component $\mathrm db\wedge \kappa $ shows that $\omega =aw+b\kappa $ is nonclosed. By proposition 15.2.7, its supporting probability is $(A/a)w\wedge \kappa ^2$. $\square$

The theorem assumes a continuous positive complex Hessian for a bounded solution. It does not establish that hypothesis for every continuous $F$; the uniqueness question for arbitrary continuous data remains open.
