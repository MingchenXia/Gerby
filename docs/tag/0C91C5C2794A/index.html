Problem 14.3.220C91C5C2794A

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Problem 14.3.22

Problem 14.3.22. Consider a compact Kähler manifold $X$ and a connected submanifold $Y$. We have defined the trace operator $\operatorname{Tr}_ Y$ from a subset of $\mathrm{QPSH}(X)/\sim _\mathcal {I}$ to $\mathrm{QPSH}(Y)/\sim _\mathcal {I}$. Is it possible to refine this operator to one from a subset of $\mathrm{QPSH}(X)/\sim _ P$ to $\mathrm{QPSH}(Y)/\sim _ P$?
