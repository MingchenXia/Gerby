From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Corollary 10.5.3

Corollary 10.5.3. Let $\Omega \subset \mathbb C^ n$ be open, let $\varphi ,\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(\Omega )$, let $b$ be a continuous real function on $\Omega $, and let $f=\varphi -\psi +b$ on $\Omega \setminus P$, where $P=\{ \varphi =\psi =-\infty \} $. Let $Z\subset \Omega $ be a pluripolar set containing $P$, and let $W\subset \Omega $ be a nonempty plurifine open set. Then

\[ \operatorname *{q-sup}_ W f=\sup _{W\setminus Z}f, \qquad \operatorname *{q-inf}_ W f=\inf _{W\setminus Z}f. \]

In particular, the right-hand sides do not depend on the choice of $Z$.

Proof. The set $W'=W\setminus P$ is plurifine open, and $f$ is plurifinely continuous on $W'$, by proposition 10.5.1. Let $N$ be a pluripolar set containing $P$. We claim that $\sup _{W'\setminus N}f=\sup _{W'}f$. The inequality $\le $ holds because $W'\setminus N\subset W'$. Conversely, if $s\in \mathbb R$ and $s<\sup _{W'}f$, then $\{ z\in W':f(z)>s\} $ is a nonempty plurifine open set. It meets $\Omega \setminus N$, which is plurifine dense by proposition 10.3.3(2), and hence $\sup _{W'\setminus N}f>s$. This proves the claim, and the same argument with the sets $\{ f<s\} $ gives $\inf _{W'\setminus N}f=\inf _{W'}f$.

Since $W\setminus N=W'\setminus N$ whenever $N\supset P$, every pluripolar set $N\supset Z$ gives $\sup _{W\setminus N}f=\sup _{W\setminus P}f$; taking $N=Z$, we also get $\sup _{W\setminus Z}f=\sup _{W\setminus P}f$. Hence $\operatorname *{q-sup}_ Wf=\sup _{W\setminus Z}f=\sup _{W\setminus P}f$, and similarly $\operatorname *{q-inf}_ Wf=\inf _{W\setminus Z}f=\inf _{W\setminus P}f$. The common values do not involve $Z$. $\square$
