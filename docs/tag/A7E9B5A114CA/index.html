From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Theorem A.1.18

Theorem A.1.18. Let $C\subseteq N$ be an open subset. Let $(f_ i)_{i>0}$ be a sequence of real-valued convex functions on $C$. Suppose that the sequence converges on a dense subset of $C$ and the limit is finite. Then the limit

\[ f(x)\coloneqq \lim _{i\to \infty } f_ i(x) \]

exists for all $x\in C$ and is convex on $C$. Moreover, the sequence $(f_ i)_ i$ converges uniformly to $f$ on each compact subset of $C$.
