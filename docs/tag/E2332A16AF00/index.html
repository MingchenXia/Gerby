Example 9.2.12

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Example 9.2.12

Example 9.2.12. Let us see what the test curve in example 9.1.6 corresponds to under the Ross–Witt Nyström correspondence. Fix $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$. We claim that

9.43
\begin{equation} \label{eq:RWNsingtype} \ell ^{\varphi }=\Gamma ^{\varphi *}, \end{equation}

where $\ell ^{\varphi }$ is as in example 4.3.26. We may assume that $\varphi \leq 0$ since both sides are invariant after adding a constant to $\varphi $.

We first prove the easy direction $\ell ^{\varphi }\geq \Gamma ^{\varphi *}$, which is equivalent to $\ell ^{\varphi *}\geq \Gamma ^{\varphi }$. Since $\ell ^{\varphi *}$ is a test curve, the latter is equivalent to

\[ \ell ^{\varphi *}_{\tau }\geq (1+\tau )\varphi -\tau V_{\theta } \]

for all $\tau \in (-1,0)$. By Legendre duality, this is equivalent to

9.44
\begin{equation} \label{eq:lvarphigeq_temp1} \ell ^{\varphi }_ t\geq \sup _{\tau \in (-1,0)} \Bigl ((1+\tau )\varphi -\tau V_{\theta }+t\tau \Bigr )=\varphi \lor (V_{\theta }-t) \end{equation}

for all $t>0$.

Using the notations of example 4.3.26, we find easily that

\[ \ell ^{\varphi ,C}_ t\geq \varphi \lor (V_{\theta }-t) \]

for any $C>0$ and $t\in [0,C]$, since it holds at $t=0$ and $t=C$. Letting $C\to \infty $, we find (9.44). Therefore, $\ell ^{\varphi }\geq \Gamma ^{\varphi *}$ follows.

In order to prove the equality in (9.43), it suffices to show that the two sides have the same energy, as a consequence of (4.94). So we compute

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathbf{E}\left(\Gamma ^{\varphi *}\right)=& \mathbf{E}\left(\Gamma ^{\varphi }\right)\\ =& \int _{-1}^0 \left(\int _ X \theta _{(1+\tau )\varphi -\tau V_{\theta }}^ n-\int _ X \theta _{V_{\theta }}^ n\right)\, \mathrm{d}\tau \\ =& \sum _{j=0}^{n} \binom {n}{j} \int _ X \theta _{\varphi }^{j}\wedge \theta _{V_{\theta }}^{n-j} \int _0^1 \tau ^ j (1-\tau )^{n-j}\, \mathrm{d}\tau -\int _ X \theta _{V_{\theta }}^ n\\ =& \sum _{j=0}^{n} \binom {n}{j}\frac{j!(n-j)!}{(n+1)!} \int _ X \theta _{\varphi }^{j}\wedge \theta _{V_{\theta }}^{n-j}-\int _ X \theta _{V_{\theta }}^ n\\ =& \mathbf{E}(\ell ^{\varphi }), \end{aligned} \]
where in the second line we used proposition 3.1.6, in the fourth line we used the value of the $\beta $-function
Note. Also known as Euler integral of the first kind.
, and the last line is just (4.103).
