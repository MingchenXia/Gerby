Hessian mixed trace

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 12.1.2

Lemma 12.1.2. If $\alpha ,\gamma \in \Gamma _ m(\omega )$, then

\[ \frac{\alpha ^{m-1}\wedge \gamma \wedge \omega ^{n-m}}{\omega ^ n} \frac{\alpha \wedge \omega ^{n-1}}{\omega ^ n} \geq \frac1{m!} \frac{\alpha ^ m\wedge \omega ^{n-m}}{\omega ^ n} \frac{\gamma \wedge \omega ^{n-1}}{\omega ^ n}. \]

The assertion extends to $\gamma \in \overline{\Gamma _ m(\omega )}$ by continuity.

Proof. Write $\sigma _ k=\alpha ^ k\wedge \omega ^{n-k}/\omega ^ n$, and $M_ k=\alpha ^{k-1}\wedge \gamma \wedge \omega ^{n-k}/\omega ^ n$. For $1\leq k<m$, the cone shift with index $k+1$ and Gårding positivity give

\[ M_{k+1}\geq tM_ k \quad \left(0\leq t<\frac{\sigma _{k+1}}{(k+1)\sigma _ k}\right). \]

Let $t$ increase to the displayed endpoint. Iterating, starting with $M_1=\gamma \wedge \omega ^{n-1}/\omega ^ n$, yields

\[ M_ m\geq \frac{\sigma _ m}{m!\sigma _1}M_1. \]

All denominators are positive. Approximating a form in the closed cone by $\gamma +\delta \omega $, $\delta \downarrow 0$, gives the last assertion. $\square$
