Affine classes with prescribed derivative

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · 24.1 Affine classes with prescribed derivative

Two real $(1,1)$-currents on $Z$ with exterior derivative $\pi ^*\mathrm{d}\theta $ differ by a closed current, and modulo $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}$ of global distributions such differences are Bott–Chern classes. Let $\mathcal D'(Z,\mathbb R)$ denote the space of real distributions on $Z$, and set

\[ \mathscr A_ Z(\theta )= \frac{\{ \pi ^*\theta +C:C\text{ is a closed real }(1,1)\text{-current on }Z\} }{\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\mathcal D'(Z,\mathbb R)}. \]

Write $\{ T\} $ for the class of $T$ in this space. By lemma 23.4.1, every class has a smooth representative, and translation by Bott–Chern classes gives

\[ \mathscr A_ Z(\theta )=\{ \pi ^*\theta \} +H^{1,1}_{\mathrm{BC}}(Z,\mathbb R). \]

A smooth real $(1,1)$-form $\eta $ represents an element of $\mathscr A_ Z(\theta )$ exactly when $\mathrm{d}\eta =\pi ^*\mathrm{d}\theta $. As for Bott–Chern classes, pullback of a class is defined on a smooth representative and pushforward on current representatives.

Proposition 24.1.1. If $g:Z'\to Z$ is a compact modification over $Y$, pushforward and pullback induce maps

\[ g_*:\mathscr A_{Z'}(\theta )\to \mathscr A_ Z(\theta ), \qquad g^*:\mathscr A_ Z(\theta )\to \mathscr A_{Z'}(\theta ), \qquad g_*g^*=\mathrm{id}. \]

Pushforward preserves classes with positive current representatives.

Proof. Write $\pi '=\pi \circ g$. Translation by the pulled-back background identifies the required maps with the Bott–Chern maps of section 23.4. Indeed, for a closed current $C'$ on $Z'$ and a smooth closed form $\beta $ on $Z$,

\[ g_*(\pi '^*\theta +C')=\pi ^*\theta +g_*C',\qquad g^*(\pi ^*\theta +\beta )=\pi '^*\theta +g^*\beta , \]

where the first equality uses the change of variables formula for the degree-one map $g$ on the smooth background. Since the Bott–Chern maps are well defined on the closed terms, these identities define the affine maps independently of the representatives. Their composition is the identity because $g_*g^*$ is the identity both on the background and on Bott–Chern classes. Finally, pushforward under a proper map preserves positive currents. $\square$

The prescribed derivative is essential for this pushforward: $g_*\pi '^*\mathrm{d}\theta =\pi ^*\mathrm{d}\theta $ is smooth, whereas the pushforward of an arbitrary smooth exact form can be a singular current.

We call a family $(\mathbb A_ Z)$, with $\mathbb A_ Z\in \mathscr A_ Z(\theta )$ for every model $Z$ and compatible with pushforward, a b-divisor class over the background $\theta $. Pseudoeffectivity and Cartier realizations are defined as in definition 23.4.2, with affine classes in place of Bott–Chern classes. A class in $\mathscr A_ Z(\theta )$ is nef if, for a Hermitian form $\gamma $ on $Z$, it has for every $\epsilon >0$ a smooth representative bounded below by $-\epsilon \gamma $, as in section 23.6.
