Proposition 9.1.8

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 9.1.8

Proposition 9.1.8. Let $\pi \colon Y\rightarrow X$ be a proper bimeromorphic morphism from a Kähler manifold $Y$. Then the pointwise pull-back induces a bijection

\[ \pi ^*\colon \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta ;\phi )\xlongrightarrow {\sim } \mathrm{TC}(Y,\pi ^*\theta ;\pi ^*\phi ). \]

Proof. The bijectivity on the underlying potentials follows from proposition 1.5.8. The compatibility with model potentials follows from proposition 3.1.23. These two facts also preserve concavity, monotonicity and the value of $\Gamma _{-\infty }$, hence give the asserted bijection of test curves. $\square$
