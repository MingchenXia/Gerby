Lemma 12.6.8F28CED8C4FAF

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 12.6.8

Lemma 12.6.8. For continuous admissible $u,v$ and $a>0$, if

\[ e^{-av}H_ m(v)\geq e^{-au}H_ m(u), \]

then $u\geq v$.

Proof. Fix $\delta >0$. On $\{ u<v-\delta \} $, the assumed inequality gives

\[ H_ m(u)\leq e^{a(u-v)}H_ m(v) \leq e^{-a\delta }H_ m(v-\delta ). \]

The constant $e^{-a\delta }$ is strictly smaller than one, so 12.6.7 applied to $u$ and $v-\delta $ gives $u\geq v-\delta $. Letting $\delta \downarrow 0$ proves the assertion. $\square$
