17.15 Operations on test curves9D751AA26579

9D751AA26579

Let $X$ be a connected compact Kähler manifold of dimension $n$ and $\theta ,\theta ',\theta ''$ be smooth closed real $(1,1)$-forms on $X$ representing big cohomology classes.

In this section, we develop several general operations on test curves, anticipating the applications in non-Archimedean geometry in section 22.1. One may read section 22.1 first and consult this section when necessary.

Definition 17.15.1.DF47E4B2060A Given $\Gamma \in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta )_{>0}$, $\Gamma '\in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta ')_{>0}$, we say $\Gamma \leq \Gamma '$ if $\Gamma _{\max }\leq \Gamma '_{\max }$ and, for all $\tau <\Gamma _{\max }$, we have

B3E2E25A3B00 17.15.2
\begin{equation} \Gamma _{\tau }\preceq _ P \Gamma '_{\tau }. \end{equation}
Observe that (17.15.2) actually holds for all $\tau \in \mathbb {R}$ if $\theta =\theta '$. Here we use the convention that $-\infty \preceq _ P \varphi $ for any $\varphi \in \mathrm{QPSH}(X)\cup \{ -\infty \} $. The relation $\leq $ defines a partial order on $\mathrm{TC}(X,\theta )_{>0}$.

Lemma 17.15.6.5BE2CC04AAD0 Let $\Gamma \in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ and $\Gamma ' \in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta ')_{>0}$. Then we have $\Gamma +\Gamma '\in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta +\theta ')_{>0}$. In particular, for any $\tau <(\Gamma +\Gamma ')_{\max }$,

\[ \sup _{\delta \in \mathbb {R}} \left(\Gamma _\delta +\Gamma '_{\tau -\delta }\right)\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta +\theta '). \]

This potential is $\mathcal{I}$-good if $\Gamma \in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ and $\Gamma ' \in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta ')_{>0}$.

In particular, (17.15.5) in definition 17.15.4 makes sense.

Proof. Let

\[ \eta _{\tau }=\sup _{\delta \in \mathbb {R}} \left(\Gamma _\delta +\Gamma '_{\tau -\delta }\right)=\sup _{\tau -\Gamma '_{\max }<\delta <\Gamma _{\max }}\left(\Gamma _\delta +\Gamma '_{\tau -\delta }\right) \]

for all $\tau \in \mathbb {R}$. Set

\[ Z=\left\{ x\in X: \Gamma _{\upsilon }(x)=-\infty \, \forall \upsilon \in \mathbb {R}\right\} \cup \left\{ x\in X: \Gamma '_{\upsilon }(x)=-\infty \, \forall \upsilon \in \mathbb {R}\right\} . \]

It follows from proposition 21.11.37 that for any $x\in X\setminus Z$, we have

\[ \eta ^*_ t(x)=\Gamma ^*_ t(x)+\Gamma '^*_ t(x) \]

for all $t>0$. The same trivially holds when $x\in Z$, so the equation holds everywhere. In particular, by corollary 21.11.26 and proposition 5.14.15, we have

\[ \eta _{\tau }=\left(\Gamma ^*+\Gamma '^*\right)^*_{\tau }\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta +\theta ') \]

when $\tau <\Gamma _{\max }+\Gamma '_{\max }$.

We now check that $\Gamma +\Gamma '$ is a test curve. The family $\tau \mapsto \eta _\tau $ is decreasing, and the monotonicity of the $P$-envelope proposition 16.4.2 3 gives the decreasingness of $\Gamma +\Gamma '$. If $\tau =(1-s)\tau _0+s\tau _1$, then for arbitrary $\delta _0,\delta _1\in \mathbb {R}$ and $\delta =(1-s)\delta _0+s\delta _1$,

\[ \Gamma _\delta +\Gamma '_{\tau -\delta }\geq (1-s)(\Gamma _{\delta _0}+\Gamma '_{\tau _0-\delta _0})+s(\Gamma _{\delta _1}+\Gamma '_{\tau _1-\delta _1}). \]

Taking suprema and using proposition 16.4.2 gives the concavity of $\Gamma +\Gamma '$.

Fix $\tau <\Gamma _{\max }+\Gamma '_{\max }$. Choose $\delta \in \mathbb {R}$ with $\delta <\Gamma _{\max }$ and $\tau -\delta <\Gamma '_{\max }$. Then

\[ \eta _\tau \geq \Gamma _\delta +\Gamma '_{\tau -\delta }. \]

By 17.12.6, $\Gamma _\delta \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ and $\Gamma '_{\tau -\delta }\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ')_{>0}$. Hence $\Gamma _\delta +\Gamma '_{\tau -\delta }$ has positive $(\theta +\theta ')$-mass by the multi-linearity of non-pluripolar products proposition 13.11.4 6. It follows from the monotonicity theorem theorem 13.13.11 that $\eta _\tau \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta +\theta ')_{>0}$. By corollary 16.3.20,

\[ (\Gamma +\Gamma ')_\tau =P_{\theta +\theta '}[\eta _\tau ] \]

is a model potential. Finally, $(\Gamma +\Gamma ')_{-\infty }\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta +\theta ')$ by proposition 5.14.2; since it dominates $(\Gamma +\Gamma ')_\tau $ for any $\tau <\Gamma _{\max }+\Gamma '_{\max }$, it has positive mass by another application of the monotonicity theorem theorem 13.13.11. Thus $\Gamma +\Gamma '\in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta +\theta ')_{>0}$.

Next, assume that $\Gamma $ and $\Gamma '$ are $\mathcal{I}$-model. Fix $\tau <\Gamma _{\max }+\Gamma '_{\max }$. Then for each $\delta \in \mathbb {R}$ such that $\delta <\Gamma _{\max }$ and $\tau -\delta <\Gamma '_{\max }$, we know that $\Gamma _\delta \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ and $\Gamma '_{\tau -\delta }\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ')_{>0}$ by 17.12.6. It follows from example 18.1.11 that $\Gamma _\delta $ and $\Gamma '_{\tau -\delta }$ are both $\mathcal{I}$-good, hence so is $\Gamma _\delta +\Gamma '_{\tau -\delta }\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta +\theta ')_{>0}$ by proposition 18.1.17. These sums are bounded above by $0$, so $\eta _{\tau }$ is $\mathcal{I}$-good by proposition 18.1.19. Since $(\Gamma +\Gamma ')_\tau =P_{\theta +\theta '}[\eta _\tau ]$, we conclude that $\Gamma +\Gamma '$ is $\mathcal{I}$-model. $\square$

Proposition 17.15.7.FBC25DBE6122 Let $\Gamma \in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ and $\Gamma ' \in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta ')_{>0}$. Then $\Gamma +\Gamma '\in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta +\theta ')_{>0}$. Moreover,

22BECA7DAF92 17.15.8
\begin{equation} (\Gamma +\Gamma ')_{-\infty }=P_{\theta +\theta '}\left[\Gamma _{-\infty }+\Gamma '_{-\infty }\right]. \end{equation}

When $\Gamma \in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ and $\Gamma '\in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta ')_{>0}$, we have $\Gamma +\Gamma '\in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta +\theta ')_{>0}$.

The operation $+$ is commutative and associative.

Proof. It follows immediately from 17.15.6 that $\Gamma +\Gamma '\in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta +\theta ')_{>0}$. It lies in $\mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta +\theta ')_{>0}$ if $\Gamma \in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ and $\Gamma '\in \mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta ')_{>0}$.

We argue (17.15.8). By definition, for any small enough $\tau $, we have

\[ (\Gamma +\Gamma ')_{-\infty }\geq (\Gamma +\Gamma ')_{2\tau }\succeq _ P \Gamma _{\tau }+\Gamma '_{\tau }. \]

Letting $\tau \to -\infty $ and applying proposition 16.9.44 and theorem 16.9.45, we find that

\[ (\Gamma +\Gamma ')_{-\infty }\succeq _ P \Gamma _{-\infty }+\Gamma '_{-\infty }. \]

On the other hand, for each small enough $\tau $, we have

\[ (\Gamma +\Gamma ')_{\tau }\sim _ P \sup _{\delta \in \mathbb {R}}\left( \Gamma _\delta +\Gamma '_{\tau -\delta }\right) \preceq _ P \Gamma _{-\infty }+\Gamma '_{-\infty } \]

by proposition 16.8.27 and proposition 16.9.44. We apply proposition 16.9.44 again, we conclude that

\[ (\Gamma +\Gamma ')_{-\infty } \preceq _ P \Gamma _{-\infty }+\Gamma '_{-\infty }. \]

So (17.15.8) follows.

Finally, let us show that $+$ is commutative and associative. Commutativity follows from the symmetry of the defining supremum. Let $\Gamma ''\in \mathrm{TC}(X,\theta '')_{>0}$. Then we want to show that

\[ (\Gamma +\Gamma ')+\Gamma ''=\Gamma +(\Gamma '+\Gamma ''). \]

First observe that

\[ \left((\Gamma +\Gamma ')+\Gamma '' \right)_{\max }=\left( \Gamma +(\Gamma '+\Gamma '')\right)_{\max }. \]

Fix $\tau $ less than this common value. We compute that

\[ \begin{aligned} & \left( (\Gamma +\Gamma ')+\Gamma ''\right)_{\tau }\\ =& P_{\theta +\theta '+\theta ''}\left[\sup _{\delta _1\in \mathbb {R}} \left((\Gamma +\Gamma ')_{\delta _1}+\Gamma ''_{\tau -\delta _1}\right)\right]\\ \sim _ P & \sup _{\delta _1\in \mathbb {R}} \left((\Gamma +\Gamma ')_{\delta _1}+\Gamma ''_{\tau -\delta _1}\right)\\ \sim _ P & \sup _{\delta _1,\delta _2\in \mathbb {R}} \left(\Gamma _{\delta _2}+\Gamma '_{\delta _1-\delta _2}+\Gamma ''_{\tau -\delta _1}\right), \end{aligned} \]

where in the last line, we applied proposition 16.9.44 and proposition 16.8.27. Similarly, for $\left( \Gamma +(\Gamma '+\Gamma '')\right)_{\tau }$, we get the same expression. The associativity follows. $\square$
