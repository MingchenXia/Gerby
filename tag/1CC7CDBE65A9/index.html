Lemma 2.4.15

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Lemma 2.4.15

Lemma 2.4.15. Let $\varphi ,\psi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$, $\varphi \preceq \psi $ and $\int _ X \theta _{\varphi }^ n>0$. Then for any

2.47
\begin{equation} \label{eq:arangetemp} a\in \left( 1, \left(\frac{\int _ X \theta _{\psi }^ n}{\int _ X \theta _{\psi }^ n-\int _ X \theta _{\varphi }^ n}\right)^{1/n} \right)\footnotemark , \end{equation}

there is $\eta \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ such that

2.48
\begin{equation} \label{eq:path_env} a^{-1}\eta +\left(1-a^{-1}\right)\psi \leq \varphi . \end{equation}

Proof. Without loss of generality, we may assume that $\varphi \leq \psi \leq 0$.

Step 1. We show the existence of $\eta \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ satisfying (2.48).

Step 1.1. We first consider the case where $\psi \sim \varphi $. In this case, we can take $\eta =\varphi -C$ enough $C>0$. But since by (2.52), we also have $P_{\theta }(a\varphi +(1-a)\psi )\leq \psi $, we find that

\[ P_{\theta }\Bigl (a\varphi +(1-a)\psi \Bigr )\sim \varphi \sim \psi \]

in this case.

Step 1.2. We make a first reduction of the problem.

Now for each $\gamma \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$, we let

\[ P_{\theta }[\gamma ]\coloneqq \operatorname *{sup}_{}{}\! {}^{*}\Bigl \{ \eta \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ): \eta \leq \min \{ \gamma +C,0\} \textup{ for some }C>0 \Bigr \} \footnotemark . \]
Note. This is precisely the $P$-envelope to be introduced later in definition 3.1.4.

Observe that due to corollary 2.4.9 and theorem 2.4.8, we have

2.53
\begin{equation} \label{eq:intthetaP_pr_mass_temp1} \int _ X \theta _{P_{\theta }[\gamma ]}^ n=\int _ X \theta _{\gamma }^ n. \end{equation}

Further, $0\geq P_{\theta }[\gamma ]\geq \gamma $ if $\gamma \leq 0$.

We now define an increasing transfinite sequence $(\psi _{\alpha })_{\alpha }$ consisting of non-positive functions in $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ as follows:

  1. $\psi _0=\psi $;

  2. $\psi _{\alpha +1}=P_{\theta }[\psi _{\alpha }]$;

  3. if $\alpha $ is a limit ordinal, we define

    \[ \psi _{\alpha }=\operatorname *{sup}_{\beta <\alpha }{}\! {}^{*} \psi _{\beta }. \]

Observe that

\[ \int _ X \theta _{\psi _{\alpha }}^ n=\int _ X \theta _{\psi }^ n \]

by corollary 2.4.9 and (2.53). Since $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ is indeed a set, the transfinite sequence must stabilize, say at some $\phi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$. Note that $0 \geq \phi \geq \psi $, and $P_{\theta }[\phi ]=\phi $.

Note that the interval (2.47) remains the same if we replace $\psi $ by $\phi $. Take $a$ in this interval, it suffices to prove the existence of $\eta \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ so that

2.54
\begin{equation} \label{eq:path_env_temp1} a^{-1}\eta +\left(1-a^{-1}\right)\phi \leq \varphi . \end{equation}

Let us record the following observation for later use. Suppose that $\tau \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ and $\tau \preceq \phi $. Then

2.55
\begin{equation} \label{eq:modelpot_sup_temp1} \sup _{\{ \phi \neq -\infty \} } (\tau -\phi )=\sup _ X \tau . \end{equation}

Observe that $\phi \leq 0$, so on the set $\{ \phi \neq -\infty \} $, we have

\[ \tau -\phi \geq \tau . \]

So the $\geq $ direction in (2.55) follows from corollary 1.3.18. Conversely, by assumption, we can find a constant $C>0$ so that

\[ \tau -\sup _ X \tau \leq \min \{ \phi +C,0 \} . \]

It follows that $\tau -\sup _ X \tau \leq P_{\theta }[\phi ]=\phi $. Therefore, the reverse inequality follows, and (2.55) follows.

For each $k>0$, we introduce

\[ \varphi _ k=\varphi \lor \left(\phi -k\right),\quad \eta _ k\coloneqq P_{\theta }\Bigl ( a \varphi _ k+(1-a)\phi \Bigr ). \]

Since $\varphi _ k\sim \phi $, using Step 1.1, we have $\eta _ k\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ and $\eta _ k\sim \phi $ as well.

Note that $(\eta _ k)_ k$ is decreasing in $k$. Let

\[ \eta \coloneqq \inf _{k\in \mathbb {N}} \eta _ k. \]

Note that $\eta $ automatically satisfies (2.54). It remains to show that $\eta \not\equiv -\infty $.

Step 1.3. We prove that $\eta \not\equiv -\infty $.

Suppose, for contradiction, that $\sup _ X \eta _ k\to -\infty $. For each $k>0$, let

\[ \gamma _ k\coloneqq a^{-1}\eta _ k+\left(1-a^{-1}\right)\phi ,\quad D_ k\coloneqq \left\{ \gamma _ k=\varphi _ k\right\} . \]

We claim that

2.56
\begin{equation} \label{eq:1Dkan_temp1} \theta _{\eta _ k}^ n\leq a^ n\mathds {1}_{D_ k}\, \theta _{\varphi _ k}^ n. \end{equation}

Since $\gamma _ k\leq \varphi _ k$ with equality on $D_ k$, 2.4.10 gives

\[ \mathds {1}_{D_ k}\, \theta _{\gamma _ k}^ n\leq \mathds {1}_{D_ k}\, \theta _{\varphi _ k}^ n. \]

On the other hand,

\[ \theta _{\gamma _ k}=a^{-1}\theta _{\eta _ k}+\left(1-a^{-1}\right)\theta _{\phi }, \]

so positivity and multilinearity of the non-pluripolar product imply

\[ a^{-n}\theta _{\eta _ k}^ n\leq \theta _{\gamma _ k}^ n. \]

Finally, it follows from theorem 2.4.14 that

\[ \mathds {1}_{D_ k}\, \theta _{\eta _ k}^ n=\theta _{\eta _ k}^ n. \]

Putting these results together, we conclude (2.56).

Fix $j>k>0$. Note that

2.57
\begin{equation} \label{eq:intthetavarphijn_temp1} \int _{\{ \varphi \leq \phi -k \} } \theta _{\varphi _ j}^ n=\int _ X \theta _{\varphi _ j}^ n-\int _{\{ \varphi >\phi -k \} } \theta _{\varphi _ j}^ n=\int _ X \theta _{\phi }^ n-\int _{\{ \varphi >\phi -k \} } \theta _{\varphi }^ n, \end{equation}

where we applied theorem 2.4.8 and proposition 2.2.2 1 in the second equality.

Next, we compute

\[ \begin{aligned} \int _{\left\{ \eta _ j\leq \phi -ak \right\} } \theta _{\eta _ j}^ n\leq & a^ n \int _{\left\{ \eta _ j\leq \phi -ak \right\} } \mathds {1}_{D_ j}\, \theta _{\varphi _ j}^ n & \textup{by (\href{../../tag/87D0AE27AE70/}{2.56})}\\ \leq & a^ n \int _{\left\{ a\varphi _ j+(1-a)\phi \leq \phi -ak \right\} \cap \left\{ \phi \neq -\infty \right\} } \theta _{\varphi _ j}^ n\\ = & a^ n \int _{\left\{ \varphi _ j\leq \phi -k \right\} } \theta _{\varphi _ j}^ n\\ = & a^ n \int _{\left\{ \varphi \leq \phi -k \right\} } \theta _{\varphi _ j}^ n\\ \leq & a^ n\left( \int _ X \theta _{\phi }^ n- \int _{\left\{ \varphi >\phi -k \right\} } \theta _{\varphi }^ n \right)& \textup{ by (\href{../../tag/2263D1F9CA2F/}{2.57})}. \end{aligned} \]

Next thanks to (2.55),

\[ \sup _{ \left\{ \phi \neq -\infty \right\} } \left(\eta _ j-\phi \right)=\sup _ X \eta _ j\to -\infty . \]

In particular, for a fixed $k$, if $j$ is large enough, we have

\[ \left\{ \eta _ j\leq \phi -ak \right\} =X. \]

Therefore, for a fixed $k$, for any large enough $j$,

\[ \int _ X \theta _{\phi }^ n=\int _ X \theta _{\eta _ j}^ n\leq a^ n\left( \int _ X \theta _{\phi }^ n- \int _{\{ \varphi >\phi -k\} } \theta _{\varphi }^ n\right). \]

Letting $k\to \infty $, we find

\[ \int _ X \theta _{\phi }^ n\leq a^ n \left(\int _ X \theta _{\phi }^ n-\int _ X \theta _{\varphi }^ n \right). \]

This is a contradiction with our choice of $a$.

Step 2. Next we argue that $P_{\theta }(a\varphi +(1-a)\psi )\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$. Choose

\[ a'\in \left( a, \left(\frac{\int _ X \theta _{\psi }^ n}{\int _ X \theta _{\psi }^ n-\int _ X \theta _{\varphi }^ n}\right)^{1/n} \right). \]

It follows from (2.49) that

2.63
\begin{equation} \label{eq:Pthetalowerbdtemp1} P_{\theta }\Bigl (a\varphi +(1-a)\psi \Bigr )\geq \frac{a}{a'} P_{\theta }\Bigl (a'\varphi +(1-a')\psi \Bigr )+\frac{a'-a}{a'}\varphi . \end{equation}

Therefore, by theorem 2.4.8, we have

2.64
\begin{equation} \label{eq:Pthetalowerbdtemp2} \int _ X \theta _{P_{\theta }\left(a\varphi +(1-a)\psi \right)}^ n\geq \frac{(a'-a)^ n}{a'^ n}\int _ X \theta _{\varphi }^ n>0. \end{equation}

We conclude the proof. $\square$
