Finite restricted null union

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 28.6.4

Corollary 28.6.4. Let $a$ be a nef class on $X$. For every positive-dimensional irreducible reduced subvariety $Y\subset X$,

\[ \mathcal R(a;Y)=\int _ Ya^{\dim Y}. \]

If $\int _ Xa^ n>0$, then

28.9
\begin{equation} \label{eq:finite-restricted-null-union} \bigcup _{\substack {Y\subset X\ \mathrm{irreducible},\ \dim Y>0\\ \mathcal R(a;Y)=0}}Y =\operatorname {Null}(a)=E_{\mathrm{nK}}(a). \end{equation}

Proof. The pullback of $a$ to a Kähler resolution of $Y$ is nef: pull back smooth representatives with arbitrarily small negative part, and bound the pullback of $\omega $ by a multiple of a Kähler form on the resolution. Since the intersection number $\int _ Ya^{\dim Y}$ is defined through the same pullback, (28.6) gives the first assertion. The first equality in (28.9) then follows from the definition of the null locus, and the second is the classical theorem on nef and big classes recalled earlier in this chapter. $\square$
