Four point least singular extendable

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 28.18.9

Corollary 28.18.9. Among all semipositive metrics on $L_0$ that extend, $h_*$ has the least singular weights: every such metric has weights $\psi $ satisfying $\psi \leq \psi _*+C$ in compatible local frames, where $\psi _*$ is the weight of $h_*$; and $h_*$ itself extends. The assertion concerns metrics that extend, not all semipositive metrics on $L_0$.

Proof. By theorem 28.18.8, such a metric has the form $h_\ell \otimes f^*h_ B$. Its weight difference from $h_*$ is the pullback of the global quasi-plurisubharmonic difference between the weights of $h_ B$ and $h_{\mathrm{FS}}$, which is bounded above on $\mathbb P^1$. This proves the inequality. That $h_*$ extends is lemma 28.18.6. $\square$
