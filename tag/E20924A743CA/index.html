Holomorphic mean lowest term

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Lemma 12.2.6

Lemma 12.2.6. Let $F$ be a holomorphic function on an open set $D\subset \mathbb C^ n$, $n\geq 1$.

  1. If $\overline{B(y,\delta )}\subset D$, then

    12.9
    \begin{equation} \label{eq:holomorphic-ball-mean-bound} |F(y)|^2\leq \frac1{|B(y,\delta )|}\int _{B(y,\delta )}|F|^2\, \mathrm dV. \end{equation}

  2. Let $x\in D$, suppose that the germ of $F$ at $x$ is not zero, and let $P_ k\neq 0$ be the first nonzero homogeneous term of the expansion of $F$ at $x$. Then for all sufficiently small $t>0$

    12.10
    \begin{equation} \label{eq:sphere-lowest-term} \int _{S^{2n-1}}|F(x+t\omega )|^2\, \mathrm dS(\omega )\geq t^{2k}\int _{S^{2n-1}}|P_ k|^2\, \mathrm dS, \qquad \int _{S^{2n-1}}|P_ k|^2\, \mathrm dS>0. \end{equation}

Proof. (1) The function $F$ is smooth with $\Delta F=4\sum _ j\partial _{z_ j}\partial _{\bar z_ j}F=0$, by (7.5); so its real and imaginary parts are harmonic and have the ball mean value property (proposition 2.1.1). Thus $F(y)$ is the mean of $F$ over $B(y,\delta )$, and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality gives (12.9).

(2) Homogeneous polynomials $P_ l$, $P_{l'}$ of different degrees are orthogonal in $L^2$ of the unit sphere: $P_ l(\mathrm e^{\mathrm i\theta }\omega )=\mathrm e^{\mathrm il\theta }P_ l(\omega )$, and surface measure is invariant under the orthogonal maps $\omega \mapsto \mathrm e^{\mathrm i\theta }\omega $ (section 2.1), so $\int _{S^{2n-1}}P_ l\overline{P_{l'}}\, \mathrm dS=\mathrm e^{\mathrm i(l-l')\theta }\int _{S^{2n-1}}P_ l\overline{P_{l'}}\, \mathrm dS$ for every $\theta $. For small $t>0$ the homogeneous expansion $F(x+t\omega )=\sum _ lt^ lP_ l(\omega )$ converges uniformly in $\omega \in S^{2n-1}$, by (7.1); integrating term by term, we obtain $\int _{S^{2n-1}}|F(x+t\omega )|^2\, \mathrm dS(\omega )=\sum _{l\geq k}t^{2l}\int _{S^{2n-1}}|P_ l|^2\, \mathrm dS$, which implies the first inequality. The last integral is positive, because $P_ k$ does not vanish identically on the sphere, by homogeneity, and nonempty relatively open subsets of the sphere have positive surface measure. $\square$
