Chapter 5 · Plurisubharmonic functions and positive currents · Tag 61D6716B27F1

Proposition 1.4.3

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 1.4.3

Proposition 1.4.3. Let $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(B_ n(0,1))$. Then

1.25
\begin{equation} \label{eq:Lelongnewdef} \nu (\varphi ,0)=\lim _{r \to 0+} \frac{\sup _{B_ n(0,r)}\varphi }{\log r^2}\in [0,\infty ). \end{equation}

Proof. Note that (1.25) is trivial when $n=0$, so we may assume that $n>0$ in the proof.

It follows from proposition 1.1.8 that the limit in (1.25) exists and is finite. We shall denote the limit by $\nu '(\varphi ,0)$ for the time being.

We first observe that by proposition 1.1.8, for any fixed $r_1\in (0,1)$ there is a constant $C_{r_1}$ such that

1.26
\begin{equation} \label{eq:varphixlocalupperbd} \varphi (x)\leq \nu '(\varphi ,0)\log |x|^2+C_{r_1} \end{equation}

when $x\in B_ n(0,r_1)\setminus \{ 0\} $. In particular, $\nu (\varphi ,0)\geq \nu '(\varphi ,0)$.

In order to argue the reverse inequality, we may assume that $\nu (\varphi ,0)>0$.

Next observe that by (1.24), for each small enough $\epsilon >0$, we can find $r_0\in (0,1)$ and $C>0$ so that for all $x\in B_ n(0,r_0)\setminus \{ 0\} $, we have

\[ \varphi (x)\leq (\nu (\varphi ,0)-\epsilon )\log |x|^2+C. \]

It follows that $\nu '(\varphi ,0)\geq \nu (\varphi ,0)-\epsilon $. Letting $\epsilon \to 0+$, we conclude that $\nu '(\varphi ,0)\geq \nu (\varphi ,0)$, which combined with the previous inequality gives (1.25). $\square$
