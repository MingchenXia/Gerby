Proposition 3.1.31

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 3.1.31

Proposition 3.1.31. Let $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$. Then the following are equivalent:

  1. $\varphi \in \mathcal{E}(X,\theta ;\phi )$;

  2. $P_{\theta }[\varphi ]=\phi $.

Proof. 2 $\implies $ 1. Assume 2. By proposition 3.1.6,

\[ \int _ X\theta _\varphi ^ n=\int _ X\theta _{P_\theta [\varphi ]}^ n=\int _ X\theta _\phi ^ n. \]

Moreover, since the mass is positive, theorem 3.1.9 implies that

\[ \varphi \preceq P_\theta [\varphi ]=\phi . \]

Thus $\varphi \in \mathcal E(X,\theta ;\phi )$.

1 $\implies $ 2. Assume 1. Then $\varphi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$. Note that $\phi $ is a candidate of $P_{\theta }[\varphi ]$ as in (3.12). So $P_{\theta }[\varphi ]\geq \phi $. The reverse inequality is trivial. $\square$
