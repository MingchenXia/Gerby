Relative energy and Perron segments · Tag 011162B56B40

Relative variational characterization

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Theorem 17.4.4

Theorem 17.4.4. Let $\nu $ be a non-pluripolar measure on $X$, and suppose that $\mathcal D_\nu =\mathcal E^1(X,\theta ,\phi )\cap L^1(\nu )$ is nonempty. Then $\phi \in L^1(\nu )$, and for the functional

\[ F_\nu (v)=E(v)-\int _ X(v-\phi )\, \mathrm d\nu , \qquad v\in \mathcal D_\nu , \]

a potential $u\in \mathcal D_\nu $ maximizes $F_\nu $ if and only if $\mu _ u=\nu $. In particular, $F_\nu $ has no maximizer unless $\nu (X)=m$.

Proof. Let $v\in \mathcal D_\nu $. Then $v\leq \phi +C$ for a constant $C$, and $\phi \leq 0$. Thus the negative part of $\phi $ is at most that of $v$ plus $C$, and the positive part of $\phi $ vanishes; hence $\phi \in L^1(\nu )$. If $\mu _ u=\nu $, the tangent bound of lemma 17.3.9 gives

\[ E(v)-E(u)\leq \int _ X(v-u)\, \mathrm d\nu , \]

that is, $F_\nu (v)\leq F_\nu (u)$.

Conversely, let $u$ be a maximizer and $f\in C(X,\mathbb R)$. By proposition 17.4.3, the potentials $u_ t=P_\theta (u+tf)$ have bounded difference from $u$ and finite energy, so they belong to $\mathcal D_\nu $; moreover $u_0=u$ and $u_ t\leq u+tf$ everywhere, hence $\nu $-almost everywhere. Therefore

\[ E(u_ t)-E(u)-t\int _ Xf\, \mathrm d\nu \leq F_\nu (u_ t)-F_\nu (u)\leq 0. \]

The left side vanishes at $t=0$ and is differentiable there by proposition 17.4.3; since it attains its maximum at $t=0$, its derivative vanishes there. Thus $\int _ Xf\, \mu _ u=\int _ Xf\, \mathrm d\nu $ for every $f\in C(X,\mathbb R)$, and lemma 17.4.2(1) gives $\mu _ u=\nu $. Since $\mu _ u(X)=m$, a maximizer can exist only if $\nu (X)=m$. $\square$
