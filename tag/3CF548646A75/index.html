Chapter 5 · Plurisubharmonic functions and positive currents · Tag 3CF548646A75

Analytic minorant product ideals

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Lemma 1.17.11

Lemma 1.17.11. Let $u,v$ be quasi-plurisubharmonic on $X$, and let $s$ have analytic singularities on $X$. Suppose that $u+v\leq s+O(1)$, and let $U_ m,V_ m$ be quasi-plurisubharmonic functions of analytic types $(1/m,\mathcal I(mu))$ and $(1/m,\mathcal I(mv))$. Then there are numbers $\delta _ m\downarrow 0$ such that

\[ U_ m+V_ m\leq (1-\delta _ m)s+O(1) \quad \hbox{for all sufficiently large integers }m. \]

The bounded error may depend on $m$.

Proof. If $s$ is bounded, upper bounds for $U_ m$ and $V_ m$ give the assertion with $\delta _ m=1/m$ for $m>1$. Otherwise principalize the ideal of $s$ by a log resolution $\pi :\widetilde X\to X$ with smooth $\widetilde X$. Let $E_ i$ be the components of the resulting divisor, with positive coefficients $c_ i$ in $s\circ \pi $; in local coordinates $z_ i$ defining these components, the singular part of $s\circ \pi $ is $\sum _ i c_ i\log |z_ i|^2$. Set $A_ i=1+\operatorname {ord}_{E_ i}(\operatorname {Jac}\pi )$. The hypothesis and lemma 1.17.8 give

\[ \nu (U_ m\circ \pi ,E_ i)+\nu (V_ m\circ \pi ,E_ i) \geq \nu (u\circ \pi ,E_ i)+\nu (v\circ \pi ,E_ i)-\frac{2A_ i}{m} \geq c_ i-\frac{2A_ i}{m}. \]

Choose a positive integer $N\geq \max _ i2A_ i/c_ i$ and put $\delta _ m=N/m$, which is less than one for large $m$. Then the right-hand side above is at least $(1-\delta _ m)c_ i$. After a smooth local correction, divisorial removal from the positive Hessian of $(U_ m+V_ m)\circ \pi $ shows that

\[ (U_ m+V_ m)\circ \pi -(1-\delta _ m)\sum _ i c_ i\log |z_ i|^2 \]

has a quasi-plurisubharmonic representative, which is locally bounded above. A finite cover of $\widetilde X$ by charts and descent along the proper map $\pi $, as in the proof of lemma 1.17.10, therefore give $U_ m+V_ m\leq (1-\delta _ m)s+O(1)$. The bounded constant may depend on $m$. $\square$
