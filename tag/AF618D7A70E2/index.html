Lelong numbers and multiplier ideals · Tag AF618D7A70E2

Divisorial lelong ideal recovery

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Corollary 12.6.8

Corollary 12.6.8. Let $\varphi $ be a weight on $X$, let $E$ be a prime divisor over $X$, and let $m>0$ be a real number. Then

12.28
\begin{equation} \label{eq:divisorial-multiplier-order-bounds} m\nu (\varphi ,E)-A_ X(E) <\operatorname {ord}_ E\mathcal I(m\varphi ) \leq m\nu (\varphi ,E). \end{equation}

Here, for a smooth model $\pi \colon Y\to X$ on which $E$ lies, $\operatorname {ord}_ E\mathcal I(m\varphi )$ is the order along $E$ of the pulled-back ideal $\mathcal I(m\varphi )\mathcal O_ Y$, defined as follows. Near a point $p\in E_{\mathrm{reg}}$, let $g_1,\ldots ,g_ r$ generate $\mathcal I(m\varphi )$ near $\pi (p)$. The minimum of the orders of the functions $g_ i\circ \pi $ along $E$ at $p$ does not depend on the choice of generators, since the members of a second system of generators are combinations of the $g_ i$ with holomorphic coefficients near $\pi (p)$, and conversely. This minimum is locally constant along $E_{\mathrm{reg}}$, because the orders of the $g_ i\circ \pi $ are, by lemma 12.6.1(2); hence it is constant on the connected manifold $E_{\mathrm{reg}}$, and it is $\operatorname {ord}_ E\mathcal I(m\varphi )$. As for the order of a function in definition 12.6.3, it depends only on the prime divisor over $X$ represented by $E$. In particular,

\[ \nu (\varphi ,E)=\lim _{m\to \infty }\frac1m \operatorname {ord}_ E\mathcal I(m\varphi ). \]

Proof. Let $k=\operatorname {ord}_ E\mathcal I(m\varphi )$, and choose a point $p\in E_{\mathrm{reg}}$. Put $y=\pi (p)$, choose a bounded polydisc $\Omega $ around $y$ in a chart on which $\varphi $ is bounded above, and choose $g_1,\ldots ,g_ r\in \mathcal H_{m\varphi }(\Omega )$ generating $\mathcal I(m\varphi )$ near $y$ (theorem 12.2.7(3)). Near $p$, in coordinates $w=(w_1,w')$ with $E=\{ w_1=0\} $, let $k_ i$ be the order of $g_ i\circ \pi $ along $E$ at $p$, so that $k=\min _ ik_ i$; since $\mathcal I(m\varphi )_ y\neq 0$, this minimum is finite.

For the lower bound, choose $i$ with $k_ i=k$. Since $|g_ i|^2\mathrm e^{-m\varphi }$ is integrable on $\Omega $, proposition 12.6.4, applied to the weight $m\varphi $, shows that $|h|^2\mathrm e^{-m\varphi \circ \pi }$, with $h=(g_ i\circ \pi )J_\pi $, is integrable near $p$. The order of $h$ along $E$ at $p$ is $k+A_ X(E)-1$, because orders add under products. By lemma 12.6.6, applied to the weight $m\varphi \circ \pi $, whose generic Lelong number along $E$ is $m\nu (\varphi ,E)$ by proposition 12.1.4, we obtain $k+A_ X(E)>m\nu (\varphi ,E)$. This is the left inequality.

For the upper bound, lemma 12.2.8 gives $m\varphi \leq \log \sum _ i|g_ i|^2+mC_ m$ on a neighborhood of $y$. Near $p$ we have $g_ i\circ \pi =w_1^{k_ i}u_ i$ with $u_ i$ holomorphic, so $|g_ i\circ \pi |\leq C|w_1|^{k_ i}\leq C|w_1|^ k$ where $|w_1|\leq 1$; it follows that $m\varphi \circ \pi \leq k\log |w_1|^2+C''$ on a neighborhood $N$ of $p$. For $q\in E\cap N$ we have $|w_1|\leq |w-q|$, so $m\varphi \circ \pi (w)\leq k\log |w-q|^2+C''$ near $q$, and proposition 12.1.1, proposition 12.1.4 give $\nu (\varphi \circ \pi ,q)\geq k/m$. Since the generic value $\nu (\varphi \circ \pi ,E)$ is attained at points of $E$ arbitrarily close to $p$ (proposition 12.3.5), we conclude that $m\nu (\varphi ,E)\geq k$. Dividing (12.28) by $m$ and letting $m\to \infty $ gives the limit formula. $\square$
