Envelopes and model potentials · Tag 3499B9FD713C

Positive mass rooftop existence

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Proposition 16.5.1

Proposition 16.5.1. Let $u,v\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ satisfy

\[ \int _ X\langle \theta _ u^ n\rangle +\int _ X\langle \theta _ v^ n\rangle >\int _ X\langle \theta _{\max \{ u,v\} }^ n\rangle . \]

Then $P_\theta (u,v)\ne -\infty $.

Proof. Subtracting the same constant from $u$ and $v$ changes neither the hypothesis nor the conclusion, so we may assume that $u,v\leq 0$. Put $m=\int _ X\langle \theta _{\max \{ u,v\} }^ n\rangle $ and $\phi =P_\theta [\max \{ u,v\} ]$. By monotonicity $\int _ X\langle \theta _ u^ n\rangle \leq m$ and $\int _ X\langle \theta _ v^ n\rangle \leq m$, so the strict inequality forces both masses to be positive. Hence $m>0$, and by theorem 16.4.7, $\phi $ is a model potential of mass $m$. Moreover $u,v\leq \max \{ u,v\} \leq \phi $, because the nonpositive potential $\max \{ u,v\} $ is a competitor for $\phi $.

Since $\mu _ u$ and $\mu _ v$ do not charge pole sets, $\mu _ u(\{ u>\phi -k\} )\to \int _ X\langle \theta _ u^ n\rangle $ and $\mu _ v(\{ v>\phi -k\} )\to \int _ X\langle \theta _ v^ n\rangle $ as $k\to \infty $. Choose $k>0$ so large that

\[ \mu _ u(\{ u>\phi -k\} )+\mu _ v(\{ v>\phi -k\} )>m. \]

For $C>k$ put

\[ u_ C=\max \{ u,\phi -C\} ,\quad v_ C=\max \{ v,\phi -C\} ,\quad w_ C=P_\theta (u_ C,v_ C). \]

Since $\phi -C$ is a common minorant, all three potentials have the singularity type of $\phi $ and mass $m$. As $C\to \infty $, $w_ C$ decreases to $P_\theta (u,v)$ by proposition 16.1.6. Suppose that $P_\theta (u,v)=-\infty $, so that $w_ C$ decreases to $-\infty $ identically. Then $\sup _ Xw_ C\to -\infty $ by corollary 14.2.5, and lemma 16.4.2 gives $w_ C\leq \phi -k$ for all large $C$. On $\{ w_ C=u_ C\} $ we then have $u_ C\leq \phi -k$, and similarly for $v_ C$, so corollary 16.2.6 gives

\[ \begin{split} m=\int _ X\langle \theta _{w_ C}^ n\rangle & \leq \mu _{u_ C}(\{ u_ C\leq \phi -k\} ) +\mu _{v_ C}(\{ v_ C\leq \phi -k\} )\\ & =2m-\mu _ u(\{ u>\phi -k\} )-\mu _ v(\{ v>\phi -k\} )<m. \end{split} \]

For the equality we used that, since $C>k$, $u_ C=u$ on the plurifine open set $\{ u>\phi -k\} $, so $\mu _{u_ C}=\mu _ u$ there, and similarly for $v$. This contradiction shows that $P_\theta (u,v)\ne -\infty $. $\square$
