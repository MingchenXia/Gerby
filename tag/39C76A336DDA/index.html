Griffiths nonpositive exact complement index

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 4.6.53

Corollary 4.6.53. If $F$ admits a smooth Hermitian metric $h$ with

\[ h(R_ h(u,\bar u)v,v)\leq 0 \quad \text{for every }x\in B,\ u\in T_ x^{1,0}B,\ v\in F_ x, \]

then the least smooth convexity index of $U_ F$ is $n+r$.

Proof. Put $G(v)=h(v,v)$. At $x\in B$, take a holomorphic frame that is unitary and Chern-normal at $x$, and write a tangent vector to $F$ at $v\in F_ x$ as $(u,w)$, with $u\in T_ x^{1,0}B$ and $w\in F_ x$. The calculation in the proof of lemma 4.6.41 gives

\[ \mathcal L_ G(u,w)=-h(R_ h(u,\bar u)v,v)+|w|_ h^2\geq 0, \]

with zero mixed block. Thus $G$ is smooth, plurisubharmonic and positive off the zero section, and it satisfies $G(cv)=|c|^2G(v)$. Apply proposition 4.6.52. $\square$
