Cancellation when the smallest eigenvalue is controlled.

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Cancellation when the smallest eigenvalue is controlled.

Fix $0<\delta <1/2$, to be chosen after $D_1,D_2$. First suppose that $\lambda _ n\geq -\delta \lambda $. Put $I=\{ k:f_ k>\delta ^{-1}f_1\} $, so that $1\notin I$. Equation (9.35) and $\phi ''=2(\phi ')^2$ give

\begin{align*} -\sum _{k\notin I}f_ k|a_ k|^2/\lambda ^2 & \geq -\phi ''\sum _{k\notin I}f_ k|V_ k|^2 -2(\psi ')^2\delta ^{-1}f_1K,\\ -2\delta \sum _{k\in I}f_ k|a_ k|^2/\lambda ^2 & \geq -2\delta \phi ”\sum _{k\in I}f_ k|V_ k|^2 -4\delta (\psi ')^2\sum _{k\in I}f_ k|v_ k|^2. \end{align*}

Choose $\delta $ small enough that $4\delta (\psi ')^2\leq \psi ''/2$ throughout $[-M,0]$. By (9.33),

\[ \frac{\mathcal C}{\lambda } \geq \sum _{k\in I}\frac{f_ k-f_1}{\lambda (\lambda -\lambda _ k)} |\nabla _1g_{k\bar1}|^2 \geq (1-2\delta )\sum _{k\in I} \frac{f_ k|\nabla _1g_{k\bar1}|^2}{\lambda ^2}. \]

Here we used $(1-\delta )/(1+\delta )\geq 1-2\delta $.

We need the following comparison of the two derivatives $\nabla _1g_{k\bar1}$ and $a_ k=\nabla _ kg_{1\bar1}$. For every $\varepsilon >0$, provided $\lambda /K$ is sufficiently large depending on the fixed function $\psi $,

9.44
\begin{equation} \label{eq:stw-derivative-cancellation} \sum _{k\in I}f_ k\frac{|\nabla _1g_{k\bar1}|^2-|a_ k|^2}{\lambda ^2} \geq -\frac{E}{12K}-C\mathcal F -\varepsilon C|\psi '|\mathcal F -C_\varepsilon |\psi '|\sum _ k f_ k|v_ k|^2. \end{equation}

To prove this, let $U=\sum _{p>1,q}|v_{pq}|$. Commuting partial derivatives in $g$ gives, for $k>1$,

\[ \nabla _1g_{k\bar1}-a_ k =W_{k\bar1,1}-W_{1\bar1,k}+O(\lambda ),\qquad |W_{k\bar1,1}|+|W_{1\bar1,k}|\leq C(\lambda +U). \]

The second inequality uses the absence of $v_1$ in $Z_{k\bar1}$, so that differentiation in the direction $1$ introduces no pure derivative $v_{11}$. All mixed second derivatives are bounded by $C\lambda $, and every other pure second derivative occurs in $U$.

Use $|a+e|^2-|a|^2\geq -2|a||e|$, and then (9.35). For $k>1$, the pure second derivatives in $V_ k$ all occur in $U$. With $H_ k=\sum _ p|v_{p\bar k}|$, the resulting negative terms, divided by $\lambda ^2$, are bounded by a constant times

\[ K^{-1/2}H_ k+K^{-1/2}U +\frac{U^2}{\lambda \sqrt K}+1 +|\psi '||v_ k|+\frac{|\psi '|}{\lambda }|v_ k|U. \]

Here we used $H_ k\leq C\lambda $ and $\lambda \geq K$ to simplify the cross terms. The weight comparison (9.31), the symmetry of pure derivatives and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality give $\mathcal F U^2\leq C E$. Consequently, after multiplication by $f_ k$ and summation, the first two terms are bounded by any fixed small fraction of $E/K$ plus $C\mathcal F$. The third is at most $CE/(\lambda \sqrt K)$, which is absorbed by another such fraction when $\lambda /K$ is large. For the last two terms we use

\[ |v_ k|\leq \varepsilon +C_\varepsilon |v_ k|^2,\qquad |v_ k|U\leq |v_ k|^2+U^2. \]

The contribution of $U^2$ is at most $C|\psi '|E/\lambda $, which can again be absorbed when $\lambda /K$ is large. Choosing the fractions with sum $1/12$ proves (9.44).

Insert this cancellation and the preceding inequalities into (9.41). The remaining derivative error satisfies, by (9.35),

\[ C\sum _ k f_ k|g_{1\bar1,k}|/\lambda \leq \frac{E}{60K}+C\mathcal F +\varepsilon C|\psi '|\mathcal F +C_\varepsilon |\psi '|\sum _ k f_ k|v_ k|^2, \]

after Young's inequality and an adjustment of constants. The torsion contraction also satisfies

\[ \left|\sum _ k f_ kW_{k\bar k}\right| =\left|\sum _ i t_ iZ_{i\bar i}\right| \leq C\sum _ k f_ k|v_ k| \leq \varepsilon C\mathcal F+C_\varepsilon \sum _ k f_ k|v_ k|^2. \]

The first inequality uses again the missing gradient index in $Z_{1\bar1}$. Since $\psi '<0$ and $v_{k\bar k}=\lambda _ k-\chi _{k\bar k}-W_{k\bar k}$, we conclude, after decreasing the remaining coefficient of $E$ if necessary, that

9.45
\begin{equation} \label{eq:stw-first-case-closure} \begin{split} 0\geq {}& f_1\left(\frac{\lambda ^2}{40K} -2(\psi ')^2\delta ^{-1}K\right) +\left(\frac{\psi ''}{2}-C_\varepsilon |\psi '|\right) \sum _ k f_ k|v_ k|^2\\ & -C_0\mathcal F-\varepsilon C_0|\psi '|\mathcal F +|\psi '|\sum _ k f_ k(\chi _{k\bar k}-\lambda _ k). \end{split} \end{equation}

For the first term we used $|v_{1\bar1}|^2\geq \lambda ^2/2-CK$, which follows from $\chi +W=O(\sqrt K)$, and $E\geq f_1|v_{1\bar1}|^2$.

For large $\lambda $, lemma 9.12.2 bounds the last sum below by $\tau \mathcal F/2$. Choose first $\varepsilon $ with $\varepsilon C_0<\tau /4$. Next choose $D_2$ so that $\psi ''/2>C_\varepsilon |\psi '|$, and then choose $D_1$ so that $|\psi '|\tau /4>C_0$ on $[-M,0]$. Finally choose $\delta $ as above. All these choices depend only on the fixed data. Inequality (9.45) then forces $\lambda ^2\leq 80\, \delta ^{-1}\sup _{[-M,0]}|\psi '|^2K^2$, which proves the desired bound in the first case.
