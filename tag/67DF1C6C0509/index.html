Chapter 10 · Hermitian geometry, torsion, and Gauduchon duality · Tag 67DF1C6C0509

Completion flat invariant mass

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 3.4.4

Corollary 3.4.4. For a Hermitian symplectic form $\omega $ on $X$, the following conditions are equivalent:

  1. $F(\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi )$ is independent of the smooth real function $\varphi $ with $\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\varphi >0$;

  2. $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(\omega ^2)=0$;

  3. $\int _ X(\omega +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u)^4$ is independent of the bounded $\omega $-plurisubharmonic function $u$.

The common mass in the third condition is $24V(\omega )$. Every local minimum of $F$ in an Aeppli class has these properties.

Proof. By (3.14), the energy is constant along smooth positive potential variations if and only if their volume is. Since $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\omega =0$, lemma 2.1.2, theorem 2.2.2 show that this smooth mass invariance is equivalent to $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}(\omega ^2)=0$ and to mass invariance for bounded potentials. The zero potential gives the common mass. The last assertion follows from theorem 3.4.3. $\square$
