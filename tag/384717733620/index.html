Normalized global psh lp bound

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Corollary 14.2.2

Corollary 14.2.2. For every $1\leq p<\infty $ there is a constant $M_ p$, depending only on $X$, $\theta $, $\omega $ and $p$, such that

\[ \| u\| _{L^ p(X,\omega ^ n)}\leq M_ p \quad \text{for all }u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )\text{ with }\sup _ Xu=0. \]

Proof. (If $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ is empty, there is nothing to prove.) Choose finitely many coordinate polydiscs $D_ k$ such that the polydiscs $\frac14D_ k$ with the same centers and a quarter of the radii cover $X$, and smooth functions $h_ k$ near $\overline{D_ k}$ with $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}h_ k=\theta $. For $u$ as in the statement, $v_ k=u+h_ k-\sup _{D_ k}h_ k$ is a nonpositive plurisubharmonic function on $D_ k$. By proposition 14.2.1 and the boundedness of the $h_ k$ and of the coordinate volume densities, the norms $\| v_ k\| _{L^1(D_ k)}$ are bounded independently of $u$. After rescaling $D_ k$ to the unit polydisc, (8.6) bounds $\| v_ k\| _{L^ p(\frac14D_ k)}$ independently of $u$. Since $u=v_ k-h_ k+\sup _{D_ k}h_ k$ on $D_ k$, the bounded functions $h_ k$ and the bounded volume densities give the assertion. $\square$
