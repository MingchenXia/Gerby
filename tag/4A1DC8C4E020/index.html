Envelopes and model potentials · Tag 4A1DC8C4E020

Positive mass model characterization

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Theorem 16.4.7

Theorem 16.4.7. Let $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ with $\int _ X\langle \theta _ u^ n\rangle >0$. Then

\[ P_\theta [u]=\max \{ v\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta ):v\leq 0, \ u\preceq v,\ \int _ X\langle \theta _ v^ n\rangle =\int _ X\langle \theta _ u^ n\rangle \} . \]

In particular $P_\theta [P_\theta [u]]=P_\theta [u]$, so $P_\theta [u]$ is a model potential of mass $\int _ X\langle \theta _ u^ n\rangle $.

Proof. Write $w=P_\theta [u]$, and let $v$ belong to the displayed family. By proposition 16.3.2, $w\leq P_\theta [v]$, and the potentials $w$, $u$, $v$ and $P_\theta [v]$ all have the same positive mass. Moreover $v\leq P_\theta [v]$, because $v\leq 0$ is a competitor for $P_\theta [v]$. Hence $w$ and $v$ both belong to $\mathcal E(X,\theta ,P_\theta [v])$, a class whose reference potential has positive mass. By proposition 16.3.3, $\mu _ w$ is carried by $\{ w=0\} $, and since $v\leq 0$, it gives no mass to $\{ w<v\} $. The domination principle, theorem 16.4.6, yields $w\geq v$. Finally, $w$ itself belongs to the family: it is nonpositive, it satisfies $u-\sup _ Xu\leq w$, and it has the same mass as $u$ by proposition 16.3.2. This proves the formula.

The potential $P_\theta [w]$ is nonpositive, satisfies $u\preceq w\preceq P_\theta [w]$, and has the same mass as $u$ by proposition 16.3.2. Hence it belongs to the family, and the formula gives $P_\theta [w]\leq w$. Conversely, $w\leq 0$ is a competitor for $P_\theta [w]$, so $P_\theta [w]=w$. Since the mass of $w$ is positive, $w$ is a model potential. $\square$
