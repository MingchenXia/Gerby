Non-pluripolar Monge–Ampère measures

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · 6 Non-pluripolar Monge–Ampère measures

Throughout this chapter, $\omega $ is a Hermitian form on $X$ and $\mathrm{d}V$ is a smooth positive probability volume form used for $L^1$ norms and integrals. As in section 1.7, we write $\theta _ v=\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v$, and $\mathrm{MA}_\theta (v)=(\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}v)^ n$ when $v$ is bounded; convergence in capacity refers to the capacity $\operatorname {Cap}_\omega $ of section 1.8.

For an unbounded $\theta $-plurisubharmonic function $u$, the Bedford–Taylor products of proposition 1.7.1 are not defined. On the set $\{ u>-t\} $, however, $u$ coincides with its bounded truncation $\max (u,-t)$, and since bounded products are plurifinely local, the restricted measures are compatible as $t$ increases. Their limit is a measure on the complement of the pole set, the non-pluripolar Monge–Ampère measure of $u$. For a general smooth background $\theta $, the truncation need not be $\theta $-plurisubharmonic, so positivity of the restricted products requires a local argument. The total mass of the limit may also be infinite. For a Hermitian background, finiteness of this mass for every potential is equivalent to a uniform bound on the Monge–Ampère masses of bounded potentials, the bounded mass property. When the property fails, we construct a potential of infinite mass whose multiplier ideals are all trivial; its complex Hessian even has integrable coefficients, although its determinant is not integrable.
