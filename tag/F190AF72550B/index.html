Proposition 10.4.10

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 10.4.10

Proposition 10.4.10. Let $T\in \mathcal{Z}_+(X,\alpha )$ be a Kähler current. Then

10.87
\begin{equation} \label{eq:volOkocur} \operatorname{vol}\Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(T)=\frac{1}{n!}\operatorname{vol}T. \end{equation}

Proof. Take a quasi-equisingular approximation $(T_ j)_ j$ of $T$ in $\mathcal{Z}_+(X,\alpha )$. Note that $\Delta _{Y_{\bullet }}(T_ j)$ is decreasing in $j$, as follows from 10.4.6. Our assertion follows from (10.86) and theorem 6.2.30. $\square$
