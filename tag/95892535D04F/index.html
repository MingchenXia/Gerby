Line bundle poincare lelong

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Proposition 14.5.4

Proposition 14.5.4. For a nonzero holomorphic section $s$ of $L$ and a smooth metric $h_0$,

14.22
\begin{equation} \label{eq:line-bundle-poincare-lelong} c_1(L,h_0)+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\log |s|_{h_0}^2=[Z(s)], \end{equation}

where $[Z(s)]$ is the current of integration along the zero divisor of $s$, counted with multiplicities. In particular $[Z(s)]$ represents $c_1(L)$.

Proof. In a local frame write $s=fe$. Since $\log |s|_{h_0}^2=\log |f|^2+\log |e|_{h_0}^2$, the left side equals $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\log |f|^2$, which is the current of integration along the zero divisor of $f$ by theorem 7.4.5. These local divisors agree on overlaps, because the transition functions have no zeros. $\square$
