Test curve

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Definition 9.1.1

Definition 9.1.1. A test curve $\Gamma $ in $\mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ consists of a real number $\Gamma _{\max }$ together with a map $(-\infty ,\Gamma _{\max })\rightarrow \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ denoted by $\tau \mapsto \Gamma _{\tau }$ satisfying the following conditions:

  1. The map $\tau \mapsto \Gamma _{\tau }$ is concave and decreasing;

  2. $P_{\theta }[\Gamma _{\tau }]=\Gamma _{\tau }$;

  3. the potential

    9.1
    \begin{equation} \label{eq:Gammaminf} \Gamma _{-\infty }\coloneqq \operatorname *{sup}_{\tau <\Gamma _{\max }}{}\! {}^{*}\Gamma _{\tau } \end{equation}

    satisfies

    \[ \int _ X \left(\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}\Gamma _{-\infty }\right)^ n>0. \]

Let $\phi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ be a model potential. The set of test curves $\Gamma $ with $\Gamma _{-\infty }=\phi $ is denoted by $\mathrm{TC}(X,\theta ;\phi )$.

The union of all $\mathrm{TC}(X,\theta ;\phi )$'s for various model potentials $\phi \in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )_{>0}$ is denoted by $\mathrm{TC}(X,\theta )_{>0}$.
