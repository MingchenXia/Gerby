Divisorial currents on compact modifications

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · 23 Divisorial currents on compact modifications

Let $X$ be a compact complex manifold and $Y\subset X$ a connected compact complex submanifold of dimension $r\geq 1$. Fix a smooth real $(1,1)$-form $\theta $ and a Hermitian form $\omega $ on $X$, and let $u\in \mathrm{PSH}(X,\theta )$ have generic Lelong number $\nu (u,Y)=\inf _{y\in Y}\nu (u,y)=0$.

The divisorial trace data of $u$ along $Y$ (chapter 19) assign a number to every prime divisor on every smooth compact modification of $Y$. A single model sees only part of these data, since blowing up can reveal vanishing that had higher codimension on $Y$. Summing the coefficients visible on one model gives a closed positive current, whose mass is controlled by a Gauduchon degree. These currents agree under pushforward, while their pullbacks acquire an effective exceptional correction. The trace envelope, which imposes the trace inequalities on all models at once, pulls back exactly. This discrepancy explains why the ordinary singularity type of the divisorial part on one model need not realize the full trace data.

The divisorial currents are closed even when the background is not. In a closed restricted background, subtracting them gives residual Bott–Chern classes that are compatible between models. To identify the exceptional correction for an infinite divisorial sum, we need pullback to be continuous on closed positive currents; this continuity comes from choosing, in each Bott–Chern class, the representative of constant Gauduchon trace. Residual classes with a nonzero prescribed exterior derivative belong to the affine spaces of chapter 24.

Let $u_ m$ and $\epsilon _ m$ be the analytic approximants and curvature errors of corollary 1.15.2: $u_ m$ has analytic singularities of type $(1/m,\mathcal I(mu))$, and $\theta +\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ m\geq -\epsilon _ m\omega $ with $\epsilon _ m\to 0$. For a smooth compact modification $\pi :Z\to Y$, put

\[ \theta _ Z=\pi ^*(\theta |_ Y),\qquad \omega _ Z^0=\pi ^*(\omega |_ Y),\qquad b_ m(E)=\operatorname {ord}_ E\bigl (\mathcal I(mu)\mathcal O_ Y\bigr ) \quad (m\in \mathbb Z_{\geq 1}). \]

Here $E$ is a prime divisor on $Z$, $[E]$ denotes its integration current, and $b_ m(E)$ is the vanishing order along $E$ of the image ideal $\mathfrak a_ m(u;Y)=\mathcal I(mu)\mathcal O_ Y$ pulled back to $Z$. By theorem 19.1.1, the trace numbers satisfy

\[ \frac{b_ m(E)}m\longrightarrow \tau _{Y,u}(E),\qquad \tau _{Y,u}(E)=\sup _{m\geq 1}\frac{b_ m(E)}m. \]

By corollary 1.22.18, the restrictions $u_ m|_ Y$ are not identically $-\infty $, and the curvature bound pulls back to $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}((u_ m|_ Y)\circ \pi )\geq -\theta _ Z-\epsilon _ m\omega _ Z^0$.
