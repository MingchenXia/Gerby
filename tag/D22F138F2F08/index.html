Chapter 16 · Sections, graded series, and Okounkov bodies · Tag D22F138F2F08

Surface curvature rank cohomology

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · Corollary 21.6.6

Corollary 21.6.6. If $\dim X=2$, then $h^ q(X,L^ p\otimes E)=O(p^ r)$ for every fixed holomorphic vector bundle $E$ and $0\leq q\leq 2$.

Proof. The endpoints have already been bounded. Since $\chi =h^0-h^1+h^2$, the middle dimension is at most $h^0+h^2+|\chi |=O(p^ r)$ by the Euler polynomial. $\square$
