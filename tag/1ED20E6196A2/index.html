Almost semigroups

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · 10.1 Almost semigroups

We give a brief presentation of the theory of almost semigroups. The arguments are provided in chapter C.

Fix an integer $n\geq 0$. Fix a closed convex cone $C\subseteq \mathbb {R}^{n}\times \mathbb {R}_{\geq 0}$ such that $C\cap \{ x_{n+1}=0\} =\{ 0\} $. Here $x_{n+1}$ is the last coordinate of $\mathbb {R}^{n+1}$.

Write $\hat{\mathcal{S}}(C)$ for the set of subsets of $C\cap \mathbb {Z}^{n+1}$ and $\mathcal{S}(C)$ for the set of subsemigroups $S\subseteq C\cap \mathbb {Z}^{n+1}$. For $S\in \hat{\mathcal{S}}(C)$ and each $k\in \mathbb {N}$ such that $S_ k\neq \varnothing $, we write

\[ S_ k\coloneqq \left\{ x\in \mathbb {Z}^ n: (x,k)\in S \right\} . \]

Note that $S_ k$ is a finite set by our assumption on $C$.

We introduce a pseudometric on $\hat{\mathcal{S}}(C)$ as follows:

10.1
\begin{equation} \label{eq:dsgdef} d_{\mathrm{sg}}(S,S')\coloneqq \varlimsup _{k\to \infty } k^{-n} \left(|S_ k|+|S'_ k|-2|(S\cap S')_ k|\right). \end{equation}

Here $|\bullet |$ denotes the cardinality of a finite set. The quantity $d_{\mathrm{sg}}$ defined above is a pseudometric on $\hat{\mathcal{S}}(C)$. Given $S,S'\in \hat{\mathcal{S}}(C)$, we say $S$ is equivalent to $S'$ and write $S\sim S'$ if $d_{\mathrm{sg}}(S,S')=0$. This is an equivalence relation.

The volume of $S\in \mathcal{S}(C)$ is defined as

\[ \operatorname{vol}S\coloneqq \lim _{k\to \infty } (ka)^{-n}|S_{ka}|=\varlimsup _{k\to \infty } k^{-n}|S_ k|, \]

where $a$ is a sufficiently divisible positive integer. The existence of the limit and its independence from $a$ both follow from theorem C.2.3.

We define $\overline{\mathcal{S}}(C)$ as the closure of $\mathcal{S}(C)$ in $\hat{\mathcal{S}}(C)$ with respect to the topology defined by the pseudometric $d_{\mathrm{sg}}$. The function $\operatorname{vol}\colon \mathcal{S}(C)\rightarrow \mathbb {R}$ admits a unique $1$-Lipschitz extension to

10.2
\begin{equation} \label{eq:volex} \operatorname{vol}\colon \overline{\mathcal{S}}(C)\rightarrow \mathbb {R}. \end{equation}

Given $S\in \hat{\mathcal{S}}(C)$, we will write $C(S)\subseteq C$ for the closed convex cone generated by $S\cup \{ 0\} $. Moreover, for each $k\in \mathbb {Z}_{>0}$ such that $S_ k\neq \varnothing $, we define

\[ \Delta _ k(S)\coloneqq \mathrm{Conv}\left\{ k^{-1}x\in \mathbb {R}^ n:x\in S_ k \right\} \subseteq \mathbb {R}^ n. \]
Here $\mathrm{Conv}$ denotes the convex hull.

Definition 10.1.1. Let $\mathcal{S}'(C)$ be the subset of $\mathcal{S}(C)$ consisting of semigroups $S$ such that $S$ generates $\mathbb {Z}^{n+1}$ (as an Abelian group).

Note that for any $S\in \mathcal{S}'(C)$, the cone $C(S)$ has full dimension (i.e., the topological interior is non-empty). Given a full-dimensional subcone $C'\subseteq C$, we have $C'\cap \mathbb {Z}^{n+1}\in \mathcal{S}'(C)$. We recall the following definition from [KK12].

Definition 10.1.2. Given $S\in \mathcal{S}'(C)$, its Okounkov body is defined by

\[ \Delta (S)\coloneqq \left\{ x\in \mathbb {R}^ n:(x,1)\in C(S) \right\} . \]

Theorem 10.1.3. For each $S\in \mathcal{S}'(C)$, we have

10.3
\begin{equation} \label{eq:volWvolDelta} \operatorname{vol}S=\lim _{k\to \infty } k^{-n}|S_ k|=\operatorname{vol}\Delta (S)>0. \end{equation}

Moreover, as $k\to \infty $,

10.4
\begin{equation} \label{eq:HausconvDeltaGLS} \Delta _{k}(S)\xrightarrow {d_{\mathrm{Haus}}}\Delta (S). \end{equation}

Corollary 10.1.4. Let $S,S'\in \mathcal{S}'(C)$. Assume that $\operatorname{vol}(S\cap S')>0$. Then we have

\[ d_{\mathrm{sg}}(S,S')=\operatorname{vol}(S)+\operatorname{vol}(S')-2\operatorname{vol}(S\cap S'). \]

Definition 10.1.5. We define $\overline{\mathcal{S}'(C)}_{>0}$ as the set of elements in the closure of $\mathcal{S}'(C)$ in $\hat{\mathcal{S}}(C)$ with positive volume. An element in $\overline{\mathcal{S}'(C)}_{>0}$ is called an almost semigroup in $C$.

Recall that the volume here is defined in (10.2).

Theorem 10.1.6. The Okounkov body map $\Delta \colon \mathcal{S}'(C) \rightarrow \mathcal{K}_ n$ as defined in definition 10.1.2 admits a unique continuous extension

10.5
\begin{equation} \label{eq:Deltagensg} \Delta \colon \overline{\mathcal{S}'(C)}_{>0}\rightarrow \mathcal{K}_ n. \end{equation}

Moreover, for any $S\in \overline{\mathcal{S}'(C)}_{>0}$, we have

10.6
\begin{equation} \label{eq:volWfinal} \operatorname{vol}S=\operatorname{vol}\Delta (S). \end{equation}

Corollary 10.1.7. Suppose that $S,S'\in \overline{\mathcal{S}'(C)}_{>0}$ with $S\subseteq S'$. Then

10.7
\begin{equation} \label{eq:Deltacontain} \Delta (S)\subseteq \Delta (S'). \end{equation}
