Vanishing lower mass

From Singularities in Hermitian pluripotential theory · 26.18 Vanishing lower mass

By (26.89), the lower mass $v_-(h)$ vanishes as soon as there are Gauduchon metrics $\eta $ whose degrees $\int _ Xh\wedge \eta ^{n-1}$ stay bounded while their volumes $\int _ X\eta ^ n$ tend to infinity. We construct such metrics on a compact threefold. Writing $\Gamma =\eta ^2$, the volume of $\eta $ is a determinant integral of the coefficient matrix of the positive $\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}$-closed form $\Gamma $. The construction first concentrates the trace of a normalized form $\Gamma $ in one direction near a hypersurface, and then adds an average of compact rational curves that fills the two complementary directions. The degree stays bounded, while the determinant, and hence the volume of the positive root, becomes large. The smooth equation then converts these metrics into potentials whose masses tend to zero.

Theorem 26.18.1. There is a connected compact complex threefold $X$ such that, for every Hermitian form $h$ on $X$, there are smooth real functions $u_ j$ satisfying

\[ h+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ j>0,\qquad \sup _ Xu_ j=0,\qquad \int _ X(h+\mathrm{dd}^{\mathrm{c}}u_ j)^3\longrightarrow 0. \]

In particular, $v_-(h)=0$ for every Hermitian form $h$ on this manifold.
