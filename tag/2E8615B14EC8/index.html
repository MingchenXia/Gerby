Proposition 11.2.7

From Singularities in global pluripotential theory · Proposition 11.2.7

Proposition 11.2.7. Let $\mathbb {D}_1,\ldots ,\mathbb {D}_ n$ be nef b-divisors over $X$. Then

\[ \left(\mathbb {D}_1,\ldots ,\mathbb {D}_ n \right)\geq \prod _{i=1}^ n \left( \operatorname{vol}\mathbb {D}_ i\right)^{1/n}. \]

Proof. We may assume that $\operatorname{vol}\mathbb {D}_ i>0$ for each $i=1,\ldots ,n$ since there is nothing to prove otherwise. In this case, our assertion follows from proposition 7.3.8. $\square$
