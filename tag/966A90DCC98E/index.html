Lelong numbers and multiplier ideals · Tag 966A90DCC98E

General necessary valuative bound

From Foundations of pluripotential theory · Proposition 12.6.7

Proposition 12.6.7. Let $\varphi $ be a weight on $X$ and $f\in \mathcal I(\varphi )_ x$. Then there are $\varepsilon >0$ and a neighborhood $V$ of $x$ on which $f$ is defined such that

12.27
\begin{equation} \label{eq:general-necessary-valuative-bound} \operatorname {ord}_ E f+A_ X(E)>(1+\varepsilon )\nu (\varphi ,E) \end{equation}

for every prime divisor $E$ over an open subset of $V$; in particular, this holds for every prime divisor over an open neighborhood of $x$ contained in $V$ whose center contains $x$.

Proof. By corollary 12.5.4 there are $\varepsilon >0$ and a neighborhood $V$ of $x$ on which $f$ is defined such that $|f|^2\mathrm e^{-(1+\varepsilon )\varphi }$ is integrable on $V$. Let $E$ be a prime divisor over an open set $U\subset V$, represented on a smooth model $\pi \colon Y\to U$; we may assume that $\operatorname {ord}_ Ef<\infty $. Since $f\in \mathcal I((1+\varepsilon )\varphi )_ y$ for every $y\in U$, proposition 12.6.4, applied to the weight $(1+\varepsilon )\varphi $ on $U$, shows that $|h|^2\mathrm e^{-(1+\varepsilon )\varphi \circ \pi }$, with $h=(f\circ \pi )J_\pi $, is integrable near every point of $Y$. Let $p\in E_{\mathrm{reg}}$. Since orders add under products, the order of $h$ along $E$ at $p$ is $\operatorname {ord}_ Ef+\operatorname {ord}_ EJ_\pi =\operatorname {ord}_ Ef+A_ X(E)-1$. By lemma 12.6.6, applied to the weight $(1+\varepsilon )\varphi \circ \pi $, whose generic Lelong number along $E$ is $(1+\varepsilon )\nu (\varphi ,E)$ by proposition 12.1.4, we obtain (12.27). $\square$
